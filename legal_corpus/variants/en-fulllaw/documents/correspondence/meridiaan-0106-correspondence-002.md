**Re: Matter 2025.0106 – Claim of infringement regarding the “Aether” interface design**

Dear Mr. Reinders,

We act for Verdana Digital B.V. (“our Client”) in response to your letter of 3 April 2025, in which you allege that our Client’s recent release of the “Aether” e-commerce platform infringes upon certain visual design elements protected under Dutch Copyright Act provisions belonging to Nordlicht Retail B.V.

Our Client categorically denies any such infringement. The allegations are factually incorrect and legally baseless. We set out our position below.

**1. Independent Creation**
The “Aether” interface was developed by Verdana Digital B.V.’s internal design team between January 2024 and March 2025. Our Client has preserved all source code, design sketches, wireframes, and version control logs (Git commits) dating back to the initial concept phase in November 2023. These records conclusively demonstrate that the work product is the result of independent creation. At no point did our Client access, copy, or utilize any proprietary assets, source files, or specific expressive elements belonging to Nordlicht Retail B.V. The coincidence in visual appearance is attributable to convergent evolution within the current UI/UX design trends prevalent in the Dutch retail technology sector, not to misappropriation.

**2. Non-Monopolizability of General Style**
Even if a superficial similarity existed between the two platforms, copyright law does not protect general styles, ideas, or functional layouts. Article 1019h DCCP governs costs but presupposes a valid infringement claim; here, no such claim can stand because the contested elements are not protected by copyright. The layout, color scheme, and navigation structures employed in “Aether” are standard industry practices designed for user efficiency and accessibility. These functional and aesthetic choices are not original enough to warrant exclusive rights under Article 1 of the Dutch Copyright Act. A competitor is free to employ similar visual strategies provided they do not copy specific protected expressions.

**3. No Obligation to Cease or Compensate**
Given that no infringement has occurred, our Client sees no legal basis for any demand for cessation, corrective measures, or financial compensation. We expect Nordlicht Retail B.V. to cease making unsubstantiated claims that disrupt our Client’s commercial operations and market positioning.

We trust this clarifies Verdana Digital B.V.’s position. Should you wish to discuss this further, please contact our office directly. Otherwise, we consider this matter closed.
