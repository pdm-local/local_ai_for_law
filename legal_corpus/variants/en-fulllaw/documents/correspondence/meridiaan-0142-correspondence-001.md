**Subject: Final Order to Restore Compliance with the Zoning Plan – Imposition of Penalty Payment**

Dear Sir/Madam,

On behalf of the Municipality of Eindhoven (hereinafter: "the Municipality"), we address you regarding the ongoing non-compliance with the zoning regulations applicable to your business premises located at De Groforei 48, Eindhoven.

### 1. Background and Facts

Following an inspection conducted on 2 October 2025 by municipal enforcement officers, it was established that Maaslicht Interactive B.V. has commenced industrial-scale server room operations within a building zone designated strictly for commercial office use under the Municipal Zoning Plan 2024 (hereinafter: "the Zoning Plan"). Specifically, the installation of high-density computing racks, associated cooling infrastructure, and the resulting excessive energy consumption violates Article 2.15 of the Zoning Plan, which prohibits industrial activities in this sector.

You were previously notified of these violations via letter dated 15 September 2025 (Reference: 2025.0142/NOTICE). Despite this prior warning and the opportunity to be heard, you failed to cease the unauthorized use or submit a valid application for a zoning plan amendment that would regularize the situation. The municipal board has therefore determined that your actions constitute a persistent breach of public law regulations, causing disturbance to the neighborhood through noise and excessive energy demand.

### 2. Legal Basis

The Municipality is empowered under the General Administrative Law Act (Algemene wet bestuursrecht, "Awb") to take necessary measures to ensure compliance with zoning regulations. 

In determining the appropriate enforcement measure, the Municipality has carefully weighed the interests of the public against your interest in continuing the current business operations. This assessment was conducted in strict accordance with **Article 3:4 Awb**, which mandates that administrative bodies must consider relevant interests and apply the principle of proportionality. The Municipality has concluded that less intrusive measures have proven ineffective due to your continued non-compliance. Consequently, the imposition of an administrative penalty payment is deemed necessary and proportional to compel immediate cessation of the illegal activity.

### 3. Decision: Order to Comply

By virtue of the powers conferred upon us, the Municipality hereby orders you to:

1.  **Immediately cease** all industrial server room operations at De Groforei 48;
2.  **Remove** or repurpose the high-density computing infrastructure such that the premises comply with the commercial office designation under the Zoning Plan;
3.  **Restore** the building to its original state regarding load-bearing capacity and energy usage patterns within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of this letter.

### 4. Imposition of Penalty Payment

Should you fail to comply with the order described in Section 3 within the stipulated compliance period, a penalty payment is imposed as follows:

*   **Amount:** A daily penalty payment of **€ 50.000** (fifty thousand euros) shall be levied for each day that the violation persists beyond 30 November 2025 at 17:00 hours.
*   **Duration:** This penalty will accrue until full compliance is verified by municipal inspectors or a final judicial decision renders this enforcement measure unenforceable.

Please note that the imposition of this penalty does not preclude the Municipality from pursuing further legal remedies, including forced removal of equipment at your expense or criminal prosecution for violation of administrative orders.

### 5. Legal Recourse

In accordance with applicable law, you have the right to file an objection against this decision within six weeks after receipt of this letter. The objection must be submitted in writing to the Municipality of Eindhoven, Attention: Legal Affairs Department, Kennedyplein 200, 5611 DD Eindhoven.

During the objection period, the enforcement order remains immediately executable unless a court explicitly grants a stay of execution. Given the ongoing nature of the violation and its impact on public infrastructure, the Municipality reserves the right to enforce this decision pending the outcome of any administrative proceedings.

We trust you will give this matter the urgent attention it requires.
