**Subject: Formal Notice and Takedown Request for Infringing Content – Urgent Action Required**

Dear Sir or Madam,

We act on behalf of our client, Meridiaan Interactive B.V. (hereinafter "Meridiaan"), a company specialized in software development and digital content creation. We are writing to you as the provider of hosting services for your platform, Lumen Cloud B.V. (hereinafter "Lumen Cloud").

This letter serves as our formal **notice-and-takedown** request regarding specific content hosted on your platform that infringes upon Meridiaan's intellectual property rights.

### 1. Facts and Background

Meridiaan is the exclusive rights holder of a series of proprietary software code libraries and associated user interface designs (collectively, the "Protected Works"). These works are registered under Dutch Copyright Law and international copyright treaties.

On 10 April 2025, Meridiaan identified that unauthorized copies of these Protected Works, including source code repositories and design assets, were being distributed on a public forum hosted by Lumen Cloud at the following URL: `https://forum.lumencloud.net/shared/code-repo-alpha-99`.

The content in question was uploaded by a user with the handle "OpenSourceFan" on 8 April 2025. The content does not constitute fair use, nor has it been licensed to any third party by Meridiaan. The unauthorized distribution constitutes a clear violation of Meridiaan’s exclusive rights under Article 13 of the Dutch Copyright Act.

### 2. Legal Basis

As a provider of information society services, Lumen Cloud benefits from the liability exemptions provided under Article 6:196c of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC) regarding hosting, provided that you act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the illegal content upon obtaining actual knowledge of it.

Furthermore, Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act, DSA) imposes clear obligations on very large online platforms and intermediaries to cooperate in the removal of illegal content. By failing to act promptly upon receipt of this notice, Lumen Cloud risks losing its safe harbor protection and may be held jointly liable for the continued infringement.

### 3. Request for Action

In accordance with the above, we hereby demand that Lumen Cloud B.V.:

1.  **Immediately remove** the infringing content located at `https://forum.lumencloud.net/shared/code-repo-alpha-99`;
2.  **Disable access** to the specific thread and any associated downloads or links related to this infringement;
3.  **Preserve all relevant data**, including user logs, IP addresses, and upload timestamps, in accordance with Article 6:107 of the DCC regarding potential third-party costs and evidence preservation;
4.  **Confirm in writing** that these actions have been taken within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt of this correspondence.

### 4. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this request will leave us no choice but to pursue all available legal remedies against Lumen Cloud B.V., including but not limited to proceedings for injunctive relief and claims for damages under Article 6:162 of the DCC (tort law). We reserve the right to seek full compensation for all costs incurred, including legal fees.

We trust that you will treat this matter with the urgency it requires and act in compliance with your obligations as an intermediary.
