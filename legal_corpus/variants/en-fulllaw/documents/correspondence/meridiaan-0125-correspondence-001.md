**Subject: Additional assessment and penalty for Corporate Income Tax Year 2024**

Dear Managing Director,

Following the examination of your corporate income tax return for the year 2024, submitted on 1 May 2025, we have determined that certain items were not processed in accordance with the requirements of the State Taxes Act (AWR).

During our review, it came to light that Kempen Labs B.V. incorrectly classified a portion of its operational expenses. Specifically, costs amounting to € 127.300 were deducted as eligible business expenses despite being related to private use by shareholders and non-business-related entertainment. This constitutes a new fact within the meaning of **Article 16 AWR (State Taxes Act)**, justifying the issuance of an additional assessment.

Consequently, we have drawn up an additional assessment notice for the corporate income tax year 2024 in the amount of € 127.300. This amount reflects the full correction of the taxable profit resulting from the disallowance of these deductions.

Furthermore, given that the omission was due to gross negligence on the part of the company’s financial administration, we consider it appropriate to impose a penalty. In accordance with **Article 67d AWR**, a penalty of 50% is applied to the additional tax assessment.

The calculation of the penalty is as follows:
50% of € 127.300 = € 63.650.

The total amount payable, comprising the additional tax and the penalty, is therefore € 190.950.

Payment must be made no later than four weeks after the date of this letter, i.e., by 12 April 2025, into account number NL30ABNA0417164000 in the name of Dutch Tax Administration, with reference KAM-2025-8892-XJ.

If you wish to contest this decision, you may file a formal objection within six weeks after the date of this letter. The objection must be submitted in writing to the address mentioned above. Please note that filing an objection does not suspend the obligation to pay, unless we explicitly state otherwise.
