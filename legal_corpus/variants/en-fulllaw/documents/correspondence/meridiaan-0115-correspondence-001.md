**Subject: Avoidance of payment under Article 42 Bankruptcy Act – Matter no. 2025.0115 (Nimbus Interactive B.V.)**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I act as the trustee in bankruptcy for Nimbus Interactive B.V., which was declared bankrupt by the District Court of Eindhoven on 28 February 2025 under case number F.A.R. 24/109. Pursuant to my appointment, I have conducted an investigation into the financial transactions of the bankrupt company prior to its insolvency.

It has come to light that Nimbus Interactive B.V. made a payment of € 320.000 to your company, Polderzicht Media B.V., on 15 January 2025. This payment was made for services rendered under an agreement dated 10 November 2024 (the "Underlying Agreement").

The payment was executed while Nimbus Interactive B.V. was already in a state of actual insolvency, defined as the situation where the debtor has ceased to pay its due and payable debts. Furthermore, the payment was made within the suspect period preceding the bankruptcy declaration. Under Dutch law, such payments may be avoided if they were made with the intent of giving Polderzicht Media B.V. an advantage over other creditors, or if the benefit conferred was not commensurate with the consideration given at that specific moment.

Given the proximity of the payment date to the onset of insolvency and the substantial amount involved, I am of the opinion that this transaction constitutes a fraudulent preference. Consequently, I hereby invoke **Article 42 Bankruptcy Act** to demand the avoidance of this payment.

You are therefore required to repay the full amount of € 320.000 to the bankruptcy estate. The funds must be transferred to the following bank account within fourteen (14) days from the date of this letter:

Bank: Rabobank
Account number: 12.34.56.789
BIC: RABONL2U
IBAN: NL12RABO1234567890
In the name of: *Boedel Nimbus Interactive B.V.*

If you believe that the avoidance is unjustified, please provide a detailed written explanation supported by documentary evidence within the aforementioned fourteen-day period. Should we not receive your response or the requested funds within this timeframe, I will be compelled to initiate legal proceedings against Polderzicht Media B.V. without further notice to recover the amount, including all associated legal costs.

I trust you will handle this matter with the urgency it requires.
