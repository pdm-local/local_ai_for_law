**Re: Maaslicht Cloud B.V. v. Maaslicht Systems B.V.**
**Subject: Intention to file a petition for inquiry proceedings pursuant to Article 2:345 of the Dutch Civil Code**

Dear Members of the Management Board,

We act as legal counsel to **Maaslicht Cloud B.V.** (hereinafter: "our Client"). With regard to the recent corporate governance disputes between our Client and **Maaslicht Systems B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Company"), we hereby inform you that our Client intends to file a petition for inquiry proceedings before the Enterprise Chamber of the Amsterdam Court of Appeal.

Our Client has raised serious concerns regarding the conduct of the Company’s management in the fiscal year 2024, particularly concerning the declaration and distribution of dividends without adequate justification or adherence to statutory solvency requirements. Specifically, our Client objects to the decision to distribute a dividend amounting to **€ 480.000** to the shareholders while the Company’s financial position was precarious, thereby jeopardizing the continuity of the business and the interests of creditors.

Under **Article 2:345 of the Dutch Civil Code** (Burgerlijk Wetboek), any person who has a legitimate interest may request the Enterprise Chamber to conduct an inquiry into the policy and the course of affairs held by the management board of a company, or into the general course of affairs of the company and its affiliated enterprises, if there are grounds to doubt the propriety of that policy or course of affairs.

Our Client considers that the distribution of the aforementioned sum constitutes a clear deviation from proper corporate conduct. The lack of transparent reasoning for this payout, combined with the subsequent liquidity constraints faced by the Company, warrants judicial scrutiny. Our Client seeks a determination that the management’s actions were improper and requests appropriate remedial measures, including the potential annulment of the dividend resolution and the dismissal or suspension of specific board members.

We expect your response regarding any proposed amicable settlement within fourteen (14) days from the date of this letter. Should no satisfactory resolution be reached within this period, we are instructed to proceed with the formal petition without further notice.

This letter is sent in our capacity as legal counsel and does not constitute a waiver of any rights or privileges held by our Client.
