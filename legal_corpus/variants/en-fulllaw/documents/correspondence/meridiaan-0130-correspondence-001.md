We act as legal counsel to Aurora Digital B.V. (hereinafter "our client") regarding the serious workplace accident that occurred at your company’s premises in Veldhoven on October 14, 2025. This letter serves as a formal demand for compensation pursuant to Dutch tort law.

**Factual Background**

On the aforementioned date, our client’s employee, Ms. Elise van den Berg, visited the offices of Veldhoven Software B.V. for a scheduled technical integration meeting. While traversing the main corridor between the server room and the conference area, Ms. van den Berg tripped over an unsecured network cable that had been left across the walkway without any warning signs or protective covering.

As a direct result of this fall, Ms. van den Berg sustained severe injuries to her right ankle and knee, including a complex fracture requiring surgical intervention. She was transported by ambulance to the Maxima Medisch Centrum in Eindhoven, where she underwent surgery on October 15, 2025. Ms. van den Berg has been medically advised to refrain from work for a period of eight weeks and requires ongoing physiotherapy for an indefinite duration.

**Legal Basis**

The circumstances described above constitute a clear breach of the standard of care expected under Dutch law. As the holder and user of the premises where the incident occurred, Veldhoven Software B.V. is responsible for maintaining a safe environment for visitors and contractors. Leaving hazardous cables unsecured in a high-traffic area creates an unreasonable risk of injury.

Under **Article 6:162 DCC**, any person who causes damage to another through a culpable act or omission is liable to compensate that damage. Your company’s failure to ensure the safety of its premises and its neglect to remove obvious hazards directly caused the injuries sustained by Ms. van den Berg. Consequently, Veldhoven Software B.V. is fully liable for all resulting damages, both material and non-material.

**Claimed Damages**

Our client has calculated the total amount of damages based on verified medical reports, wage loss statements, and the severe impact on Ms. van den Berg’s quality of life. We hereby claim the following:

1.  **Medical expenses and rehabilitation costs:** € 9,200
2.  **Loss of income (wage continuation):** € 12,800
3.  **Non-material damages (pain and suffering)**: € 5,500

**Total claimed amount: € 27.500**

These figures are supported by the attached medical dossier, invoices from healthcare providers, and the employer’s payroll administration confirming the wage loss.

**Resolution Proposal**

We trust that Veldhoven Software B.V. recognizes its liability in this matter and will act responsibly to resolve this dispute amicably. We request that you transfer the total sum of € 27.500 to our client’s bank account (IBAN: NL91MERIDIAAN0123456789) within fourteen days of receipt of this letter, under reference "Matter 2025.0130".

If we do not receive the payment or a satisfactory response by **November 16, 2025**, we have been instructed to initiate legal proceedings without further notice. In such an event, our client will also claim all legal costs and statutory interest applicable under Dutch law.

We look forward to your prompt cooperation in settling this matter.
