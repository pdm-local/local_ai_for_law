Slootweg, acting as counsel for **Maaslicht Interactive B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Company"), herewith submit this formal notice of objection against the penalty order issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven on 10 April 2025, reference number 2025-EIN-8892. The Company requests that this penalty order be revoked or substantially reduced.

### 1. Facts of the Case

The Company operates from its registered office and primary business location at **Kennedyplein 200, 5611 ER Eindhoven**. On 15 March 2025, an inspection was conducted by municipal enforcement officers regarding alleged deviations from the applicable zoning plan (Bestemmingsplan) concerning the use of the ground floor premises for server hosting and data processing activities.

The Municipality contends that this activity constitutes "industrial manufacturing" or "heavy logistics," which is not permitted under the current residential-commercial mixed-use designation. Consequently, on 10 April 2025, the College of Mayor and Aldermen imposed a penalty order imposing a fine of **€ 50.000** to compel compliance with the zoning regulations.

The Company acknowledges the presence of server racks within the premises but disputes the classification of these activities as industrial manufacturing under the relevant statutory interpretation applicable at the time of the initial lease agreement and subsequent municipal guidance.

### 2. Grounds for Objection

#### 2.1. Departure from Policy (Article 4:84 GALA)

The imposition of a penalty order constitutes a binding decision (*bindend voorschrift*). According to well-established case law, the administrative body must adhere to its own policy rules unless there are compelling reasons to depart from them.

In this matter, the Municipality has departed from its long-standing enforcement policy without providing adequate motivation for such departure, in violation of **Article 4:84 GALA**. 

For a period of five years (2019–2024), the Municipality of Eindhoven operated under an internal "Digital Innovation Hub" guideline which explicitly permitted low-emission, low-noise data processing activities in mixed-use zones like Kennedyplein, provided that noise levels remained below 45 dB during daytime hours. The Company relied on this public policy stance when renewing its lease in January 2023. 

The sudden imposition of a maximum-level penalty without prior warning or notification of a policy shift violates the principle of legitimate expectations (*vertrouwensbeginsel*). Article 4:84 GALA requires that any departure from published policy must be explicitly motivated by overriding public interest reasons that were not previously known to the party. No such motivation has been provided in the penalty order.

#### 2.2. Disproportionality of the Penalty

The imposed fine of **€ 50.000** is grossly disproportionate to the infringement and the Company’s financial position, rendering the decision unreasonable under **Article 6:7 GALA**.

1.  **Nature of the Infringement:** The alleged violation involves no environmental hazards, no public nuisance, and no structural safety risks. The activities are purely digital and cause negligible externalities compared to traditional industrial uses.
2.  **Proportionality Principle:** Under **Article 6:7 GALA**, the severity of the sanction must stand in reasonable proportion to the gravity of the infringement. A fine of **€ 50.000** is typically reserved for severe, repeated, or willful violations involving public safety or significant environmental damage. Applying such a maximum-level sanction to a technical zoning classification dispute regarding digital infrastructure is excessive.
3.  **Financial Impact:** Maaslicht Interactive B.V. is an SME with an annual turnover of approximately € 1.2 million. A single fine of **€ 50.000** represents over 4% of its annual revenue and threatens its solvency, effectively amounting to a forced closure of the business rather than a corrective measure.

### 3. Request

In light of the above, we request that the Municipality of Eindhoven:

1.  **Revoke** the penalty order No. 2025-EIN-8892 in its entirety; or alternatively
2.  **Reduce** the fine to a nominal amount of **€ 500** pending a formal zoning plan amendment procedure, acknowledging the lack of prior notification regarding policy changes as required by **Article 4:84 GALA**.

We reserve the right to appeal this objection decision to the Administrative Jurisdiction Division of the Council of State (*Afdeling Bestuursrechtspraak van de Raad van State*) should this matter not be resolved amicably.
