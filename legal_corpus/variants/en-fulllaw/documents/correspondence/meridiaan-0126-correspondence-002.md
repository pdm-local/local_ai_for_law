**Subject: Notice of Partial Rent Suspension Due to Unrepaired Defect — Leidenstraat Location**

Dear Mr. van der Meer,

We write regarding the lease agreement for our retail premises located at Leidenstraat 88, Eindhoven (the “Premises”), currently held by Brightlane Retail B.V. as tenant and Helix Networks B.V. as landlord.

It has come to our attention that a significant water leak originating from the plumbing infrastructure above the second-floor storage area has remained unrepaired for several months. Despite our initial notification on 10 November 2024, followed by a formal request for urgent repair on 5 December 2024 and again on 10 January 2025, no effective action has been taken to rectify the defect. The continuous leakage has caused substantial damage to our inventory of electronic goods and has forced us to close section B of the Premises for safety reasons.

Under Dutch law, the landlord is obligated to maintain the leased property in a condition suitable for its intended use. Specifically, **Article 7:206 DCC** stipulates that the tenant may withhold rent or demand a reduction if the landlord fails to perform their obligations regarding repairs and maintenance. Given the severity of the defect and its direct impact on our ability to conduct business normally, we are compelled to exercise our right to suspend a portion of the rental payments until the defect is fully remedied.

We hereby notify you that effective immediately, 14 March 2025, Brightlane Retail B.V. will suspend 40% of the monthly base rent for the Premises. This reduction will remain in effect until such time as the leak is completely repaired and the affected area is restored to a safe and usable condition, confirmed by a joint inspection.

We expect this matter to be resolved with urgency. Please confirm receipt of this letter and provide a concrete timeline for the commencement of repairs within three working days.
