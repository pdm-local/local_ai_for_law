**Re: Matter no. 2025.0129 – Set-off invocation and denial of prejudice regarding payment to Aurora Logistics B.V.**

Dear mr. van Raay,

We refer to your request for information dated 10 November 2025 concerning the financial transactions between Meridiaan Solutions B.V. (“Meridiaan Solutions”) and Aurora Logistics B.V. (“Aurora Logistics”), specifically regarding the payment executed on 28 October 2025.

This letter serves as our formal response to your inquiry. We wish to clarify the legal standing of Meridiaan Solutions in relation to the funds transferred and our position regarding the alleged prejudice.

**1. Invocation of Set-Off under Article 53 Bankruptcy Act**

Meridiaan Solutions hereby formally invokes the right of set-off (set-off in bankruptcy) pursuant to **Article 53 Bankruptcy Act**. It is our firm position that the debt owed by Meridiaan Solutions to Aurora Logistics is extinguished *ipso jure* to the extent of the mutual claims. The conditions for set-off are fully met: the debts are liquid, due, and fungible. Consequently, any claim Aurora Logistics might assert against Meridiaan Solutions in the context of its current insolvency proceedings is effectively neutralized by our counter-claims.

**2. Denial of Knowledge Regarding Prejudice**

Regarding your specific query about whether Meridiaan Solutions was aware of any potential prejudice to the bankruptcy estate concerning the transfer of funds, we categorically deny such knowledge. 

On 28 October 2025, Meridiaan Solutions transferred the sum of **€ 95.000** to Aurora Logistics B.V., account number NL91ABNA0417164302, at ABN AMRO Bank N.V. This payment was made in full satisfaction of our outstanding commercial invoice no. MS-2025-884, which had been due since 1 September 2025. 

At the time of this transaction, Meridiaan Solutions acted in good faith and in the ordinary course of business. We had no knowledge, nor could we have reasonably suspected, that Aurora Logistics was experiencing financial difficulties or that such a payment would constitute a fraudulent preference or otherwise prejudice its creditors. The payment was made for valid consideration provided by Aurora Logistics in the form of completed consulting services rendered in Q3 2025.

**3. Conclusion**

Meridiaan Solutions maintains that the transfer of € 95.000 was a lawful performance of an existing obligation, extinguished later by our invocation of set-off under **Article 53 Bankruptcy Act**. We have no knowledge of any prejudice caused to the estate by this transaction, as the debt was discharged in accordance with standard commercial practices and prior to any official declaration of bankruptcy or awareness of insolvency proceedings.

We trust this clarifies our position. Should you require further documentation regarding the underlying contract and service delivery for invoice MS-2025-884, please let us know immediately.
