**Subject:** Termination of Employment Contract – Mr. J. van den Berg

Dear mr. Havelaar,

We are writing to you regarding the ongoing negotiations concerning the termination of the employment contract between our client, Helix Data B.V., and its former employee, Mr. J. van den Berg.

Our client is prepared to agree to the immediate dissolution of the employment relationship, provided that a clear resolution is reached regarding the restrictive covenant contained in the agreement. As you are aware, Section 4 of the contract includes a non-compete clause pursuant to Article 7:653 DCC, which stipulates a prohibition on engaging in competing activities for a period of **12 months** following the termination date.

Helix Data B.V. recognizes that enforcing such a broad restriction may pose significant practical challenges and could be subject to judicial reduction given the specific nature of Mr. van den Berg’s role and the limited protective interest of the employer at this stage. To facilitate a swift and amicable conclusion to this matter, our client is willing to waive the right to enforce the non-compete clause entirely, effectively rendering it null and void for both parties.

In exchange for this waiver, Helix Data B.V. requires that Mr. van den Berg formally consents to the termination of his contract without further objection or claims for damages related to the cessation of employment. We propose that this mutual agreement be formalized in a settlement deed to be signed by both parties no later than 15 April 2025.

Please confirm whether you find this proposal acceptable so that we may proceed with drafting the necessary documentation. We look forward to your prompt response.
