**Re: Unauthorized engagement of sub-processors under the Data Processing Agreement dated 12 January 2025**

Dear Mr. van den Berg,

We act for **Meridiaan Media B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Controller") in relation to the Data Processing Agreement concluded between Meridiaan Media B.V. and Veldhoven Interactive B.V. (hereinafter: "the Processor") on 12 January 2025.

It has come to our attention that, during the execution of the data processing activities for the joint marketing campaign "SpringForward 2025," your company engaged additional sub-processors without prior specific or general written authorization from Meridiaan Media B.V. Specifically, evidence indicates that personal data was transferred to servers located outside the European Economic Area via an infrastructure provider not listed in Annex II of the Agreement.

As you are aware, **Article 28 GDPR** strictly regulates the engagement of sub-processors. Under Section 4 of Article 28 GDPR, a processor may not engage another processor without prior specific or general written authorization of the controller. Where general written authorization has been given, the processor must inform the controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of other processors, thereby giving the controller the opportunity to object to such changes.

Our client’s approved list of sub-processors explicitly includes only **Hetzner Online GmbH (Duitsland)** for cloud storage and computing resources, as formally notified in writing on 15 January 2025. The engagement of any other entity constitutes a direct violation of Article 28 GDPR and the contractual obligations set forth in Annex II of the Data Processing Agreement.

Consequently, Meridiaan Media B.V. hereby objects to the engagement of these unauthorized sub-processors effective immediately. You are required to:

1.  Cease all data processing activities performed by the unauthorized sub-processor(s) no later than **17 March 2025**;
2.  Ensure the complete deletion or return of all personal data processed by such unauthorized entities;
3.  Provide written confirmation of compliance with these instructions by **19 March 2025**.

Failure to comply will result in further legal action, including a claim for damages under Article 82 GDPR and contractual penalties as stipulated in the Agreement.

We trust you will treat this matter with the urgency it requires to ensure continued compliance with Dutch and EU data protection laws.
