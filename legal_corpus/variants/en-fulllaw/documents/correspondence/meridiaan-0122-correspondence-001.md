**Re: Intended Dissolution of Employment Contract – Ground d (Underperformance)**

This letter serves as formal notification that Tinexus Systems B.V. (“the Employer”) intends to dissolve the employment contract concluded between you and the Employer on 1 September 2023, with effect from 30 April 2025.

As you are aware, your performance in the role of Senior Backend Developer has not met the agreed-upon standards over the past twelve months. Specifically, despite previous verbal warnings issued on 15 January 2024 and 20 June 2024, there was a continued failure to meet key performance indicators regarding code review turnaround times and bug resolution rates within the Alpha Project framework.

In an effort to support your professional development and rectify these deficiencies, the Employer implemented a structured Performance Improvement Plan (“PIP”) starting on 1 October 2024. This plan outlined specific, measurable goals to be achieved by 31 January 2025. The PIP was regularly monitored through bi-weekly check-ins and documented in writing.

Unfortunately, as of the deadline of 31 January 2025, the required improvements were not realized. A final review meeting was held on 5 February 2025, during which it was confirmed that the objectives set forth in the PIP remained unmet. Consequently, the Employer has concluded that there is no reasonable prospect of improvement in the foreseeable future.

Therefore, pursuant to **Article 7:669 DCC**, ground d (underperformance), the Employer hereby declares its intention to dissolve the employment contract. The dissolution will take effect on 30 April 2025, allowing for a statutory notice period of three months as required under Article 7:672 DCC.

During this notice period, you are expected to continue performing your duties diligently and in good faith. We request that you cooperate fully with the handover process regarding your current projects, particularly the migration of the legacy database systems to the new cloud infrastructure.

We wish you success in your future professional endeavors.
