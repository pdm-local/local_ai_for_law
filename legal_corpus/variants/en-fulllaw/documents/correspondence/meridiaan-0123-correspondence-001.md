**Re: Matter 2025.0123 – Dispute regarding spousal maintenance obligation of Zenith Cloud B.V.**

Dear Mr. de Wit,

We act on behalf of our clients, **Zenith Cloud B.V.** and **Meridiaan Technologies B.V.**, with their registered offices at Herikerbergweg 238, 1101 CM Amsterdam and Kennedyplein 200, 5611 ED Eindhoven respectively. We refer to your recent correspondence dated 2 May 2025 concerning the spousal maintenance claim against Zenith Cloud B.V. in the context of the ongoing separation proceedings.

Our clients have carefully reviewed the proposed monthly spousal maintenance amount of € 1.850 and express serious objection to this figure. While our clients acknowledge the legal framework governing spousal support, specifically **Article 1:157 DCC**, it is firmly of the opinion that the requested amount is disproportionately high relative to Zenith Cloud B.V.'s actual financial capacity and current operational liquidity constraints.

The calculation provided in your previous letter appears to rely on historical revenue figures from the fiscal year 2023, which no longer reflect the current economic reality of our clients. Since the onset of the restructuring process initiated in January 2025, Zenith Cloud B.V.'s net disposable income has decreased significantly due to necessary operational adjustments and reduced profit margins. A maintenance claim of € 1.850 would impose an unreasonable burden on the company, potentially jeopardizing its continued viability and ability to meet statutory obligations towards other creditors and employees.

In light of **Article 1:157 DCC**, spousal maintenance must be determined based on what is reasonable and fair given the circumstances of both parties. It is our contention that a monthly amount of € 1.850 exceeds this threshold. Our clients propose a recalculation based on the audited financial statements for the first quarter of 2025, which demonstrate a substantially lower net profit available for distribution or personal appropriation.

We therefore request that you review the financial data attached hereto and propose a revised, more realistic maintenance amount that aligns with Zenith Cloud B.V.'s current financial position. We are willing to engage in good-faith negotiations to reach an equitable solution, provided it reflects the actual economic capacity of the company at this time.

We look forward to your response within fourteen days.
