**Re: Objection to unauthorized sub-processor engagement – Matter 2025.0133**

Dear mr. Okafor,

We act on behalf of our client, Tinexus Networks B.V. ("the Controller"), regarding the Data Processing Agreement dated 15 January 2025 (the "Agreement") concluded with Maaslicht Software B.V. ("the Processor").

It has come to our attention that during the period from 1 March 2025 to 9 April 2025, Maaslicht Software B.V. engaged a third-party entity for the processing of personal data without obtaining prior specific or general written authorization from Tinexus Networks B.V. This action constitutes a direct breach of Article 28 GDPR and Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

Under Article 28 GDPR, a processor may not engage another processor without prior authorisation from the controller. Where the processor has obtained general written authorisation for the engagement of other processors, the processor shall inform the controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of other processors by giving notice of at least thirty days prior to the take-over and shall afford the controller the opportunity to object to such changes. Maaslicht Software B.V. failed to provide this notice and did not offer Tinexus Networks B.V. the opportunity to object within the statutory timeframe.

While it is acknowledged that certain technical operations may utilize infrastructure provided by Microsoft Azure (Nederland), the specific engagement of independent sub-processors for data analysis and storage management outside the approved list is strictly prohibited. The only authorized sub-processors listed in Annex A of the Agreement are those explicitly named therein. Any reliance on standard cloud infrastructure does not absolve the Processor of its obligation to manage and approve distinct legal entities acting as sub-processors in compliance with Article 28 GDPR.

**Demands**

In light of the above, Tinexus Networks B.V. demands that Maaslicht Software B.V.:

1. Immediately cease all processing activities conducted by the unauthorized sub-processor;
2. Provide a detailed audit report of all data processed by this unauthorized entity between 1 March 2025 and the present date;
3. Certify in writing that all personal data held by this unauthorized sub-processor has been securely deleted or returned to Tinexus Networks B.V.;
4. Confirm that no further sub-processors will be engaged without prior specific written consent from Tinexus Networks B.V.

We expect a response confirming compliance with these demands no later than **26 April 2025**. Failure to respond adequately or to rectify the breach may result in further legal action, including claims for damages under Article 82 GDPR and termination of the Agreement pursuant to Section 12 of the Agreement.
