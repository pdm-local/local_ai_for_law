Dear Mr. van der Meer,

On 10 February 2025, inspectors from the Municipality of Eindhoven conducted an unannounced site visit to your premises located at Kennedyplein 200. The inspection revealed that you have executed structural modifications to the building’s facade and internal load-bearing walls without obtaining a required building permit (omgevingsvergunning). Specifically, three large glazed partitions were installed connecting the ground floor server room to the public-facing lobby, and an external ventilation shaft was mounted on the north-facing elevation.

These works contravene Article 2.1 of the Decree on the Building Regulations for Spatial Planning (Besluit bouwregels) and violate the applicable Municipal Zoning Plan (Omgevingsplan Eindhoven), which designates this site strictly as a commercial office zone with no exemptions for external structural alterations without prior planning permission.

On 1 March 2025, we issued a formal notice of non-compliance, granting you ten calendar days to rectify the situation by submitting a permit application or restoring the premises to their original state. To date, we have received neither a valid permit application nor evidence of restoration. Consequently, we are compelled to take coercive measures.

**1. Legal Basis and Proportionality**

In accordance with Article 5:2 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb), the Municipality is authorized to impose administrative coercion. Furthermore, in determining the nature and extent of this coercion, we have observed the principle of proportionality as mandated by Article 1:3 Awb and Article 3:4 GALA (Awb). We have assessed the severity of the breach against your interest in maintaining the modifications. Given that the unauthorized structures pose potential safety risks regarding fire evacuation routes and structural integrity, and that no legitimate planning interest justifies their retention without a permit, the imposition of administrative coercion is deemed necessary and proportionate.

**2. The Order**

You are hereby ordered to:

1.  Demolish the three glazed partitions mentioned above;
2.  Remove the external ventilation shaft from the north-facing elevation;
3.  Restore all affected surfaces to their original condition as recorded in the building’s blueprints dated 1 January 2020;
4.  Provide written confirmation of completion, accompanied by a certificate from an independent structural engineer, within ten (10) days following receipt of this letter.

**3. Periodic Penalty Payment (Dwangsom)**

Should you fail to comply with the order described in paragraph 2 within the stipulated period, or if the work performed is insufficiently compliant, we will impose a periodic penalty payment.

We hereby fix the periodic penalty payment at **€ 25.000** per day of delay until full compliance is achieved. This amount has been calculated based on the financial strength of your company and the gravity of the violation, ensuring it serves as an effective deterrent without being excessive relative to your capacity to pay.

**4. Appeal Rights**

You have the right to appeal this decision in accordance with Article 6:7 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb). The appeal must be submitted in writing to the Municipality of Eindhoven within six weeks from the date of this letter. Please note that filing an appeal does not suspend the execution of this order unless explicitly granted by the competent court.

We expect your immediate attention to this matter to avoid further legal and financial consequences.
