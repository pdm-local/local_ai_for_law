**Re: Novael Cloud B.V.**
**Subject: Response to Request for Suspension of Seizure Measures regarding € 72.500**

Dear Mr. Bahadoer,

We refer to your letter dated 3 April 2025, in which you represented Novael Cloud B.V. (“the Company”) and submitted a formal request to suspend the seizure measures imposed on the Company’s bank accounts, specifically targeting the sum of € 72.500. You argued that the continued detention of these funds causes disproportionate hardship to the Company’s ongoing operations and that the risk of flight or evidence tampering is negligible given the Company’s stable corporate structure.

After a thorough review of the investigation file and the arguments presented in your correspondence, we hereby communicate our decision regarding this request.

**1. Maintenance of Suspicion**

The investigation into suspected violations of Article 420bis of the Dutch Criminal Code (Wetboek van Strafrecht) remains active. The Public Prosecution Service has established sufficient grounds to maintain the suspicion that Novael Cloud B.V. was involved in money laundering activities linked to illicit proceeds from unreported digital service revenues during the fiscal period ending December 2024.

The evidence gathered, including transaction logs and cross-border payment analyses, continues to substantiate the initial charge. There has been no development in the investigation that would warrant dropping the suspicion or terminating the proceedings against the legal entity.

**2. Assessment of the Request for Suspension**

Your request to lift the seizure of € 72.500 is rejected. The decision to maintain these preventive measures is grounded in the following considerations:

*   **Risk of Dissipation:** Although Novael Cloud B.V. operates from a fixed location in Eindhoven, the nature of cloud services allows for rapid asset restructuring and cross-border fund transfers. The specific sum of € 72.500 represents a critical portion of the suspected illicit profits. Allowing its release would significantly increase the risk that these funds could be moved out of the jurisdiction or obscured through complex corporate layering before the final judicial determination.
*   **Proportionality and Necessity:** Under the current phase of the investigation, the seizure is deemed necessary to secure potential future confiscation orders. The Public Prosecution Service considers the financial impact on the Company to be proportionate to the interest of the state in ensuring effective enforcement of criminal law. The Company remains operational through other unseized accounts and revenue streams; therefore, the burden placed on the Company does not outweigh the public interest in preserving the assets for potential forfeiture.

**3. Conclusion**

Consequently, the seizure order dated 15 March 2025 remains in full force and effect. The freeze on the bank account ending in ...8942 at Rabobank Eindhoven, encompassing the balance of € 72.500, will not be lifted.

You are advised that this decision pertains solely to the suspension request regarding the seizure measures. It does not constitute a final judgment on the merits of the case. Should new evidence emerge or if the investigation reaches a stage where further preservation of assets is no longer required, the Public Prosecution Service may revisit this matter upon written request.

Should you wish to appeal this administrative decision, please note that such appeals are subject to strict procedural deadlines under the Dutch Code of Criminal Procedure.
