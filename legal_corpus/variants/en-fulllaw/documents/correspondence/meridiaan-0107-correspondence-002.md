**Re:** Response to Cease and Desist Letter dated April 5, 2025 – Matter Ref: 2025.0107

Dear Sir/Madam,

We refer to your letter of April 5, 2025, in which you demand the immediate removal of certain content hosted on our platform, claiming that such content infringes upon your intellectual property rights. We have conducted a thorough assessment of your request in accordance with our internal compliance procedures and applicable Dutch and European Union law, specifically Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act).

**1. Assessment of the Request**

Upon review, we acknowledge that three specific files hosted by your user, identified as User ID #4492 (name withheld for privacy), constitute a clear violation of your copyright claims under Article 1 of the Dutch Copyright Act. These files were uploaded on March 10, 2025, and contain unauthorized reproductions of your proprietary marketing materials.

However, we must respectfully disagree with your assertion that Lumen Cloud B.V. is liable for the initial publication of this content. As a hosting provider, our liability is governed by Article 6:196c DCC and the relevant provisions of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065. We did not have actual knowledge of the illegal activity prior to your notice, nor did we exercise control over the specific content in question. Therefore, the conditions for intermediary immunity are met, provided that we act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the content upon obtaining such knowledge.

**2. Action Taken (Partial Removal)**

Acting in compliance with our obligations and to mitigate any potential prejudice to Meridiaan Interactive B.V., Lumen Cloud B.V. has taken the following measures effective immediately, April 12, 2025:

1.  The three infringing files referenced in your letter have been permanently deleted from our servers.
2.  Access to the directory containing these files has been revoked for User ID #4492.
3.  We have issued a warning to User ID #4492 regarding future violations, with a notice that further breaches will result in account termination in accordance with our Terms of Service.

**3. Remaining Content**

Your request also demanded the removal of additional material hosted by different users on general forum threads. After careful legal evaluation, we have determined that this remaining content does not meet the threshold for mandatory removal under Article 6:196c DCC. The material in question consists of user-generated commentary and comparative analysis, which falls within the realm of lawful expression and fair use. We cannot fulfill the demand to remove this non-infringing content without violating our duty to maintain an open platform and our own contractual obligations to other users.

**4. Conclusion**

Lumen Cloud B.V. has acted responsibly and in full compliance with its legal duties as a hosting provider. The infringing material has been removed, and we trust that this partial compliance resolves the immediate issue raised in your letter. We reserve all rights available to us under Dutch law and request that Meridiaan Interactive B.V. refrain from further legal action regarding this specific matter unless new facts of illegality are presented.
