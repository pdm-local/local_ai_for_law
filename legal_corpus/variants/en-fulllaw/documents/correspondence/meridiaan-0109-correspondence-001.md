Dear Mr. de Wit,  

We act for Saffier Digital B.V. (hereinafter: "our client") in the above-mentioned matter concerning the claim for spousal maintenance brought by Helix Media B.V. (hereinafter: "Helix").  

Our client has received your letter of 28 February 2025, in which you demand a monthly spousal maintenance payment of € 3.100. Our client expressly contests the necessity and the quantum of this amount.  

**1. Dispute regarding the necessity and amount**  

According to Article 1:157 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC), the court shall determine the amount of spousal maintenance taking into account all circumstances of the case, including the reasonable needs of both parties and their financial means. The determination must be equitable and take into account the duration of the marriage and any other relevant factors.  

Our client argues that the demanded sum of € 3.100 is disproportionately high given the current financial reality. Our client’s net disposable income has decreased significantly due to recent operational restructuring, as evidenced by the audited financial statements for the fiscal year ending 31 December 2024. Furthermore, Helix’s own financial disclosures indicate that their living expenses do not justify such a high maintenance claim, particularly considering that they have substantial personal assets and investment income.  

The figure of € 3.100 represents an unjustified burden on our client’s business continuity and personal financial stability. It is inconsistent with the principle of reasonableness and fairness (redelijkheid en billijkheid) under Article 6:248 DCC, as well as the specific requirements for spousal maintenance under Article 1:157 DCC.  

**2. Request for recalculation**  

In light of the above, our client requests that you provide a detailed breakdown of how Helix has calculated their reasonable needs and how this leads to the demanded amount of € 3.100. Additionally, we request that you recalculate the spousal maintenance based on updated financial data, including:  

- The audited financial statements of Saffier Digital B.V. for FY2024;  
- Helix’s bank statements for the period January 2025 to March 2025;  
- A revised statement of expenses from Helix, itemizing all claimed living costs.  

Our client is prepared to engage in good-faith negotiations to reach a mutually acceptable arrangement that complies with Article 1:157 DCC. However, we cannot accept the current demand as it stands.  

**3. Next steps**  

Please provide the requested documentation and a revised calculation proposal by **14 April 2025**. If no agreement can be reached within this period, our client reserves the right to initiate proceedings to have the spousal maintenance amount determined by the court in accordance with Article 1:157 DCC.  

We trust that you will respond promptly and constructively to this request.
