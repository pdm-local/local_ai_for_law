**Subject: Intended termination of employment contract due to underperformance (Article 7:669 DCC, ground d)**

Dear Management Team of Forta Logistics B.V.,

We refer to the employment contract dated January 12, 2021, concluded between Helix Data B.V. ("the Employer") and Mr. J. van der Meer ("the Employee"), who has been employed as a Senior IT Project Manager at your premises in Rotterdam.

As you are aware, significant concerns regarding the Employee’s performance have persisted since Q3 of last year. Despite previous verbal warnings issued on November 10, 2024, and February 5, 2025, the required improvements were not achieved. Consequently, on March 1, 2025, we jointly implemented a structured improvement plan with clear, measurable objectives to be met by April 1, 2025.

The final evaluation conducted on April 14, 2025, confirms that the Employee has failed to meet the stipulated performance criteria. Specifically, the delivery of the logistics integration module was delayed by three weeks, and critical quality assurance protocols were ignored twice, resulting in data integrity errors for your clients. These shortcomings constitute a serious reason warranting immediate dismissal.

Therefore, we hereby notify you of our intention to terminate the employment contract on grounds of personal circumstances, specifically underperformance, pursuant to **Article 7:669 DCC**, ground d. In accordance with Dutch labor law, this termination will take effect effective May 15, 2025.

During the notice period, we expect the Employee to continue performing his duties with due care and to facilitate a complete handover of all project files and credentials by April 30, 2025. The Employer is prepared to discuss the transition payment amount as required by law, which will be calculated based on the standard formula applicable to long-term employees.

We trust this matter will be handled in a professional manner to ensure a smooth separation. Please confirm receipt of this letter and your agreement on the handover schedule by April 18, 2025.

Sincerely,
