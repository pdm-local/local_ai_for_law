**Subject: Notice of Objection – Kempen Networks B.V. vs. Municipality of Eindhoven**  

Dear Sir/Madam,  

We act on behalf of our client, **Kempen Networks B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Client"), with its registered office at Strijp-S 42, 5611 DD Eindhoven.  

The Client has received your letter dated 10 March 2025 (reference: 2025-9941), imposing a coercive penalty payment (*dwangsom*) of **€ 10.000** for non-compliance with the enforcement notice regarding the temporary installation of fiber-optic infrastructure on private property located at Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven.  

This letter constitutes a formal **Notice of Objection** pursuant to **Article 6:7 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb)**, submitted within the statutory period. We request that you suspend the enforcement of the penalty order pending the decision on this objection.  

### 1. Grounds for Objection  

#### 1.1 Departure from Policy (*Beleidsafwijking*)  
The imposition of a **€ 10.000** penalty in this specific instance constitutes an unjustified departure from the Municipality’s own published enforcement policy (*Handhavingsbeleid Gemeente Eindhoven* 2024).  

According to the Municipality’s guidelines, coercive penalties are considered a measure of last resort. The policy explicitly states that for first-time infractions involving temporary technical installations where no public safety risk exists, the administration must first issue a final warning with a reasonable cure period before imposing financial sanctions. In this case, the Client was notified of the alleged irregularity on 15 February 2025 but had not yet completed the required administrative registration process due to internal delays unrelated to malice. The immediate imposition of a **€ 10.000** penalty bypasses this procedural safeguard.  

Therefore, the decision violates **Article 4:84 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb)**, which prohibits authorities from making decisions that depart from published policy without providing adequate justification or considering the specific circumstances of the case. The Municipality failed to demonstrate why a less intrusive measure was insufficient in this instance.  

#### 1.2 Disproportionality  
The amount of **€ 10.000** is disproportionate to the nature of the violation and the Client’s financial position. Kempen Networks B.V. is a small-to-medium enterprise with limited turnover relative to larger contractors. The alleged breach did not result in any actual damage to public interests, nor did it pose an immediate threat to public order or safety.  

Imposing a penalty of this magnitude for a procedural delay in a technical installation creates an imbalance between the objective pursued and the burden placed on the Client. Under general principles of administrative law, which are incorporated into the GALA, sanctions must be commensurate with the severity of the fault. The total lack of prior warning renders the sanction punitive rather than coercive, thereby exceeding the bounds of proportionality.  

### 2. Request  

In light of the above, we request that the Municipality of Eindhoven:  

1. **Quash** the penalty order No. 2025-9941 in its entirety due to the violation of **Article 4:84 GALA (Awb)** and the principle of proportionality;  
2. Alternatively, reduce the penalty to **€ 0**, acknowledging that the Client has now complied with all registration requirements as of 1 April 2025;  
3. **Suspend** the enforcement of the penalty pending the final decision on this objection.  

We reserve the right to appeal this decision to the Administrative Law Division of the District Court of The Hague should our objection be rejected.  

Please confirm receipt of this notice and provide a written decision within eight weeks, as required by law.
