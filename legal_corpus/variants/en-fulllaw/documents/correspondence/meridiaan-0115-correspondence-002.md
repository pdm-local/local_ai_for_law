**Date:** 12 April 2025

**Subject:** Re: Insolvency proceedings Nimbus Interactive B.V. – Set-off claim and clarification on payment of € 320.000

Dear mr. van Raay,

We acknowledge receipt of your letter dated 28 March 2025, in which you informed us that the court has declared Nimbus Interactive B.V. bankrupt and that you have been appointed as the curator. We write to you on behalf of our client, Polderzicht Media B.V., regarding the outstanding matters between our parties.

Regarding the mutual claims, we wish to formally invoke the right of set-off pursuant to **Article 53 Bankruptcy Act**. As both our claim against Nimbus Interactive B.V. and the claim Nimbus Interactive B.V. has against us arose prior to the date of bankruptcy, and since both obligations are of a similar nature (monetary debts), set-off is legally permissible. We therefore declare that the amount owed by Polderzicht Media B.V. to the bankruptcy estate is set off against the claim held by Polderzicht Media B.V. against Nimbus Interactive B.V.

Furthermore, regarding your inquiry concerning the payment of **€ 320.000** made by Polderzicht Media B.V. to Nimbus Interactive B.V. on 15 February 2024, we wish to place on record that our client denies any knowledge of prejudice resulting from this transaction. The payment was made in accordance with the agreed invoice terms (Invoice No. PM-2024-089) for services rendered under our collaboration agreement dated 10 January 2023. At the time of the transfer, Nimbus Interactive B.V. was fully solvent and operational. There is no basis to assume that this specific payment constituted a fraudulent preference or an act detrimental to the other creditors, nor did our client have any reason to suspect insolvency proceedings were imminent.

We request that you review the set-off declaration in light of Article 53 Bankruptcy Act and adjust your records accordingly. Should you require further documentation to substantiate the origin and timing of the mutual debts, please indicate this within fourteen days.
