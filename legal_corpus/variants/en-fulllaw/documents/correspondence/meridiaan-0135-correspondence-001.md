**Subject: Formal Notice of Unlawful Content – Demand for Immediate Removal (notice-and-takedown)**

Dear Sir/Madam,

We act on behalf of our client, **Helix Online B.V.** (“Helix”), regarding the illegal publication of protected intellectual property and unlawful content on your social media platform, “Aurora Stream” (“Platform”). This letter serves as a formal notice under the applicable provisions of Dutch law and EU regulations governing intermediary liability.

**1. Factual Background**

On 12 January 2025, Helix identified specific user-generated content on the Platform that infringes upon its exclusive rights and violates general terms of service and statutory obligations. The offending content consists of a video file titled *“Helix Exclusive: Behind the Scenes Q4 2024”* which was uploaded by an unknown user with the handle @streamer_x99 to the URL: `https://www.aurorastream.com/watch?v=xyz123abc`.

This video contains high-definition footage and audio recordings that are the sole proprietary property of Helix Online B.V. The content was disseminated without any license, authorization, or consent from Helix. The publication constitutes a direct infringement of our client’s copyright and creates an immediate threat of further unauthorized distribution.

**2. Legal Basis for Removal Request**

As the hosting provider of the Platform, Aurora Media B.V. is subject to the due diligence obligations set forth in **Article 6:196c DCC**, which implements the liability regime for information society services. Under this framework, while providers are generally not liable for third-party content, they must act expeditiously to remove or disable access to illegal content upon obtaining actual knowledge of its unlawfulness.

Furthermore, Regulation **(EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act)** imposes strict transparency and cooperation duties on Very Large Online Platforms. The continued availability of this infringing material violates the integrity of the Platform’s ecosystem and our client’s rights. We hereby invoke the **notice-and-takedown** procedure to demand the immediate removal of the identified content.

**3. Demand for Action**

Helix Online B.V. requires Aurora Media B.V. to take the following actions immediately:

1.  **Remove** the infringing video file titled *“Helix Exclusive: Behind the Scenes Q4 2024”* from the Platform and any associated caches or mirrors within 24 hours of receipt of this letter;
2.  **Disable access** to the URL `https://www.aurorastream.com/watch?v=xyz123abc` permanently;
3.  **Preserve** all logs and data related to the account @streamer_x99, including IP addresses, registration details, and upload timestamps, for potential legal proceedings or handover to law enforcement authorities under Article 6:196c DCC;
4.  **Provide written confirmation** of the removal and preservation measures taken by 17 January 2025 at 17:00 CET.

**4. Consequences of Non-Compliance**

Failure to comply with this request within the stipulated timeframe will be regarded as a refusal to cooperate with the notice-and-takedown mechanism. Helix Online B.V. reserves the right to initiate legal proceedings against Aurora Media B.V. for joint liability regarding damages resulting from the continued unlawful dissemination of its content. Such proceedings may include claims for injunctive relief, compensation for material and non-material damages, and recovery of all legal costs pursuant to Article 1019h DCCP.

We trust that Aurora Media B.V. will act promptly to uphold its obligations under Dutch law and EU regulation. Please direct all future correspondence regarding this matter to the undersigned.
