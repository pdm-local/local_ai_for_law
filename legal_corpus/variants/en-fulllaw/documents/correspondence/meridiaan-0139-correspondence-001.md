**Subject: Notice of additional assessment and imposition of a penalty for corporate income tax year 2023**

Dear Sir or Madam,

We refer to the corporate income tax return filed by Klaver Logistics B.V. for the fiscal year 2023, which was submitted on 15 May 2024. Following our routine audit of your declared profits and cross-referencing with data obtained from third-party financial intermediaries, we have identified discrepancies in the reported revenue figures.

Specifically, our examination reveals that income derived from international transit services performed between January and March 2024 was not included in your tax base. This information constitutes new facts or circumstances within the meaning of Article 16 AWR (State Taxes Act), as this data was not available to you at the time of filing the original return and could not reasonably have been anticipated by the Administration earlier.

Consequently, we are obligated to make an additional assessment. The corrected taxable profit for the fiscal year 2023 is higher than initially declared. After applying the relevant corporate income tax rates, the resulting unpaid tax amount amounts to € 460.000.

Furthermore, in light of the nature of the omission and our assessment of your level of culpability, we have applied a penalty under Article 67d AWR. We consider the failure to declare this significant revenue stream to be attributable to gross negligence on the part of the administration of Klaver Logistics B.V. Therefore, we impose a penalty surcharge of 50% on the unpaid tax amount.

The calculation is as follows:
1. Unpaid principal tax: € 460.000
2. Penalty (50%): € 230.000
3. Total amount due: € 690.000

You are hereby requested to pay the total sum of € 690.000 within fourteen days of the date of this letter to account number NL12ABNA0417164000, in the name of Dutch Tax Administration, under reference 2025-INT-AW-8842.

If you wish to contest this additional assessment and penalty, you may file an objection with us within six weeks after the date of this notice, in accordance with the General Administrative Law Act (Awb). Please note that filing an objection does not suspend the obligation to pay unless we explicitly grant a deferral.

We remain at your disposal for any further questions regarding this matter.
