**Subject:** Legal Advice Request – Termination of Employment Contract and Non-Compete Clause (Mr. J. van den Berg)

Dear mr. Havelaar,

We are writing to you regarding the ongoing negotiation between our client, Klaver Software B.V., and its former employee, Mr. Jan van den Berg, who was employed by Tinexus Systems B.V. until 15 February 2025.

As you are aware, the employment contract in question contains a non-compete clause stipulating a restriction period of 12 months following termination. Our client is willing to agree to the immediate dissolution of the employment relationship effective today, 14 March 2025, provided that Tinexus Systems B.V. waives the enforcement of this non-compete clause.

Tinexus Systems B.V. has indicated a preference to retain the restrictive covenant, arguing that Mr. van den Berg had access to sensitive client lists and proprietary source code during his tenure. However, Klaver Software B.V. considers this enforcement disproportionate given that Mr. van den Berg’s role was primarily administrative and that he has not engaged in any competitive activity since his departure.

We request your legal opinion on the following:

1.  Under **Article 7:653 DCC**, what are our chances of successfully challenging the validity or enforceability of this non-compete clause if Tinexus Systems B.V. refuses to waive it?
2.  Is there a strategic advantage in formally requesting the waiver in writing as part of the termination settlement, and would such a request be viewed favorably by the subdistrict court in the event of future litigation?
3.  Please advise on the specific wording we should use in the termination agreement to ensure that the non-compete clause is explicitly nullified upon signing.

We look forward to your prompt response so that we may proceed with finalizing the separation package.
