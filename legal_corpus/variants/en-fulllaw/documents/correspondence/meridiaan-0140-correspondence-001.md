Dear Sirs,

We act on behalf of our client, **Nimbus Retail B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Landlord"), pursuant to the general power of attorney provided by the aforementioned company. We refer to the lease agreement concluded between the Landlord and your company, **Stroomdal Media B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Tenant"), for the commercial premises located at Herestraat 42 in Nijmegen (hereinafter: "the Premises").

It has come to our client's attention that the Tenant is in serious default of its primary obligations under the lease agreement. Specifically, the Tenant has failed to pay the monthly rental amount of **€ 9.500**, exclusive of value added tax and service costs, for the last **4 months**. The unpaid periods cover the months of November 2024, December 2024, January 2025, and February 2025.

Consequently, the total outstanding principal amount amounts to **€ 38.000** (thirty-eight thousand euros), not including statutory interest and any accrued administrative fees as stipulated in clause 4.2 of the lease agreement. Despite multiple informal reminders sent by our client’s property management department on 15 December 2024 and 10 January 2025, no payment has been received, nor has any communication regarding the inability to pay been provided by your company.

Given this persistent breach of contract, our client considers it necessary to take immediate legal action to protect its financial interests. Pursuant to **Article 7:274 DCC** (Book 7, Title 7, Section 10 of the Dutch Civil Code), a landlord may dissolve the lease if the tenant fails to perform an obligation arising from the agreement, provided that such failure constitutes a ground for dissolution under the law or the contract.

Therefore, in accordance with Article 7:274 DCC and the relevant provisions of the lease agreement, we hereby formally notify you that:

1.  The lease agreement between Nimbus Retail B.V. and Stroomdal Media B.V. is deemed **dissolved** as of today’s date due to your material breach;
2.  You are required to vacate the Premises at Herestraat 42, 6811 CL Nijmegen, and return them in good condition, with all keys and access cards, no later than **30 days** from the receipt of this letter;
3.  You are ordered to pay the outstanding rent arrears totaling **€ 38.000**, plus statutory interest from the due date of each monthly installment until the date of full payment, within **14 days** of receipt of this correspondence.

Failure to comply with these demands will result in our client initiating proceedings before the competent court to enforce eviction and claim the full amount of damages, including legal costs and collection expenses. Such litigation will be pursued without further notice.

We expect your immediate cooperation to resolve this matter amicably and avoid unnecessary judicial intervention.
