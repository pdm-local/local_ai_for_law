**Re: Matter 2025.0109 – Child maintenance obligations under Article 1:404 DCC**

Dear Mr. van der Meer,

We represent Helix Media B.V. (hereinafter: "our client") in the above-referenced matter concerning the determination of child maintenance for the minor L. de Vries. You have recently submitted your latest financial statements and income declarations for the fiscal year 2024 to facilitate this calculation.

Our client acknowledges receipt of these documents. After a thorough review of the provided figures, we wish to confirm that our client accepts the proposed monthly child maintenance amount of **390** euros. This acceptance is based on the following legal and factual grounds:

1.  **Statutory Basis**: The obligation to provide maintenance for children is governed by Article 1:404 of the Dutch Civil Code (Burgerlijk Wetboek). Under this provision, parents are required to maintain their minor children in proportion to their respective means and income.

2.  **Assessment of Means**: We have compared the net taxable incomes of both parties as reported in your submission against the standard guidelines established by the Trema Institute (Trema-normen), which serve as the primary benchmark for determining reasonable maintenance amounts in the Netherlands. The calculation reflects a proportional share based on the income disparity between our client and Saffier Digital B.V., taking into account the specific household situation and the needs of the minor.

3.  **Reasonableness**: The figure of **390** euros per month aligns with the Trema standards for the combined income bracket and the number of care days allocated to each parent. Our client has verified that this amount does not constitute an undue burden given your reported financial position, nor is it insufficient relative to our client's obligations.

Consequently, Helix Media B.V. hereby agrees to the payment of **390** euros per month for child maintenance, effective from 1 May 2025, until the minor reaches the age of majority or completes secondary education, in accordance with Article 1:404 DCC.

Please ensure that the direct debit mandate is arranged with your bank to guarantee timely payment on the first day of each month. Failure to pay may result in the initiation of enforcement proceedings without further notice.

We trust this clarifies the matter to your satisfaction. Should you have any questions regarding the calculation details, please do not hesitate to contact us.
