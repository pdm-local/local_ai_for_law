**Re: Service Agreement dated 15 January 2025 between Orion Interactive B.V. and Novael Networks B.V.**
**Matter reference: 2025.0104**

Dear Sirs,

We act for Orion Interactive B.V. (hereinafter: "Orion") in connection with the above-referenced Service Agreement (hereinafter: "the Agreement"). We are writing to you regarding a discrepancy concerning the limitation of liability provisions within the executed contract.

Upon our client's recent internal review of the final signed copy, it has come to our attention that Clause 12.3 ("Limitation of Liability") contains conflicting figures. The main body of the clause specifies a maximum aggregate liability cap of **50.000** euros. However, the introductory text of the same clause references a different threshold in relation to previous draft versions circulated during negotiations.

To ensure legal certainty and prevent any future disputes regarding the scope of potential claims, Orion requires formal confirmation from Novael Networks B.V. as to which figure constitutes the final, binding liability cap under the Agreement. Specifically, we request your written confirmation that the limit is definitively set at **50.000** euros, and that no other conflicting thresholds apply.

Please note that in the event of such ambiguity, our client reserves all rights to interpret the contract in accordance with the standards of reasonableness and fairness as prescribed by Article 6:248 DCC. It is our expectation that both parties intend for the explicitly stated figure in the operative part of the clause to prevail, but we require your explicit confirmation to close this matter.

We kindly request your response no later than 19 April 2025.
