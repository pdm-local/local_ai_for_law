**Re:** Saffier Logistics B.V. – Request for suspension of seizure and lifting of charges

Dear Mr. Bahadoer,

We refer to your letter dated 3 April 2025 regarding the above-mentioned matter. In that correspondence, you requested the suspension of the investigative measures concerning the financial assets of your client, Saffier Logistics B.V., specifically asking for the return of the seized funds amounting to € 48.000.

After careful review of the case file and the arguments presented in your letter, we must inform you that our request is denied. The Public Prosecution Service maintains its suspicion against Saffier Logistics B.V. regarding violations of Article 420bis of the Criminal Code (money laundering). The suspicion is based on substantial objective elements indicating that the funds in question originate from illicit activities related to the company’s logistics operations between January and March 2025.

The seizure of the assets was imposed under the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure as a precautionary measure to ensure that the proceeds of crime can be recovered should the investigation lead to a conviction. The value of € 48.000 is directly linked to the suspected illicit transactions. We have determined that there are serious grounds to believe that these funds constitute proceeds of crime.

Regarding your request for suspension, we note that the investigative phase is still ongoing. Critical forensic accounting analyses are currently being finalized by our financial crime unit. These analyses are essential to trace the flow of capital and establish the link between the seized assets and the alleged criminal conduct. Lifting the seizure at this stage would jeopardize the effectiveness of the investigation and the possibility of subsequent recovery proceedings.

Furthermore, we reject the argument that the seizure disproportionately affects the normal operations of Saffier Logistics B.V. The company has other liquidity reserves available for daily operations, as evidenced by the bank statements submitted during the initial inquiry. The partial release of funds requested is not justified given the severity of the suspicion and the risk of dissipation of assets.

Consequently, we maintain the suspicion and keep the seizure of € 48.000 in place until further notice. We will review this decision again once the forensic accounting report has been submitted to our office, expected no earlier than June 2025.

You are advised to continue to cooperate fully with the investigation. Any attempt to conceal or transfer assets may lead to more severe legal consequences.
