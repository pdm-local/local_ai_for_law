**Re: Notification of defect and partial suspension of rent — Lease Agreement dated 1 January 2023**

Dear mr. Koster,

We write to you on behalf of our client, Stroomdal Media B.V., concerning the lease agreement for the commercial premises located at Keizersgracht 124, Amsterdam, currently occupied by Nimbus Retail B.V. as the landlord and ourselves as the tenant.

It has come to our urgent attention that a significant water leak originating from the roof of the building has remained unrepaired for several months. The first formal notification of this defect was sent to your office on 10 November 2024, followed by reminders on 5 December 2024 and 20 January 2025. Despite these communications, no effective remedial action has been taken, and the leakage persists, causing substantial damage to our inventory and disrupting our business operations.

Under Article 7:206 DCC, the landlord is obligated to carry out all repairs necessary for the proper functioning of the leased property, unless such repairs are minor and explicitly placed on the tenant by agreement. The leak in question clearly exceeds the threshold of minor maintenance and falls squarely within the landlord’s statutory duty to ensure the premises remain fit for their intended commercial use. The failure to address this defect constitutes a breach of the lease agreement and a violation of the legal standards governing business tenancies.

Consequently, Stroomdal Media B.V. hereby exercises its right to suspend a portion of the rent payable for the period from 1 February 2025 onwards, due to the diminished utility and value of the leased premises during this time. We calculate the partial suspension at €850 per month until the defect is fully repaired and the premises are restored to their proper condition. This amount reflects the loss of usable square footage and the operational hindrances caused by the ongoing water damage.

We demand that Nimbus Retail B.V. commence repairs no later than 28 March 2025. Failure to comply will force us to seek judicial relief, including a court-ordered dissolution of the lease under Article 7:671b DCC and claim for damages, should we be compelled to relocate or cease operations temporarily.

We await your immediate confirmation that repair works have been commissioned by 28 March 2025.
