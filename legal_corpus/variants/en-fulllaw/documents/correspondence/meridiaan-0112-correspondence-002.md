**Subject: Notification of Defect and Partial Suspension of Rent**

Dear mr. Koster,

We write to you in our capacity as the landlord of the commercial premises located at Industrieweg 88, Eindhoven, currently leased by Brightlane Technologies B.V. under the above-referenced agreement.

This letter serves to formally notify your company that a significant defect has persisted on the property for several months without adequate remediation. Specifically, a continuous water leak originating from the roof structure above the main server room and adjacent office spaces has been evident since early January 2025. Despite our receipt of multiple informal complaints via email in February and March 2025, no permanent repair has been executed. The situation has worsened, causing visible dampness on the ceilings and posing a risk to your IT infrastructure.

In accordance with **Article 7:206 DCC**, which establishes the landlord’s obligation to keep the leased property in good repair during the term of the lease, we acknowledge that the current state of the premises fails to meet this statutory requirement. The inability to use the premises for their intended purpose due to this defect entitles us to demand appropriate repairs from you as the tenant to mitigate further damage, but it also highlights your right to seek relief regarding the rent due to the diminished utility of the property.

Consequently, Polderzicht Digital B.V. hereby declares that we are suspending 30% of the monthly rent for the period of April 2025. The total rent for this month is reduced from €4,500.00 to €3,150.00. This amount will be reflected in our invoice due on 1 May 2025.

We demand that Brightlane Technologies B.V. immediately arrange for professional contractors to assess and repair the roof leak. We insist that this work be completed no later than 30 April 2025. Failure to rectify this defect promptly may result in further legal measures, including a full suspension of rent or claims for damages incurred due to business interruption.

We await your immediate confirmation that repairs have been commissioned.

Sincerely,
