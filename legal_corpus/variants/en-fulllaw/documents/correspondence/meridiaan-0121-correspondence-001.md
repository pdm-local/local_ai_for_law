**Re: Immediate takedown of infringing content – Matter Ref: ZN-ATK-2025-0410**

Dear Sir/Madam,

We act on behalf of our client, Zenith Networks B.V. ("Zenith"), a leading provider of specialized industrial automation software. We write to you in your capacity as the operator of the online platform accessible at www.aurorasystems.eu (the "Platform").

It has come to our attention that users of your Platform are distributing unlicensed copies of Zenith’s proprietary software, specifically the "Zenith Core Suite v4.2" and associated technical documentation. This distribution constitutes a clear violation of Dutch copyright law and European Union intellectual property directives.

The specific content in question is located at the following URL:
`https://www.aurorasystems.eu/forum/threads/unlicensed-zenth-core-v4-2-download.89214/`

This post was created on 5 April 2025 by a user with the handle "TechDistributor" and has received over five hundred views since its publication. The attached files include executable installers and source code snippets that are protected under Article 1 of the Dutch Copyright Act (Auteurswet).

As the provider of this information society service, Aurora Systems B.V. benefits from the liability exemption for hosting services provided under Article 6:196c DCC, subject to compliance with the "notice-and-takedown" procedure. Upon receipt of this notice, which contains precise and sufficient details enabling the identification of the illegal content, you are obligated to act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the infringing material.

We therefore formally request that you:

1. Immediately remove the aforementioned post and all associated files from your Platform;
2. Disable access to any mirrors or cached versions hosted within your network;
3. Suspend the account of "TechDistributor" pending a review of your terms of service regarding intellectual property infringement;
4. Provide written confirmation of these actions within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt of this letter.

Failure to comply with this request will leave Zenith Networks B.V. no choice but to pursue all available legal remedies, including but not limited to claims for damages under Article 6:162 DCC and injunctive relief before the competent court. We reserve all rights.

We expect your prompt cooperation in resolving this matter.
