We act as legal counsel to **Hanze Data B.V.** (hereinafter: "the Client"), a company registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 87.654.321. We hereby submit this notice of objection against the penalty order issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven on 10 February 2025, reference no. 2025-EIN-892.

The penalty order imposes a fine of **€ 25.000** on our Client for alleged violations concerning the unauthorized use of public space for server cooling infrastructure at its registered address. Our Client requests the immediate withdrawal of this penalty order on the grounds that it constitutes a disproportionate measure and represents a departure from established administrative policy, contrary to the principles of lawfulness and fairness in administrative decision-making.

### 1. Factual Background

Our Client operates a data center facility located at Kennedyplein 45, Eindhoven. In late 2024, our Client initiated a project to upgrade its cooling systems to comply with new energy efficiency standards. During the installation phase, temporary piping was placed on the sidewalk adjacent to the building for a period of four days between 15 and 19 January 2025.

The Municipality of Eindhoven issued the penalty order on 10 February 2025, alleging that this placement violated general permit regulations regarding public space usage. However, prior to the installation, our Client’s project manager, Mr. J. van der Meer, received verbal confirmation from a municipal official at the counter service desk on 10 January 2025 that no formal permit was required for such short-term maintenance work, provided the sidewalk remained passable. This verbal assurance is documented in our internal email correspondence dated 11 January 2025, attached hereto as Annex A.

### 2. Departure from Administrative Policy (Article 4:84 GALA)

The imposition of a severe financial penalty in this instance constitutes an unjustified departure from consistent administrative policy. For several years, the Municipality of Eindhoven has applied a lenient enforcement policy towards short-term (<7 days) technical maintenance activities that do not disrupt public order or safety. This policy is evident in previous enforcement records regarding similar minor infractions by local businesses in the Kennedyplein area, where warnings were issued instead of financial penalties.

By imposing a heavy fine without prior warning or opportunity to rectify the situation, the Municipality has departed from its own established practice without providing adequate motivation for this deviation. According to **Article 4:84 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb)**, an authority may only deviate from published policy or consistent practice if there are compelling reasons related to the public interest or the specific circumstances of the case. No such compelling reason exists here, as no harm was caused to third parties or public order.

### 3. Disproportionality and Principle of Fairness

The penalty of **€ 25.000** is grossly disproportionate to the alleged infringement. The violation was minor, temporary, and rectified immediately upon completion of the work. Our Client is a SME with no previous history of administrative violations. Imposing such a high sanction for a technical oversight, especially in light of the verbal assurance received from municipal staff, violates the principle of proportionality and the requirement for reasonable decision-making under **Article 6:7 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb)**.

Article 6:7 GALA mandates that administrative bodies must act with due care and fairness. The failure to verify the client's compliance through a formal channel before the work commenced, followed by an immediate harsh penalty, demonstrates a lack of procedural fairness. The sanction fails to meet the requirements of suitability and necessity, as a mere warning would have sufficed to ensure future compliance.

### 4. Conclusion and Request

In light of the above, and relying on **Article 6:7 GALA** regarding the duty of care and **Article 4:84 GALA** regarding adherence to administrative policy, we request that the Municipality of Eindhoven:

1. Immediately withdraw penalty order no. 2025-EIN-892 in its entirety;
2. Refund any amounts already paid by our Client under protest;
3. Confirm in writing that no further enforcement measures will be taken regarding this specific incident.

We expect your response within the statutory objection period. Should you fail to withdraw the penalty, we are instructed to initiate administrative litigation proceedings before the competent court, with all costs to be claimed against the Municipality.
