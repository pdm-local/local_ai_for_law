Re: Matter 2025.0137 – Dispute concerning spousal maintenance between Novael Systems B.V. and Hanze Interactive B.V.

Dear Mr. de Wit,

I represent Hanze Interactive B.V. (hereinafter: "our client") in the above-referenced matter. I am writing to you regarding the recent calculation of spousal maintenance submitted by your side.

Our client has reviewed the proposed amount and wishes to formally dispute the necessity and magnitude of the requested sum. Specifically, our client objects to the claimed monthly spousal maintenance of € 2.400. In our client’s view, this figure is disproportionately high given the current financial reality and the standard of living established during the marriage.

As you are aware, the assessment of spousal maintenance is governed by **Article 1:157 DCC**. This provision dictates that the obligation to provide maintenance must be determined in light of all circumstances of the case, including the parties' income, assets, and personal needs. It is our position that the current proposal fails to adequately weigh these factors against each other, particularly regarding our client's reduced disposable income following recent business restructuring.

Therefore, we request that you recalculate the spousal maintenance amount based on a more accurate assessment of both parties' financial positions. We propose that the maintenance be adjusted to reflect a fair and reasonable contribution, taking into account the actual net income available after tax and necessary living expenses.

We look forward to your prompt response with a revised proposal or a schedule for further negotiation. Please note that our client is willing to engage in good faith discussions to reach an amicable settlement, provided that the final amount aligns with the legal standards set forth in **Article 1:157 DCC**.
