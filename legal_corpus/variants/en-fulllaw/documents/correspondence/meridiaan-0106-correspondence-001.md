**Subject: Cease and Desist / Demand for Payment regarding infringement of the "Cascade" intellectual property rights**

Dear Sirs,

We act for **Nordlicht Retail B.V.**, hereinafter referred to as "our client", with regard to the above matter.

It has come to our client's attention that **Verdana Digital B.V.** is currently engaging in activities that constitute a renewed infringement of our client's exclusive intellectual property rights concerning the work and brand known as **"Cascade"**. Specifically, your company has republished unauthorized derivative works and utilized the protected "Cascade" logo on its new digital platform, which was launched on 1 May 2025.

This conduct is in direct violation of **Article 13 Dutch Copyright Act**, which protects the exclusive rights of the author to reproduce and publish the work, as well as **Article 2.20 BCIP**, regarding trademark infringement and unfair competition. Our client holds valid proof of registration and creation for these assets.

Reference is made to the settlement agreement concluded between our parties on 15 January 2024 (hereinafter: "the Settlement"). In Article 4 of that Settlement, you expressly acknowledged the prior infringements and agreed to a strict prohibition against any further violation. Furthermore, Article 5 of the Settlement stipulates a contractual penalty of **€ 5.000** per distinct breach of the non-infringement obligation.

The current actions by your company constitute at least two separate breaches of the Settlement agreement:
1. The unauthorized use of the "Cascade" logo on your website; and
2. The publication of derivative content based on our client's copyrighted material.

Consequently, the contractual penalty of **€ 5.000** is immediately due and payable for each breach, resulting in a total claim of **€ 10.000**.

We hereby formally demand that you:

1. **Immediately cease** all infringements of our client's intellectual property rights;
2. **Remove** all infringing content and logos from your website and digital platforms within seven (7) days of receipt of this letter;
3. **Pay** the sum of **€ 10.000** to our client’s bank account (IBAN: NL91 MERI 1234 5678 90, under reference "Matter 2025.0106") within fourteen (14) days of receipt; and
4. **Provide written confirmation** of compliance with the above demands within the aforementioned periods.

Should you fail to comply with these demands within the specified time limits, our client reserves all rights to pursue legal action without further notice. This will include claims for full compensation for damages, statutory interest, and all extrajudicial and judicial costs incurred.

We expect your immediate cooperation in resolving this matter amicably.
