**Suspicion of money laundering / Fraudulent preference**
**Seizure of funds amounting to € 31.000**

Dear mr. Bahadoer,

We refer to your letter of 28 February 2025 in which you requested the suspension of the seizure ordered against Orion Systems B.V. (hereinafter: "the Company") and the return of the seized funds.

After reviewing your arguments, we must inform you that our request for the suspension of the seizure is rejected. The suspicion against the Company remains intact.

**1. Maintenance of Suspicion**

The investigation into potential financial irregularities involving the Company continues to yield substantial indications that satisfy the threshold for a reasonable suspicion under Article 420bis of the Criminal Code. Specifically, the pattern of transfers leading up to the insolvency filing suggests an intent to place assets beyond the reach of creditors in a manner consistent with fraudulent preference. The evidence gathered during the search at the Company’s registered office on 15 February 2025 supports this line of inquiry. There is no new factual development that would warrant dropping the suspicion or halting the investigative measures.

**2. Rejection of Suspension Request**

You have argued that the seizure imposes an undue burden on the Company’s liquidity, preventing it from meeting daily operational costs and paying employee salaries. However, the proportionality principle must be balanced against the interest of preserving evidence and ensuring the availability of assets for potential restitution to victims or the state.

The amount seized was precisely determined based on the estimated value of the disputed transactions. The figure in question is **€ 31.000**. This sum represents a specific tranche of funds identified as proceeds of the alleged unlawful acts. Lifting this seizure would compromise the effectiveness of the investigation and the potential recovery of assets.

While we acknowledge the operational difficulties, the Company is not entirely without recourse. The seized accounts are frozen only up to the amount of € 31.000. Funds exceeding this threshold remain accessible to the Company for legitimate business operations. Furthermore, the Company may submit a separate request for the provision of necessary funds for payroll purposes, subject to strict verification and conditional release mechanisms proposed by the investigating officer.

**3. Conclusion**

The seizure remains in force. The suspicion is maintained, and no further investigative steps will be suspended at this time. We reserve the right to expand the scope of the seizure should new evidence indicate a higher value of illicit proceeds.
