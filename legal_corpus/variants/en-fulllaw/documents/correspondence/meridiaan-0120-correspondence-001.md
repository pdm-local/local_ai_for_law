Dear Sirs,

We act on behalf of Cobalt Software B.V., with its registered office in Rotterdam (Kerkstraat 12), hereinafter referred to as "our client". Our client has instructed us to address you formally regarding the unauthorized use and distribution of its proprietary software product known as "Cascade" and the associated "Cascade" trademark.

**1. Factual Background**

As you are aware, our client is the sole holder of the copyright in the source code and executable files of the "Cascade" application, registered under Article 13 of the Dutch Copyright Act (*Auteurswet*). Furthermore, our client holds the exclusive rights to the "Cascade" brand name as a registered trademark pursuant to Article 2.20 of the Dutch Civil Code, Book 2 (BCIP), registration number NL-B-2491005.

On 12 February 2024, following an initial investigation by Meridiaan Advocaten into your unauthorized reproduction and resale of "Cascade" licenses, both parties entered into a settlement agreement (*Vaststellingsovereenkomst*). In this agreement, you acknowledged the infringement and committed to immediately cease all infringing activities. Crucially, the settlement included a contractual penalty clause stipulating that any future breach of the cessation obligation would result in a forfeited penalty of € 5.000 per distinct act of infringement, payable to our client without need for further proof of actual damage.

**2. Current Infringements**

It has come to our client's attention that on 2 May 2025, Tinexus Digital B.V. began offering "Cascade" software keys via its online platform (tinexus-digital.nl/store). Specifically:

1.  You are distributing copies of the "Cascade" software without authorization from our client, violating our client’s exclusive rights under Article 13 Dutch Copyright Act.
2.  You are using the "Cascade" designation in your commercial communications and product listings, thereby infringing on our client’s registered trademark rights under Article 2.20 BCIP.

Evidence of these activities, including screenshots dated 3 May 2025 and purchase records confirming the sale of unauthorized licenses to third parties on 7 May 2025, has been securely preserved by our client as evidence for potential litigation.

**3. Legal Consequences and Demand**

Your actions constitute a clear and renewed violation of the settlement agreement concluded on 12 February 2024. Consequently, the contractual penalty of € 5.000 per breach is now immediately due and payable. Furthermore, your continued use of the software and trademark constitutes an ongoing unlawful act (*onrechtmatige daad*) against our client’s intellectual property rights.

We hereby demand that you:

1.  Immediately cease all reproduction, distribution, marketing, and sale of the "Cascade" software and any derivatives thereof;
2.  Remove all references to "Cascade" from your website, marketing materials, and third-party platforms;
3.  Pay the forfeited penalty of € 5.000 for each confirmed act of infringement (currently totaling at least € 10.000 based on two distinct incidents) to our client’s bank account within fourteen (14) days of receipt of this letter;
4.  Provide written confirmation of compliance with these demands within the same period.

**4. Conclusion**

Should you fail to comply with this demand within the specified deadline, we have received explicit instructions from our client to initiate legal proceedings against Tinexus Digital B.V. This will include claims for damages beyond the contractual penalty, recovery of all legal costs (including attorney fees pursuant to Article 1019h DCCP), and a request for a provisional measure to enforce cessation.

We trust that you will appreciate the seriousness with which our client views the protection of its intellectual property and will act accordingly to avoid further escalation.
