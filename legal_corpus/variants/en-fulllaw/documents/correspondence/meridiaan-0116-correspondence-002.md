Dear Mr. Nolte,

We act as the legal counsel and claims handler for Tinexus Media B.V. (hereinafter "our Insured"). We refer to your letter of 28 February 2025 regarding the accident that occurred on 12 January 2025 at the premises of Orion Media B.V., resulting in damage to proprietary equipment and alleged consequential loss.

**1. Admission of Liability**

Our investigation into the circumstances of the incident confirms that our Insured, through its employee Mr. J. van der Meer, inadvertently caused physical damage to the server rack belonging to Orion Media B.V. due to a failure to observe safety markings during the scheduled maintenance window. Accordingly, Tinexus Media B.V. admits tortious liability for the direct physical damage in an amount of € 15.000,00.

**2. Dispute Regarding Quantum and Contributory Negligence**

We have reviewed your claim for a total sum of € 310.000,00. While we acknowledge that damages are recoverable, we fundamentally dispute the figure you have presented. Your calculation includes significant amounts for alleged business interruption and loss of profit, which we argue are not sufficiently substantiated by direct evidence of causation.

Furthermore, we invoke the doctrine of contributory negligence pursuant to Article 6:101 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC). Our analysis reveals that Orion Media B.V. failed to comply with its duty of care under Article 6:248(2) DCC in the following respects:

*   **Inadequate Signage:** The area where the accident occurred was not clearly marked with temporary safety barriers or high-visibility signage, despite being an active maintenance zone.
*   **Lack of Supervision:** Orion Media B.V. did not provide a site supervisor to guide our Insured’s employees during the critical phase of the work.
*   **Procedural Non-Compliance:** The access protocol required by Article 7:206 DCC (mutually applicable principles of care in tenancy/maintenance contexts) was not enforced by your facility management team.

Given these omissions, we contend that Orion Media B.V. is at least 40% responsible for the extent of the damage suffered.

**3. Proposed Settlement**

Taking into account the admitted direct damage (€ 15.000,00) and applying a 60% liability ratio to the total claimed amount based on our apportionment of fault, we propose a settlement offer of € 186.000,00. This figure represents a fair compromise given the disputed nature of the consequential losses.

We request your written confirmation of this offer by 14 April 2025. Should you fail to accept this proposal, we reserve the right to litigate the matter before the competent court, where we will vigorously argue for a reduction of liability and a stricter assessment of the claimed damages.
