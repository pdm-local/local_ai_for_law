**Subject: Notice of Additional Assessment for Corporate Income Tax Year 2024 and Imposition of a Penalty**

Dear Sir/Madam,

In my capacity as the authorized representative of the Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst), I hereby inform you of the outcome of the audit conducted into your corporate income tax return for the fiscal year ending 31 December 2024.

**1. Factual Background and Assessment Basis**

During the course of the examination, it was established that Saffier Software B.V. (hereinafter: "the Company") failed to report certain taxable profits correctly in the submitted tax return for the assessment year 2024. Specifically, deductible expenses related to software development activities were overstated due to the incorrect classification of capital expenditures as immediate operational costs. This error resulted in a substantial understatement of the taxable profit for the relevant fiscal period.

Pursuant to **Article 16 AWR (State Taxes Act)**, the tax inspector has issued an additional assessment. The correction leads to an increased tax liability amounting to **€ 460.000**. This figure represents the principal tax debt arising from the corrected taxable profit, calculated at the statutory corporate income tax rate applicable for the fiscal year 2024.

**2. Imposition of Penalty**

The Tax Administration has determined that the incorrect filing was not merely an inadvertent error but resulted from a lack of adequate internal controls and verification procedures regarding capitalization rules. Consequently, the behavior is classified as culpable negligence (grove schuld).

In accordance with **Article 67d AWR**, a penalty is imposed on the unpaid tax amount. The applicable penalty rate for this category of non-compliance is set at **50%** of the additional tax assessment.

The calculation of the penalty is as follows:
*   Additional Tax Assessment: € 460.000
*   Penalty Rate: 50%
*   **Total Penalty Amount: € 230.000**

**3. Payment Obligations**

You are hereby ordered to pay the following amounts to the account number of the Dutch Tax Administration, IBAN: NL12ABNA0123456789, BIC: ABNANL2A, in the reference code: ZAAK-NR 2025/SAF-SW-0892.

*   Principal tax debt: € 460.000
*   Penalty: € 230.000
*   **Total payable: € 690.000**

Payment must be received no later than 14 days after the date of this notice, i.e., by 29 April 2025. Failure to pay within this period will result in the accrual of statutory interest and potential enforcement measures.

**4. Right to Object**

If you disagree with this additional assessment or the imposition of the penalty, you may file a formal objection (bezwaarschrift). The objection must be submitted in writing to the Dutch Tax Administration, Postbus 9000, 6401 DA Heerlen, within six weeks after the date of this notice. Please note that filing an objection does not suspend the obligation to pay unless you have provided sufficient security for the disputed amount as required by law.
