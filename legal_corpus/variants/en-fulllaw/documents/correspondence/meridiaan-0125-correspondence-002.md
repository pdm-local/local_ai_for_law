**Subject: Notice of objection against the additional assessment for Corporate Income Tax 2023 and the imposed penalty under Article 67d AWR – Kempen Labs B.V.**

Dear Sir/Madam,

We act on behalf of our client, **Kempen Labs B.V.**, headquartered at Strijp-S 15, 5611 DD Eindhoven, represented by its director, Mr. J. de Vries.

This letter constitutes a formal notice of objection pursuant to Articles 6:7 and 8:1 of the General Administrative Law Act (Awb) against the additional assessment for Corporate Income Tax 2023 issued on 15 January 2025, as well as the penalty imposed under Article 67d of the State Taxes Act (AWR).

**1. Facts and Background**

Kempen Labs B.V. is a technology development firm specializing in secure software architectures. For the fiscal year 2023, the company submitted its tax return on 30 April 2024, reporting a taxable profit of EUR 450,000. The additional assessment was issued following a routine data match by the Dutch Tax Administration, which identified discrepancies regarding the classification of certain R&D expenditures linked to government grants received in Q3 2023.

The Inspectorate has determined that these expenditures were incorrectly deducted and has invoked Article 16 AWR (State Taxes Act), alleging the presence of a "new fact" or circumstance that justifies the reassessment beyond the statutory period for standard adjustments. Furthermore, a penalty equivalent to **50%** of the additional tax due (amounting to EUR 187,500) has been levied on the basis of intent or gross negligence.

**2. Objection Regarding the Application of Article 16 AWR (New Fact)**

We firmly reject the characterization of the situation as involving a "new fact" that was previously unknown and not reasonably discoverable by the Dutch Tax Administration. The expenditures in question were fully documented in our financial statements and supplementary R&D schedules, which were submitted alongside the original tax return. The reclassification of these costs is a matter of legal interpretation regarding the eligibility criteria for innovation subsidies, not a factual omission.

Kempen Labs B.V. acted on the basis of a binding ruling obtained from the Advance Pricing Agreement (APA) desk in November 2023, which confirmed the deductibility of these specific grant-linked costs. The reliance on this official guidance precludes the application of Article 16 AWR, as the facts were known and disclosed at the time of filing. Any discrepancy arises solely from a change in administrative interpretation by your office, which does not constitute a new fact under established case law.

**3. Objection Regarding the Penalty under Article 67d AWR**

Even if the Dutch Tax Administration were to sustain the additional assessment (which we contest), the imposition of a penalty under Article 67d AWR is legally unfounded and disproportionate.

Article 67d AWR requires proof of intent or gross negligence. Kempen Labs B.V. maintains that it acted with due diligence at all times. We engaged an external tax advisor specialized in R&D incentives prior to filing, and the internal accounting team followed established protocols based on the APA ruling. There was no intent to evade taxes, nor was there any reckless disregard for tax obligations.

The penalty rate of **50%** is typically reserved for cases involving deliberate concealment or systematic fraud. In this matter, the company’s conduct was transparent and cooperative. The alleged error, if any, was a good-faith misunderstanding of complex subsidy regulations, which does not meet the threshold of gross negligence required to justify such a severe financial sanction.

**4. Conclusion and Request**

In light of the above, Kempen Labs B.V. requests that:

1.  The additional assessment for Corporate Income Tax 2023 be revoked in its entirety;
2.  Alternatively, if the assessment is upheld, the penalty under Article 67d AWR be reduced to zero or a nominal amount given the absence of intent or gross negligence;
3.  All related interest charges be waived.

We trust that you will review this objection fairly and promptly. We are prepared to provide further documentation, including copies of the APA ruling and correspondence with our tax advisor, should you require it. Please acknowledge receipt of this notice within fourteen days.
