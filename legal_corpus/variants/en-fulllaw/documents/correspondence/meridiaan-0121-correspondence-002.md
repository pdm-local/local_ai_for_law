**Re: Response to Takedown Request dated 10 March 2025 – Matter Ref: ZN-2025-TK-889**

Dear Mr. Van den Berg,

We write on behalf of our client, Aurora Systems B.V. (“Aurora”), in response to your email of 10 March 2025 regarding the takedown of certain content hosted on Zenith Networks’ platform. We have carefully reviewed your allegations and the legal basis provided therein. This letter constitutes Aurora’s formal response to your request.

### 1. Partial Compliance: Removal of Defamatory Comments

Aurora acknowledges that specific user-generated comments identified in Annex A of your request contain statements that are factually incorrect, defamatory, and violate Zenith Networks’ Community Guidelines regarding harassment and false information.

In the interest of maintaining a safe and lawful platform environment, Aurora has instructed its content moderation team to remove the ten (10) specific comments listed in **Annex A** attached hereto. This removal has been executed effective immediately as of 14 March 2025 at 09:00 CET. We confirm that these items are no longer accessible on the platform.

### 2. Dispute Regarding Core Content: No Takedown Warranted

Regarding your demand to remove the main article titled *“The Aurora Data Leak: Corporate Negligence Exposed”* (published on 5 March 2025), Aurora firmly rejects this request. The content in question consists of journalistic commentary and opinion based on publicly available information regarding a regulatory inquiry announced by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens on 1 February 2025.

As an intermediary providing hosting services, Zenith Networks is protected under **Article 6:196c DCC**, which implements the e-Commerce Directive. This provision grants immunity to service providers who host information provided by a recipient of the service, provided they do not have actual knowledge of the illegal activity or are not aware of facts or circumstances from which the illegal activity is apparent. Aurora has not been notified prior to this correspondence of any such illegal activity by authorities.

Furthermore, under **Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act)**, platforms are required to act expeditiously only when notified of specific illegal content that violates Union law. The article in question does not constitute defamation under Dutch law nor does it violate EU-wide prohibitions on illegal content, as it reports on a matter of public interest. Removing this content would constitute an unjustified restriction on freedom of expression and could expose Zenith Networks to liability for disproportionate moderation actions.

### 3. Legal Basis for Refusal

Aurora relies on the following legal grounds to refuse the takedown of the main article:

1.  **Lack of Actual Knowledge**: Under **Article 6:196c DCC**, Zenith Networks cannot be held liable for the hosted content unless it has actual knowledge of its illegality. A private party’s takedown request does not constitute such knowledge.
2.  **Proportionality and Fairness**: Pursuant to **Article 6:248(2) DCC** (reasonableness and fairness), it would be unacceptable for Zenith Networks to remove lawful speech based solely on a commercial dispute between private parties without judicial oversight.
3.  **Freedom of Expression**: Any interference with the publication must meet strict necessity tests. The content is protected opinion and factual reporting, not illegal speech.

### 4. Conclusion and Next Steps

Aurora Systems B.V. has complied with your request to the extent necessary by removing the clearly defamatory comments. We refuse to remove the main article as it is lawful and protected under intermediary liability exemptions and freedom of expression principles.

We expect Zenith Networks B.V. to cease any further attempts to force the removal of this lawful content. Should you wish to pursue this matter further, we advise you to seek a provisional measures order from the competent court under **Article 1019h DCCP** rather than relying on private enforcement mechanisms that exceed your rights as an intermediary.

We trust this clarifies Aurora’s position. We reserve all rights.
