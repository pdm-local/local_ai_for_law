**Re: Intended dismissal for underperformance (Article 7:669 DCC, ground d) regarding Mr. J. de Vries**

Dear Managing Board of Novael Technologies B.V.,

We write to you as the legal counsel representing Nordlicht Networks B.V. regarding the employment contract concluded between our client and Mr. J. de Vries (Employee ID: NL-8842), who has been employed as a Senior Software Engineer at your company since 1 January 2022.

As you are aware, Nordlicht Networks B.V. intends to dissolve the employment contract with Mr. de Vries by way of a court order based on ground (d) of Article 7:669 DCC, namely urgent reasons attributable to the personal circumstances of the employee, specifically persistent underperformance. This decision follows the conclusion of the performance improvement plan (PIP) that was initiated on 15 February 2025 and concluded on 10 May 2025.

Despite the structured support provided during the PIP, including monthly reviews with your HR director, Ms. A. Jansen, and specific training sessions arranged in March 2025, Mr. de Vries has failed to meet the agreed-upon technical milestones. The final evaluation report dated 11 May 2025, which was shared with you on 12 May 2025, clearly demonstrates that his output remains significantly below the standard required for his position.

It is our assessment that no further reasonable efforts to improve his performance can be expected from Nordlicht Networks B.V. given the prolonged duration of the underperformance and the lack of improvement despite adequate support. Therefore, we must proceed with the legal dissolution of the contract.

We expect your company to cooperate in the preparation of the necessary documentation for the court proceedings. Specifically, please provide the signed PIP agreement from February 2025 and the email correspondence regarding the final evaluation by 20 May 2025. Please note that any delay in providing these documents may impact the scheduling of the hearing at the District Court of Eindhoven.

We reserve all rights under Dutch law, including but not limited to Article 6:74 DCC regarding potential damages for delays caused by non-cooperation. The final decision rests with the court, but we are confident that the facts support our position fully.

Should you have any questions regarding this matter, please contact mr. J. Havelaar of Meridiaan Advocaten B.V. directly at +31 40 555 1234 or via email at j.havelaar@meridiaan-law.nl.
