**Re: Cease-and-desist demand regarding renewed infringement of "Mistral"**

Dear Sirs,

We represent Stroomdal Online B.V. (hereinafter: "our client"), pursuant to their instructions and in accordance with the settlement agreement dated 15 January 2024 (hereinafter: "the Settlement Agreement").

It has come to our attention that your company has again engaged in activities constituting a direct infringement of our client’s intellectual property rights. Specifically, you are using the designation "Mistral" and reproducing substantial parts of our client's protected content in connection with your new cloud service offerings, without any license or authorization from Stroomdal Online B.V.

This conduct violates **Article 13 Dutch Copyright Act**, which grants the author exclusive rights to reproduce and distribute their work. Furthermore, your unauthorized use of the sign "Mistral" for identical or similar services infringes upon the trademark protection conferred by **Article 2.20 BCIP**. Our client holds the exclusive right to this designation in Class 9 and Class 42 of the Nice Classification.

We explicitly refer to Clause 4.2 of the Settlement Agreement, in which you acknowledged the previous infringement and agreed to a contractual penalty of € 7.500 per breach for any future violation of these rights. Your current actions constitute a clear and willful repetition of the prohibited behavior, thereby triggering this penalty immediately upon cessation.

**We hereby formally demand that you:**

1. Immediately cease all use of the "Mistral" designation and any confusingly similar signs in your marketing materials, website, and service descriptions;
2. Remove all content infringing on our client’s copyright from your platforms and servers;
3. Confirm in writing within fourteen (14) days that you have complied with these demands.

Failure to comply will leave our client no choice but to initiate legal proceedings without further notice. In such an event, we will claim the contractual penalty of € 7.500 per confirmed incident, along with all legal costs and damages.

We trust that you will give this matter the urgent attention it requires.
