**Legal Basis:** Article 6:162 Dutch Civil Code (DCC)

Dear Mr. van den Berg,

We act as legal counsel to Orion Media B.V. ("our client"). We are writing to you regarding the serious workplace accident that occurred on 15 February 2025 at your premises located at Herengracht 182, Amsterdam, involving our client’s employee, Mr. L. de Vries.

On the aforementioned date, Mr. de Vries was visiting your office to discuss a potential collaboration between Orion Media B.V. and Tinexus Media B.V. While traversing the common hallway of your building, he tripped over an unmarked cable that had been left across the walkway without any warning signs or protective covering. This failure to maintain a safe environment resulted in Mr. de Vries sustaining severe injuries, including a fractured tibia and fibula, as well as significant soft tissue damage requiring extensive medical intervention and rehabilitation.

As your legal counsel, we have reviewed the circumstances surrounding this incident. It is evident that Tinexus Media B.V., as the occupier of the premises, owed a duty of care to all visitors, including our client’s employee, to ensure their safety. By failing to secure the cable or provide adequate warnings, you breached this duty of care. This breach constitutes an unlawful act (onrechtmatige daad) within the meaning of **Article 6:162 DCC**.

Consequently, Tinexus Media B.V. is liable for all damages suffered by our client and its employee as a direct result of this accident. Our client has incurred substantial costs related to medical treatment, rehabilitation, lost wages, and non-material damages due to pain and suffering. After careful assessment of the evidence, including medical reports and financial records, we have calculated the total amount of damages owed to be **€ 310.000**.

We hereby demand that Tinexus Media B.V. pay the sum of **€ 310.000** to our client within fourteen (14) days from the date of this letter. Payments should be made to the following bank account:
