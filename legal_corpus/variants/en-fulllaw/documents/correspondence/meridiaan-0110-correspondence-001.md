**Subject:** Objection to continued pre-trial detention under Article 67 Code of Criminal Procedure; Request for Suspension

Dear Prosecutor,

We act as legal counsel for Novael Cloud B.V. and its director, Mr. J. van der Meer, in the criminal investigation concerning allegations of money laundering under Article 420bis of the Criminal Code.

This letter serves as our formal objection to the continued pre-trial detention of our client, pursuant to **Article 67 Code of Criminal Procedure**. As you are aware, the statutory maximum period for pre-trial detention without a court order or specific judicial extension expired on 14 March 2025. Specifically, the initial period of **14 days** has now elapsed, and no valid judicial decision extending this detention has been communicated to our office in accordance with procedural requirements.

Our client maintains that he is not a flight risk and poses no threat to public order. Novael Cloud B.V. is a registered entity with its registered office at Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven, and Mr. van der Meer has a fixed residence at Keizer Karelweg 45, Den Haag. He has consistently cooperated with the investigation, provided all requested documentation regarding server logs and transaction records, and has not interfered with the course of justice.

Given the expiration of the detention period and the lack of sufficient grounds to justify further deprivation of liberty, we respectfully request that you immediately suspend the pre-trial detention order and release Mr. van der Meer pending further investigation. We propose that he be subjected to alternative measures, such as surrendering his passport, if deemed necessary by your office.

We expect a written response confirming the suspension of the detention no later than 16 March 2025. Should you fail to comply with this request, we will immediately petition the investigating judge to rule on the lawfulness of the continued detention and seek damages for unlawful deprivation of liberty.

Yours faithfully,
