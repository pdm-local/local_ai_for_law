**Re: Response to Notice of Copyright Infringement dated April 10, 2025**

Dear Mr. Van Der Linden,

We refer to your correspondence received on April 10, 2025, in which Helix Online B.V. (“Helix”) alleges that Aurora Media B.V. (“Aurora”) has hosted content infringing upon your copyright in the photographic work titled *“Urban Silence”* (registration number NL-99284-OP).

Aurora acknowledges receipt of this notice and has immediately conducted an internal review of the reported material. We take intellectual property rights seriously and are committed to complying with our obligations as a hosting service provider under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act) and Article 6:196c DCC.

**Assessment of the Claim**

Upon review, we confirm that three specific images hosted on the user profile of user *@streetview_julian* (User ID: 884920) are identical to the work *“Urban Silence”* and were uploaded without authorization from Helix or its assignees. Consequently, we have verified the infringement claim with respect to these three files.

However, we must note that four other images reported in your notice contain only incidental visual similarities to your work but do not constitute a reproduction of the protected expression. These remaining items involve distinct compositional choices and lighting techniques that create a new original work. As such, we consider the infringement claim partially substantiated.

**Action Taken**

In accordance with our duty to act expeditiously upon obtaining actual knowledge of illegal activity, Aurora Media B.V. has taken the following measures effective immediately:

1.  **Removal:** The three infringing images listed in Annex A of your notice have been permanently removed from our platform and deleted from our active servers.
2.  **Access Blocked:** The specific URL paths associated with these images now return a 404 status code, rendering them inaccessible to the public.
3.  **User Notification:** The user responsible for the upload (*@streetview_julian*) has been issued a formal warning regarding repeat offenses and informed of the removal decision.

**Reservation of Rights**

While we have addressed the confirmed infringement, Aurora Media B.V. reserves all rights available to us under Dutch law and international treaties. We reject any allegation that our platform acts as a publisher of such content or bears liability for the initial upload, given our role strictly as a passive intermediary prior to the receipt of your notice.

We trust this resolution meets your requirements. Should you have further questions regarding this matter, please do not hesitate to contact us.
