We act on behalf of Lumen Digital B.V. (hereinafter: "Lumen") regarding the Master Services Agreement dated January 12, 2025 (hereinafter: the "Agreement") entered into between Lumen and Hanze Networks B.V. (hereinafter: "Hanze").

During our routine compliance review of the Agreement, we identified a material inconsistency between Clause 14.2 ("Limitation of Liability") and Clause 17.3 ("Post-Termination Obligations"). Specifically, Clause 14.2 stipulates that Lumen’s aggregate liability for any single incident shall not exceed the fees paid in the **twelve months** preceding the claim. However, Clause 17.3 attempts to limit Hanze’s right to claim damages for service failures after termination to a period of six (6) months.

This divergence creates legal uncertainty regarding which liability cap prevails in the event of a dispute arising from services performed during the final month of the contractual term but discovered post-termination. Under Dutch law, specifically the principle of reasonableness and fairness as codified in **Article 6:248 DCC**, parties are bound not only by the provisions of their agreement but also by what they have given, created, or promised to each other in previous dealings, or by what is required in accordance with prevailing opinion in social relations.

To ensure legal certainty and prevent future disputes, we request that Hanze confirm in writing which liability cap shall govern such scenarios:

1. The **twelve months** period referenced in Clause 14.2 as the standard limitation of Lumen's exposure; or
2. The **six months** period referenced in Clause 17.3 as a specific post-termination restriction.
