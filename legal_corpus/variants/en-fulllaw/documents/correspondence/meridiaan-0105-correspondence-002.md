**Re: Matter 2025.0105 – Breach of Data Processing Agreement; Unauthorized Sub-processor Engagement**

Dear Representatives of Polderzicht Solutions B.V.,

We act for Maaslicht Retail B.V. ("our Client") in connection with the ongoing data processing activities governed by the Data Processing Agreement dated 15 January 2024 (the "DPA").

It has come to our Client’s attention that on 3 April 2025, Polderzicht Solutions B.V. engaged a new sub-processor for the purpose of cloud infrastructure hosting and data storage services. Specifically, this engagement involved **Amazon Web Services EMEA (Ierland)**.

Our Client notes with concern that Amazon Web Services EMEA (Ierland) does not appear in the approved list of sub-processors as set out in Annex A to the DPA. The only previously authorized cloud infrastructure providers were Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited and Oracle Corporation Netherlands B.V.

Under **Article 28 GDPR**, a data controller may not engage another processor without prior specific or general written authorization of the controller. Where the controller has given general authorization for the engagement of sub-processors, the processor shall inform the controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of other processors by giving at least thirty days’ notice of such changes, thereby affording the controller the opportunity to object to such changes.

Polderzicht Solutions B.V. failed to provide the requisite thirty-day notice and did not obtain our Client’s prior written consent for this specific sub-processor. Consequently, the engagement of Amazon Web Services EMEA (Ierland) constitutes a direct violation of Article 28 GDPR and the contractual terms agreed upon in the DPA.

**Demands**

In light of the above, we demand that Polderzicht Solutions B.V.:

1. Immediately suspend any processing activities involving the personal data of our Client’s customers on Amazon Web Services EMEA (Ierland);
2. Provide written confirmation within five business days that all such data has been migrated back to an approved sub-processor as listed in Annex A;
3. Confirm that no further unauthorized sub-processors will be engaged without strict adherence to the notification and consent mechanisms required by Article 28 GDPR.

Failure to comply with these demands will leave our Client with no alternative but to consider further legal action, including a complaint to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) and a claim for damages under Article 82 GDPR.

We await your urgent response.
