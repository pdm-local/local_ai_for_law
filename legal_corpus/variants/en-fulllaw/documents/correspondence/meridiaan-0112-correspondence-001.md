Subject: **Formal Notice of Dissolution of Lease Agreement and Eviction – Unit 10B, Keizer Karelstraat 50, Eindhoven**

Dear Sir or Madam,

We act on behalf of our client, **Brightlane Technologies B.V.** (“Landlord”), regarding the commercial lease agreement for the business premises located at Keizer Karelstraat 50, Unit 10B, 5611 CL Eindhoven (“the Premises”), currently occupied by your company, **Polderzicht Digital B.V.** (“Tenant”).

It has come to our client’s attention that the Tenant is in significant arrears of the monthly rent. Despite previous informal reminders sent via email on 10 February 2025 and 10 March 2025, no payment has been received. The Tenant has failed to pay rent for a period of **5 months**, specifically covering the periods from November 2024 through March 2025.

The total amount of outstanding rent, excluding contractual interest and administrative costs, amounts to **€ 3.100 per month**. Consequently, the principal debt stands at € 15.500. In addition to this principal sum, the Tenant is liable for statutory interest accrued on these overdue amounts in accordance with Article 6:82 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC), as well as any administrative costs incurred by the Landlord due to the default.

Given that the arrears exceed three months’ rent and that the Tenant has failed to remedy this breach after being urged to do so, our client considers it necessary to terminate the lease agreement immediately. Pursuant to **Article 7:274 DCC**, the Landlord is entitled to dissolve the lease if the tenant fails to perform its obligations, particularly the payment of rent, and such failure constitutes a ground for termination as recognized by law or contract.

**Therefore, we hereby formally notify you of the following:**

1.  **Dissolution of Lease**: The lease agreement for the Premises is hereby dissolved with immediate effect due to material breach of contract.
2.  **Eviction Notice**: You are required to vacate the Premises and remove all personal property, equipment, and belongings no later than **30 April 2025**.
3.  **Payment Demand**: You are ordered to pay the outstanding rent arrears of **€ 15.500** (representing **5 months** at € **3.100** per month) plus applicable interest and costs within fourteen (14) days of receipt of this letter, i.e., by **29 April 2025**.

Payments must be made via bank transfer to IBAN NL80INGB0001234567, BIC INGBNL2A, in the name of Brightlane Technologies B.V., under reference `2025.0112-RENT`.

Should you fail to comply with this demand for payment and vacate the Premises by the stipulated deadline, our client will instruct us to initiate legal proceedings without further notice. This will include a request for an urgent preliminary relief proceedings (kort geding) or direct enforcement action to recover the debt and secure eviction, at which point you will also be liable for all legal costs, including those incurred by Meridiaan Advocaten B.V., as governed by Article 1019h of the Dutch Code of Civil Procedure (DCCP).

We hope to resolve this matter amicably and without further litigation. However, please be advised that our client is prepared to take all necessary legal steps to protect its interests.
