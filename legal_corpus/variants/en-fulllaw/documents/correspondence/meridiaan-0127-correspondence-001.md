**Re: Inquiry Petition filed before the Enterprise Chamber of the Court of Appeal in Amsterdam**
**Matter:** Duinrand Networks B.V. versus Cobalt Labs B.V.
**Date:** 10 April 2025

Dear Sirs,

We act on behalf of our client, **Duinrand Networks B.V.**, with its registered office at Herstraat 44, 5611 ER Eindhoven (hereinafter: "Duinrand"). We write to you concerning the corporate governance and financial conduct of **Cobalt Labs B.V.**, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam (hereinafter: "Cobalt Labs" or the "Company").

It has come to our attention that on 1 March 2025, the General Meeting of Shareholders of Cobalt Labs approved a dividend distribution amounting to **€ 480.000**. This distribution was allocated primarily to the majority shareholders who also serve as directors of the Company. Duinrand Networks B.V., holding a minority stake of 15%, has raised serious concerns regarding the propriety of this decision, alleging that it violates the standard of care expected from directors and undermines the legitimate interests of minority shareholders.

Consequently, Duinrand Networks B.V. has instituted proceedings by way of petition (*verzoekschrift*) before the Enterprise Chamber of the Court of Appeal in Amsterdam (the "Enterprise Chamber"). The petition seeks an investigation into the policy and affairs of the Company, specifically focusing on the decision-making process surrounding the aforementioned dividend distribution.

You are hereby informed that the filing of this petition is based on **Article 2:345 DCC** (Book 2 of the Dutch Civil Code). Under this provision, shareholders holding at least one-tenth of the issued share capital, or shares with a par value of at least € 100.000 in the aggregate, have the right to request an inquiry into the policy and ordinary affairs of a public limited liability company (NV) if there are facts of such a nature that justifies fears that the interests of the Company or its connected enterprises have been seriously harmed.

Given the magnitude of the sum involved—€ 480.000—and the apparent concentration of benefits among the controlling directors without adequate justification or reserve building, Duinrand contends that the requirement for "serious harm" to corporate interests is met. The Enterprise Chamber will be asked to determine whether the decision was taken in accordance with the standards of due care and whether it serves the joint interest of the Company and its stakeholders.

We expect you to preserve all relevant documents related to the board meetings, financial projections, and shareholder resolutions concerning this dividend distribution from 1 January 2024 onwards. Any attempt to alter or destroy such records may have serious legal consequences.

We shall keep you informed of further developments in these proceedings. We reserve all rights.
