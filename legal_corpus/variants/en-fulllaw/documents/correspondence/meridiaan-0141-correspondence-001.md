**Re: Matter 2025.0141 – Verdana Retail B.V. v. Novael Digital B.V.**

Dear Sirs,

We act as legal counsel to Verdana Retail B.V. (hereinafter: "Verdana"), headquartered in Rotterdam at Wilhelminakade 95, 3072 AP Rotterdam. We write to you regarding the corporate governance conduct of Novael Digital B.V. (hereinafter: "Novael") and specifically concerning the extraordinary dividend distribution approved by your shareholders' meeting on February 10, 2025.

It has come to our client's attention that Novael declared a dividend payment amounting to **€ 750.000** to its majority shareholder, Digital Holdings Ltd., despite clear indicators of insufficient distributable reserves and conflicting fiduciary duties owed by the board members under Article 2:9 DCC. This distribution was executed on March 1, 2025, without adequate justification in the annual accounts or prior independent audit reports, thereby potentially violating the distribution test under Article 2:216 DCC and harming the interests of minority stakeholders and creditors alike.

Our client has exhausted all internal avenues for resolution. The request for an extraordinary shareholders' meeting to review this decision was rejected by Novael’s board on March 5, 2025. Consequently, Verdana Retail B.V. intends to file a petition for inquiry proceedings with the Enterprise Chamber of the Amsterdam Court of Appeal pursuant to **Article 2:345 DCC**.

The petition will seek:
1. An investigation into the manner in which Novael Digital B.V. has conducted its affairs, particularly regarding the financial management leading up to and following the dividend distribution;
2. Temporary measures to freeze further asset transfers pending the outcome of the inquiry;
3. Potential removal or suspension of board members involved in authorizing the contested payment;
4. An order for Novael Digital B.V. to restore the **€ 750.000** to its balance sheet or provide full restitution to affected parties.

We trust that this notice will prompt you to reconsider your position and engage in serious negotiations to avoid litigation. However, should no satisfactory solution be reached within fourteen (14) days of receipt of this letter, we are instructed to proceed with the filing of the petition without further notice.

Please direct all future correspondence regarding this matter to our office.
