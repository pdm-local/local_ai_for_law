Dear Representatives of Meridiaan Technologies B.V.,

We refer to our recent correspondence regarding the calculation of child maintenance obligations for the clients involved in the above-mentioned matter, specifically concerning Zenith Cloud B.V. and Meridiaan Technologies B.V.

Following a detailed review of the reported financial circumstances and current income declarations, we have applied the standard Trema norms to determine the appropriate maintenance amount. The analysis confirms that the monthly figure of **390** euro is fully justified and compliant with applicable legal standards.

This determination is based on Article 1:404 DCC, which governs the obligation of parents to contribute to the cost of maintaining their children in proportion to their respective financial capacities. Given the reported incomes of both parties, the calculated amount of € 390 per month accurately reflects the statutory requirements and ensures equitable distribution of costs relative to each party's earning power.

We therefore formally support this amount as the correct and legally sound figure for this case. Should you require further documentation regarding the income verification or the specific application of the Trema tables, please do not hesitate to contact our office.
