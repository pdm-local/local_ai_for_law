**Subject: Intent to reject asylum application (Article 29 Aliens Act 2000) and notice of period for comment regarding Article 8 Aliens Act 2000**

Dear Mr. Yildiz,

This letter serves as official notification from the Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) concerning the asylum application filed by Mr. Aris Thorne on behalf of Brightlane Software B.V.’s subsidiary interests in the Netherlands. After a thorough examination of the submitted dossier and the current legal framework governing foreign nationals, the IND has reached the following preliminary conclusions.

**1. Assessment under Article 29 of the Aliens Act 2000**

The IND has determined that Mr. Thorne does not meet the criteria for refugee status as defined in the Dutch implementation of the Geneva Convention, nor does he qualify for subsidiary protection. The evidence provided regarding the risk of persecution or serious harm in his country of origin is deemed insufficient to establish a well-founded fear of being persecuted. Consequently, the IND intends to reject the asylum application pursuant to **Article 29 Aliens Act 2000**.

**2. Assessment of Lawful Residence under Article 8 of the Aliens Act 2000**

Furthermore, the IND notes that Mr. Thorne does not hold a valid residence permit for any other purpose. The application for a residence permit as a highly skilled migrant was rejected due to a failure to meet the income requirements set by the employer. As such, he currently lacks a lawful basis for residence in the Netherlands under **Article 8 Aliens Act 2000**.

The IND considers that his continued stay is not justified on humanitarian grounds and does not serve the public interest. Given that the rejection of the asylum application will result in the revocation of any existing temporary stay permits, the IND intends to issue a declaration of undesirability for entry and residence (VVR) once the final decision on the asylum application is rendered.

**3. Opportunity to Submit Views**

In accordance with the principles of good administration and procedural fairness, you are hereby invited to submit your views on this intent to reject. Specifically, we request that you address whether there are any compelling humanitarian reasons or factual errors in our assessment that warrant a different outcome.

You have **6 weeks** from the date of receipt of this letter to submit your written response. All correspondence regarding this matter must be sent electronically to `intent-comments@ind.nl` with reference number IND-2025-INT-8842-BL or by post to the address listed above.

If no response is received within this period, or if the arguments submitted are insufficient to alter our position, the IND will proceed with issuing the final rejection decision and the accompanying declaration of undesirability.
