Dear Mr. van der Meer,

We refer to our previous correspondence regarding the family law dispute between Novael Systems B.V. (acting on behalf of its director, Ms. E. Visser) and Hanze Interactive B.V. concerning child maintenance for the minor child L. Visser, born on 14 May 2018.

Following a thorough review of the provided financial documentation, including the audited annual reports for the fiscal years 2023 and 2024, and the current payroll administration as of March 2025, we have calculated the appropriate maintenance obligation. The calculation strictly adheres to the guidelines established by the Dutch Child Maintenance Standards (Trema standards), which serve as the primary reference point for determining fair and reasonable child support in the Netherlands.

Based on the net disposable income of Hanze Interactive B.V. as attributed to Mr. J. van der Meer and the standard deduction for personal expenses, the resulting maintenance amount is fixed at **€ 485** per month. This figure aligns precisely with the statutory framework governing child support obligations under **Article 1:404 DCC**, which mandates that parents are obligated to provide for the reasonable needs of their children in proportion to their respective financial capacities.

Novael Systems B.V. has formally accepted this amount as sufficient to cover the educational, care, and general living expenses of the minor child. We consider this matter resolved regarding the quantum of maintenance. Please ensure that the payment of € 485 is executed monthly via bank transfer to account number NL91 ABNA 0417 1643 20 in the name of Novael Systems B.V., with the payment reference "Child Maintenance L. Visser".

Should any significant change in your financial circumstances occur that would warrant a revision of this amount, please notify us in writing immediately so that we may reassess the obligation in light of the current legal standards.

We trust this correspondence clarifies the position and brings closure to this specific aspect of the matter.
