**Re: Notification of Personal Data Breach – Incident ID TNX-2025-04-18**

Dear Mr. van der Meer,

In accordance with our obligations under the processing agreement dated 15 January 2024 and in compliance with **Article 33 GDPR**, we hereby notify Maaslicht Software B.V. of a personal data breach involving backup systems managed by Tinexus Networks B.V. as the processor.

**1. Nature and Discovery of the Breach**
On 16 April 2025, at approximately 14:30 CET, our Security Operations Center detected anomalous network traffic originating from an internal administrative subnet. Upon immediate investigation, it was confirmed that an unauthorized actor had gained access to a specific backup repository containing data related to Maaslicht Software B.V.’s client management system. The intruder did not encrypt the data but downloaded a subset of configuration logs and metadata files.

**2. Scope of the Breach**
The breach affects data processed on behalf of Maaslicht Software B.V. between 10 April 2025 and 16 April 2025. The affected data categories include:
*   Technical identifiers (IP addresses, server logs);
*   Application configuration parameters;
*   Non-personal operational metadata.

No names, email addresses, or financial data of end-users were contained within the specific backup files accessed in this incident. However, the exposed technical metadata could potentially be used to infer client identities if combined with other datasets. The risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons is currently assessed as low, but we treat this matter with utmost seriousness.

**3. Timeline and Reporting Compliance**
Tinexus Networks B.V. became aware of the breach on 16 April 2025 at 14:30 CET. We have contained the vulnerability and secured the affected systems. This notification is being transmitted to you **36 hours** after our initial detection, well within the 72-hour deadline prescribed by Article 33(1) GDPR.

**4. Measures Taken and Mitigation**
To address this incident, Tinexus Networks B.V. has taken the following immediate measures:
*   The unauthorized access point has been identified and closed;
*   All administrative credentials for the affected backup repository have been rotated;
*   Forensic analysis is underway to determine if any lateral movement occurred within your environment;
*   We are enhancing monitoring protocols for similar backup repositories across all client environments.

**5. Further Information**
We will provide a follow-up report once the forensic investigation is complete, expected no later than 25 April 2025. If we determine that this breach poses a high risk to individuals, we will notify the competent supervisory authority and, where required, inform affected data subjects in accordance with Articles 34 GDPR.

We regret any inconvenience this may cause and remain at your disposal for any further clarification required under our processing agreement.
