**1. Purpose of the Meeting**

The purpose of this intake was to discuss recent findings by Nordlicht Retail B.V. ("Nordlicht") regarding a closely similar product design marketed and sold by Verdana Digital B.V. ("Verdana"). Specifically, we addressed the potential infringement of copyright under the Dutch Copyright Act and reviewed the implications of a previous settlement agreement concluded between the parties in 2023, which included a contractual penalty clause.

**2. Facts Presented by Client**

Ms. van der Meer reported that during a routine market surveillance exercise in March 2025, Nordlicht identified a new product line launched by Verdana Digital B.V. on their e-commerce platform. Upon comparative analysis, it was established that the visual appearance of Verdana’s "EcoGarden" series mirrors the distinctive graphical user interface and ornamental design of Nordlicht’s protected "GreenLife" collection.

Key points raised:
*   The similarity extends to specific color palettes, layout structures, and decorative elements that are not generic to the industry but constitute original intellectual property of Nordlicht.
*   Verdana Digital B.V. has been actively promoting these products via targeted digital advertising since 15 February 2025.
*   Nordlicht wishes to enforce its rights while leveraging historical precedents between the parties to strengthen their negotiating position.

**3. Reference to Prior Settlement (2023)**

Ms. van der Meer explicitly referred to the settlement agreement dated 10 June 2023, which resolved a previous dispute regarding unauthorized use of Nordlicht’s branding assets. A critical component of that 2023 agreement was a penalty clause triggered by any future non-compliance with cease-and-desist obligations.

The client emphasized that the prior penalty amount was fixed at **€ 5.000** per confirmed breach. While this figure may appear modest relative to current market damages, Nordlicht intends to invoke this clause as a formal notice of default and a baseline for immediate injunctive relief, arguing that Verdana’s new actions constitute a direct repetition of the conduct addressed in the prior settlement.

**4. Legal Assessment by Meridiaan Advocaten**

Mr. Reinders provided the following initial legal assessment:

*   **Copyright Infringement:** Under Article 1 and Article 13 of the Dutch Copyright Act, the graphical elements in question may qualify as protected works if they display a personal intellectual creation. The high degree of similarity observed suggests that Verdana has likely exceeded the threshold of independent creation.
*   **Enforceability of Penalty Clause:** We must carefully review the exact wording of the 2023 settlement to determine if the new product line falls within the scope of the original definition of "breach." If the prior agreement broadly prohibited all unauthorized use of Nordlicht’s intellectual property, the € 5.000 penalty could serve as a liquidated damages claim or a contractual lever for negotiation.
*   **Strategic Options:**
    1.  Issue a formal letter of default citing the 2023 settlement and demanding immediate cessation of sales, invoking the penalty clause.
    2.  Prepare for interim proceedings under Article 1019h DCCP to seek an urgent injunction against Verdana Digital B.V.
    3.  Evaluate whether the € 5.000 penalty is sufficient or if we should pursue actual damages under general tort principles, though this requires more extensive evidence of financial loss.

**5. Next Steps**

1.  Mr. Reinders will obtain and review the full text of the 2023 settlement agreement to verify the scope of the penalty clause and the definition of breach.
2.  Nordlicht Retail B.V. will provide high-resolution screenshots and timestamped links documenting Verdana’s current sales activities.
3.  A follow-up memo will be drafted within five business days, outlining a proposed cease-and-desist letter that references the prior € 5.000 penalty as a deterrent for compliance.
