**Attendance Note**

**Matter:** 2025.0143 – *Duinrand Data B.V. vs Duinrand Retail B.V.*
**Date:** 12 March 2025
**Attorney:** mr. D. van Raay
**Client:** Mr. J. de Vries, Director of Duinrand Data B.V.

**1. Context and Facts**

During our meeting at the Meridiaan Advocaten office in Eindhoven today, you raised concerns regarding the financial stability of your group structure following the recent insolvency filing of Duinrand Retail B.V. on 10 March 2025. Specifically, you noted a significant inter-company transfer from Duinrand Data B.V. to Duinrand Retail B.V. in late January 2025.

Upon reviewing the bank statements provided, I confirm that a payment was executed on 28 January 2025 via ABN AMRO (account NL42ABNA0412345678) from Duinrand Data B.V. to Duinrand Retail B.V. for the exact amount of **180.000** euros. This transaction occurred approximately six weeks before the declaration of bankruptcy for Duinrand Retail B.V. by the District Court of Amsterdam (KvK registration 72345678).

**2. Legal Analysis: Avoidance of Fraudulent Preference**

As the appointed curator for Duinrand Retail B.V., my primary duty is to maximize the estate's assets. A critical aspect of this duty is investigating transactions that may constitute fraudulent preferences (*wanbegunstiging*). Under Dutch insolvency law, transactions performed by the debtor before bankruptcy can be avoided if they were made while the debtor was insolvent or became insolvent as a result, and without adequate consideration or in violation of legal restrictions.

The key issue here is whether Duinrand Retail B.V. was already insolvent on 28 January 2025 when it received the **180.000** euros. Insolvency is defined by Article 1 of the Bankruptcy Act as the inability to pay due debts. If we can demonstrate that Duinrand Retail B.V. was over-indebted or had ceased payments in late January, this payment could be challenged.

The relevant statutory basis for avoidance is found in Article 42 of the Bankruptcy Act (*Faillissementswet*). This article allows the curator to avoid legal acts performed by the bankrupt party that result in a reduction of the estate, provided certain conditions are met regarding the timing and the counterparty's knowledge (or constructive knowledge) of the insolvency.

Furthermore, we must consider Article 40 of the Bankruptcy Act, which addresses the termination or continuation of contracts. If the payment was linked to an ongoing service agreement between Duinrand Data B.V. and Duinrand Retail B.V., we must analyze whether this represented a preferential settlement of an existing debt or a new advance payment. Given that Duinrand Data B.V. is an affiliate, there is a heightened risk that this transaction may be viewed as an attempt to shift assets away from the failing retail entity toward the more stable technology subsidiary prior to the insolvency date.

**3. Strategic Recommendation**

To proceed with avoidance proceedings, we need to establish the following:
1.  **Insolvency Date:** Evidence that Duinrand Retail B.V. was unable to pay its due debts on or before 28 January 2025. This may include unpaid invoices to third-party suppliers and bounced checks during that period.
2.  **Lack of Adequate Consideration:** We must determine if the **180.000** euros was for goods or services actually delivered simultaneously. If it was an advance payment for future performance, and no significant value was exchanged at that time, the risk of avoidance is higher.
3.  **Counterparty's Knowledge:** As an affiliated entity, Duinrand Data B.V. may be presumed to have known of the financial difficulties of Duinrand Retail B.V., which strengthens our position under Article 42 Bankruptcy Act.

**4. Next Steps**

I recommend we immediately request the full accounting records of Duinrand Retail B.V. for the period from 1 December 2024 to 31 January 2025. We will conduct a forensic review to pinpoint the exact date of insolvency. If the payment of **180.000** euros falls within the "suspect period" (typically the three months preceding the bankruptcy declaration) and occurs after the onset of insolvency, we will file a petition for avoidance with the District Court of Amsterdam.
