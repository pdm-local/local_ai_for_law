**1. Introduction and Facts**

Ms. van der Linden attended this intake to discuss the corporate governance situation at Novael Digital B.V., a technology platform in which Verdana Retail B.V. holds shares. Specifically, Ms. van der Linden confirmed that Verdana Retail B.V. is a minority shareholder with a holding of exactly **26%** of the issued shares and voting rights in Novael Digital B.V.

The core issue concerns a dividend resolution adopted by the General Meeting of Shareholders of Novael Digital B.V. on 10 March 2025. The Board of Directors, dominated by the majority shareholder (TechHoldings International B.V.), declared a total dividend payout amounting to **€ 750.000**. Ms. van der Linden reports that this distribution was executed without prior consultation with minority shareholders and appears to have drained significant liquidity from Novael Digital B.V. despite pending operational challenges.

Ms. van der Linden states that the majority shareholder’s conduct is arbitrary, as the dividend was paid while the company’s financial statements indicated a precarious cash flow position for Q1 2025. She seeks to challenge this distribution and protect the interests of Verdana Retail B.V.

**2. Legal Assessment**

I have advised Ms. van der Linden on the available remedies under Dutch corporate law, specifically focusing on the Civil Code (DCC).

**A. Dividend Distribution and Article 2:216 DCC**
The declaration of dividends is governed by **Article 2:216 DCC**. A distribution may only be made from the profit reserve or, if no such reserve exists, from assets that have been free for distribution according to the annual accounts. Given the reported liquidity issues, I noted that the Board may have violated its duty of care under **Article 2:9 DCC** by approving a distribution that threatens the company’s ability to meet its due debts. However, individual shareholders generally lack direct standing to annul a dividend resolution unless they can prove immediate and irreparable damage, which is difficult to establish solely on the basis of reduced future dividends.

**B. Inquiry Proceedings (Article 2:345 DCC)**
The most viable strategic avenue discussed was the initiation of inquiry proceedings (*enquête*) under **Article 2:345 DCC**. This procedure allows a shareholder holding at least 1% of the issued capital or shares with a nominal value of at least **€ 100.000** to request an investigation by the Enterprise Chamber of the Court of Appeal of Amsterdam.

Given that Verdana Retail B.V. holds **26%** of Novael Digital B.V., it clearly meets the threshold requirements. Ms. van der Linden expressed interest in this route because it allows for a broad investigation into the affairs of the company and its management, not just the specific dividend resolution. Grounds for such an inquiry include mismanagement (*wanbeleid*) or conduct that constitutes a serious breach of duties toward the company or other shareholders. The arbitrary nature of the dividend distribution, combined with alleged lack of transparency in board meetings, may constitute such grounds.

**C. Alternative Measures**
I also briefly mentioned the possibility of a claim for damages under **Article 2:9 DCC** against the directors personally if it can be proven that they acted grossly negligently or intentionally. However, this is subsidiary to the inquiry proceedings and requires a different burden of proof.

**3. Advice and Next Steps**

I advised Ms. van der Linden that initiating inquiry proceedings under **Article 2:345 DCC** is the most effective mechanism to scrutinize the management’s actions regarding the **€ 750.000** distribution and the overall governance of Novael Digital B.V. The minority status (holding only **26%**) necessitates this formal judicial route rather than informal negotiation, as the majority shareholder controls the General Meeting.

**Action Items:**
1. Meridiaan Advocaten will draft a formal request for inquiry proceedings to be filed with the Enterprise Chamber of the Court of Appeal of Amsterdam within two weeks.
2. We will prepare the petition outlining the specific grievances regarding the dividend distribution and alleged mismanagement.
3. Ms. van der Linden is to provide all relevant board minutes and financial statements from Novael Digital B.V. dated between 1 January 2024 and 12 March 2025 for review.

**4. Conclusion**

The intake concluded with Ms. van der Linden acknowledging the seriousness of the situation and instructing Meridiaan Advocaten to proceed with the preparation of the inquiry petition. The firm will assess the costs involved, noting that inquiry proceedings can be expensive, but emphasized the strategic value in protecting her client’s investment.
