**1. Introduction and Mandate**
This note records the intake meeting held between meritorian advocate mr. P. Slootweg and Mr. J. de Vries, representing Hanze Data B.V. ("Hanze"), concerning an administrative penalty order issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven ("the Municipality"). Mr. de Vries has appointed Meridiaan Advocaten to represent Hanze in this matter. We confirm that we act solely on behalf of Hanze Data B.V.

**2. Facts of the Case**
On 10 January 2025, Hanze received a formal penalty order (lasterafschrift) from the Municipality of Eindhoven. The penalty is imposed for alleged violations of local municipal regulations regarding data processing activities conducted by Hanze at its premises located at Strijp-S, Eindhoven.

The key facts established during this intake are as follows:
*   **Penalty Amount:** The total fine levied by the Municipality amounts to € 25.000.
*   **Grounds for Penalty:** The Municipality alleges that Hanze failed to provide required information regarding the nature of data transfers in response to a preliminary administrative inquiry dated 15 December 2024.
*   **Date of Receipt:** The penalty order was physically received by Hanze’s registered office on 10 January 2025.

**3. Legal Procedure and Deadlines**
We have reviewed the procedural requirements for filing an objection against administrative decisions in the Netherlands. The relevant statutory period for lodging a formal objection (bezwaar) against a penalty order is strictly regulated.

Mr. de Vries was explicitly informed that Hanze has exactly **4 weeks** from the date of receipt of the penalty order to file a written objection with the Municipality of Eindhoven.

*   **Date of Receipt:** 10 January 2025
*   **Deadline for Objection:** 7 February 2025

It is critical that any objection is received by the Municipality on or before 7 February 2025. Late objections will be declared inadmissible unless there are exceptional circumstances preventing timely filing, which are unlikely to apply in this instance given the clarity of the deadline.

**4. Preliminary Legal Assessment**
While we have not yet conducted a full substantive review of the penalty order’s legal basis under the General Administrative Law Act (Awb), the initial assessment suggests that the procedural grounds for objection may focus on:
1.  Whether the preliminary inquiry provided sufficient clarity on the information requested.
2.  Whether the proportionality principle was respected in imposing a fine of € 25.000 without prior warning or opportunity to cure the defect.

**5. Next Steps**
1.  **Documentation Collection:** Mr. de Vries will provide all correspondence between Hanze and the Municipality from 1 November 2024 onwards, including emails, letters, and internal notes regarding data processing compliance.
2.  **Drafting Objection:** Meridiaan Advocaten will draft a formal statement of objection (bezwaarschrift). This draft will be shared with Mr. de Vries for review by 31 January 2025 to allow sufficient time for submission before the 7 February 2025 deadline.
3.  **Further Advice:** If the objection is rejected, we will discuss the possibility of filing an appeal (beroep) at the District Court of Eindhoven.

**6. Confirmation**
Mr. de Vries confirms that the facts stated above are correct and authorizes Meridiaan Advocaten to proceed with drafting the objection within the stipulated timeframe.
