**Subject:** Intake and assessment of tortious liability claim against Tinexus Media B.V.

**1. Introduction and Mandate**

The client, Orion Media B.V. (hereinafter: "Orion"), engaged Meridiaan Advocaten to represent them in a matter concerning financial losses incurred due to the conduct of their former business partner, Tinexus Media B.V. (hereinafter: "Tinexus"). The objective of the intake meeting was to establish the factual background, assess the legal basis for a claim under Dutch tort law, and determine the viability of pursuing damages.

**2. Factual Background**

Ms. van der Meer explained that Orion and Tinexus entered into a strategic cooperation agreement on 15 January 2024, aimed at jointly developing digital advertising infrastructure. Under this arrangement, Orion transferred certain proprietary data processing algorithms and client lists to Tinexus for exclusive use during the project term.

According to the instructions received, Tinexus breached its contractual and pre-contractual duties of care in late 2024. Specifically, Tinexus is alleged to have unauthorizedly shared Orion’s confidential technical specifications with a third-party competitor, thereby violating the duty of confidentiality and good faith inherent in their cooperation. This disclosure allegedly allowed the competitor to replicate Orion’s core technology, leading to a significant loss of market share and direct financial damage for Orion.

**3. Quantification of Damages**

Mr. de Vries presented the financial impact assessment prepared by Orion’s internal audit team. The direct costs associated with this incident include:
*   Loss of projected revenue from lost contracts: € 210.000;
*   Legal and forensic IT investigation costs: € 40.000;
*   Reputational damage mitigation expenses: € 60.000.

The total claimed amount is € 310.000. The client insists on recovering this full sum from Tinexus as compensation for the harm suffered. Ms. van der Meer confirmed that no insurance coverage applies to this specific type of commercial dispute, making legal recovery the primary avenue for restitution.

**4. Legal Assessment**

Mr. Nolte advised Orion that the claim will primarily be founded on Article 6:162 DCC (tort law), requiring proof of an unlawful act, attributability, damage, and a causal link between the conduct and the loss. Given the pre-contractual nature of the relationship, the breach of the duty of care under Article 6:248(2) DCC (reasonableness and fairness) is also relevant.

Mr. Nolte noted that establishing causation will be critical. Tinexus is likely to argue that market fluctuations, rather than the disclosure of data, caused the revenue loss. To strengthen the case, Orion must provide concrete evidence linking the competitor’s product launch directly to Tinexus’s actions. The forensic report mentioned by Mr. de Vries will be pivotal in this regard.

**5. Next Steps**

It was agreed that Meridiaan Advocaten will:
1.  Draft a formal letter of demand to Tinexus Media B.V., citing their liability under Article 6:162 DCC and demanding payment of € 310.000 within fourteen days;
2.  Preserve all relevant digital evidence, including email chains and server logs, in accordance with data retention guidelines;
3.  Await Tinexus’s response before initiating civil proceedings if the demand is not met.

The matter is marked as active. A follow-up consultation is scheduled for 26 March 2025 to review Tinexus’s reply.
