**ATTENDANCE NOTE**

**Matter:** 2025.0124 – Saffier Logistics B.V.
**Date:** 15 March 2025
**Time:** 14:30 – 15:45
**Location:** Meridiaan Advocaten B.V., Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven (Client’s premises via secure video link)
**Attendees:** mr. R. Bahadoer (Attorney), Mr. J. de Vries (Managing Director, Saffier Logistics B.V.)

**1. Introduction and Purpose**
I attended a meeting with Mr. J. de Vries, Managing Director of Saffier Logistics B.V., to discuss the ongoing criminal investigation into money laundering activities. The purpose of this meeting was to review the client's position following his recent deprivation of liberty and to assess the evidentiary basis for the funds in question.

**2. Facts Regarding Detention**
Mr. de Vries confirmed that he was held in pre-trial detention at the Detention Centre in Den Bosch for **14 days**. The detention was ordered by the investigating judge based on reasonable suspicion of involvement in financial crimes linked to the company's logistics operations. He states that his release on bail occurred on 30 March 2025, subject to strict reporting obligations and travel restrictions.

**3. Denial of Money Laundering Charges**
Mr. de Vries firmly denies any knowledge of or participation in money laundering activities as defined under Article 420bis of the Criminal Code. He asserts that Saffier Logistics B.V. operates with full transparency and adheres strictly to all anti-money laundering (AML) regulations applicable to freight forwarding and logistics services in the Netherlands.

**4. Explanation of Fund Origin (€ 48.000)**
The investigation focuses on a specific transaction involving the sum of **€ 48.000**. Mr. de Vries provided the following account regarding the origin of these funds:

*   **Nature of Transaction:** The amount represents a legitimate advance payment from a client, *Nordic Freight Solutions ApS*, for consolidated logistics services rendered between January and February 2025.
*   **Contractual Basis:** He presented the signed service agreement (Ref: NFS-SL-2025-042) which explicitly stipulates this advance payment to cover fuel surcharges and customs clearance fees in Rotterdam.
*   **Payment Trail:** The funds were transferred via SWIFT from the client’s corporate account in Copenhagen directly to Saffier Logistics B.V.’s business account at ING Bank, Eindhoven branch, on 10 February 2025. He provided the bank statement and the corresponding invoice (Invoice No. SL-8921) for review.
*   **Lack of Illicit Intent:** Mr. de Vries emphasized that there was no attempt to obscure the source of funds or layer them through multiple jurisdictions. The transaction is fully documented and traceable within the company’s accounting system.

**5. Assessment and Next Steps**
Based on the documentation provided (service agreement, invoice, and bank transfer confirmation), the explanation for the **€ 48.000** appears consistent with standard commercial practices in the logistics sector. The denial of money laundering intent is supported by the transparent nature of the transaction records.

I have advised Mr. de Vries to:
1. Preserve all electronic communications related to *Nordic Freight Solutions ApS*.
2. Prepare a formal written statement reiterating these facts for submission to the Public Prosecution Service.
3. Refrain from any further contact with the client until cleared by legal counsel.

We will monitor the case file closely and prepare a defense strategy focusing on the absence of *mens rea* (criminal intent) under Article 420bis of the Criminal Code.
