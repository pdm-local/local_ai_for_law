**Matter:** Lease agreement between Brightlane Technologies B.V. (Landlord) and Polderzicht Digital B.V. (Tenant) regarding the commercial premises at Kanalendijk 45, Eindhoven.

**1. Facts Presented by Client**
Ms. van der Meer reported that Polderzicht Digital B.V. has failed to pay rent for the past **5 months**. The arrears accumulate from October 2024 through February 2025. The monthly contractual rent is set at €8,500.00, resulting in a total outstanding debt of €42,500.00.

Despite repeated written demands sent via registered mail on 1 November 2024 and 1 December 2024, the tenant has ceased communication. The last known correspondence from the tenant was dated 20 September 2024, in which they alleged significant defects in the heating system and roof insulation of the premises.

**2. Legal Assessment**
The non-payment of rent constitutes a serious breach of the lease agreement under Article 7:290 DCC. However, Mr. Koster advised Ms. van der Meer that we must carefully navigate the tenant’s potential defence based on defects in the leased property.

Under Article 7:206 DCC, the landlord is generally responsible for major repairs and defects that render the property unfit for its intended purpose. If Polderzicht Digital B.V. can substantiate that the heating system failure rendered the office space unusable during winter months, a court may reduce the rent obligation or suspend the duty to pay until repairs are made. This defence is legally valid but requires proof from the tenant regarding the severity of the defect and its impact on usability.

**3. Strategy and Recommendations**
Mr. Koster outlined the following course of action:

1.  **Formal Notice (Ingebrekestelling):** We will immediately issue a formal notice of default to Polderzicht Digital B.V., demanding payment of the full arrears within 14 days, alongside a demand for immediate cure of any alleged defects with proper documentation (photos, expert reports).
2.  **Preparation for Eviction Proceedings:** If payment is not received, we will file for dissolution of the lease agreement under Article 7:274 DCC. The claim will seek both eviction and payment of arrears.
3.  **Evidence Gathering:** Ms. van der Meer is instructed to gather all maintenance records showing that reasonable efforts were made to repair the heating system between September and October 2024, and to commission an independent expert report confirming that the premises remained suitable for commercial use during the disputed period.

**4. Next Steps**
*   Mr. Koster will draft the formal notice of default by 14 March 2025.
*   Ms. van der Meer is requested to provide all maintenance invoices and emails regarding the repair attempts by 13 March 2025.
*   A follow-up consultation is scheduled for 20 March 2025 to review the tenant’s response (if any) and decide on filing the summons with the Subdistrict Court (Kantonrechter) in Eindhoven.
