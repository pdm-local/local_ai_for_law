**SUBJECT:** Post-Detention Strategy and Financial Explanation

### 1. Introduction and Context

This note records the consultation held on 14 November 2025 with Mr. J. van den Berg, sole director of Orion Systems B.V., following his release from pre-trial detention. The client was held in custody for a period of **30 days** under Article 67 Code of Criminal Procedure (Sv) regarding allegations of money laundering under Article 420bis of the Dutch Penal Code (Wetboek van Strafrecht).

The investigation focused on a specific transaction dated 28 August 2025, in which funds amounting to **31.000** euros were transferred from an offshore account held by a subsidiary, "Orion Holdings Ltd," to the operational bank account of Orion Systems B.V. The Public Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie) alleged that these funds originated from illegal gambling operations in an unregulated jurisdiction.

### 2. Client’s Statement and Defense Strategy

Mr. van den Berg vehemently denies any criminal intent or knowledge regarding illicit origins of the funds. He provided a detailed explanation for the origin of the **31.000** euros, supported by documentary evidence gathered during the detention period.

#### A. Origin of Funds
The funds in question represent the repayment of a commercial loan extended by Orion Holdings Ltd to Orion Systems B.V. on 15 January 2024. This loan was documented via a formal inter-company credit agreement (ref: INT-LOAN-2024-01). The interest accrued and the principal repayment were calculated according to market rates agreed upon at the time of lending.

#### B. Lack of Criminal Intent
Mr. van den Berg asserts that all due diligence procedures regarding the source of funds were completed prior to the transfer. He produced a Due Diligence Report dated 10 August 2025, prepared by an independent compliance firm in Amsterdam, confirming the clean status of Orion Holdings Ltd at that time. The report was not known to be under investigation at the moment of transfer.

#### C. Procedural Arguments
During the detention hearings, our argument centered on the lack of reasonable suspicion for prolonged detention given the clear commercial nature of the transaction. The release after **30 days** is viewed as a tactical victory, limiting the reputational damage and operational disruption to Orion Systems B.V.

### 3. Documentary Evidence Reviewed

The following documents were reviewed during this consultation and will be included in the file:
1. Inter-company Credit Agreement dated 15 January 2024.
2. Bank statements showing the outgoing loan transfer on 16 January 2024.
3. Bank statements showing the incoming repayment of **31.000** euros on 28 August 2025.
4. Independent Due Diligence Report dated 10 August 2025.
5. Minutes of the Board Meeting approving the receipt of funds, dated 27 August 2025.

### 4. Legal Assessment and Next Steps

While the detention has ended, the investigation by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-NL) remains active. The Public Prosecution Service may still pursue a criminal charge if they can prove *mens rea* (criminal intent), which is difficult given the documented commercial history between the entities.

**Action Items:**
1. **Formal Submission of Evidence:** We will draft a formal statement to the Public Prosecution Service, attaching the Due Diligence Report and the inter-company loan agreements, explicitly denying any violation of Article 420bis Criminal Code.
2. **Administrative Compliance:** We must ensure that Orion Systems B.V. has filed its Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) correctly with the FIU-NL to demonstrate transparency. Any omission could be construed as aiding and abetting.
3. **Reputational Management:** Given the public nature of pre-trial detention decisions, we will prepare a standard press release clarifying the commercial nature of the transactions to protect the company's standing with its SME clients and suppliers.

### 5. Conclusion

The client remains cooperative but concerned about the impact on his professional reputation. The denial of money laundering charges is firm. Our strategy shifts from defense against detention to proactive defense against indictment, focusing on the civil/commercial nature of the fund transfer rather than a criminal one.

We will await the next procedural step from the Investigating Judge (Rechtbank) regarding the potential referral to trial or closure of the case.
