**Attendance Note**

**Matter No.:** 2025.0128
**Date:** 14 March 2025
**Subject:** Intake – Administrative Penalty Order (Laster)

**1. Introduction**
On 14 March 2025, I held a consultation with Mr. J. van der Meer, Director of Kempen Networks B.V., located at Stratumseind 88, 5611 EG Eindhoven. The purpose of this meeting was to review the legal standing and procedural options following the receipt of an administrative penalty order (laster) issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven.

**2. Facts of the Case**
The client informed me that on 10 February 2025, they received a formal notification from the Municipality of Eindhoven imposing a financial penalty for violations related to the installation of unauthorized telecommunications infrastructure on municipal property. The municipality has assessed the total amount of the penalty at € 10.000.

The client maintains that the installation was necessary for emergency network redundancy and that prior verbal agreements with municipal employees existed, although no written permit was secured. However, the client acknowledges that they proceeded with the work without a formal administrative decision.

**3. Legal Assessment and Procedural Deadlines**
I have advised Mr. van der Meer on the immediate procedural requirements. It is crucial to note that the penalty order includes a formal objection period. I explicitly emphasized that the deadline to file a written objection (bezwaarschrift) against this decision is strictly limited to 8 weeks from the date of notification.

Given that the notification was received on 10 February 2025, the final day for submitting a valid objection is 7 April 2025. Failure to submit an objection within this timeframe will result in the penalty becoming legally enforceable and irrevocable.

I have reviewed the principles of proportionality under Article 3:4 GALA and the requirements for proper administrative procedure. While the client’s arguments regarding necessity may hold merit, the lack of prior authorization presents a significant challenge. I advised that any objection must focus primarily on procedural errors in the issuance of the order rather than solely on the merits of the violation.

**4. Recommendations and Next Steps**
1.  **Preparation of Objection:** We will begin drafting a detailed objection immediately to ensure it is filed well before the 8-week deadline. The objection will argue that the penalty amount of € 10.000 is disproportionate given the minor nature of the intrusion and the client’s willingness to regularize the situation retrospectively.
2.  **Evidence Collection:** Mr. van der Meer is to provide all email correspondence with municipal staff from Q4 2024 to support the claim of implied permission.
3.  **Risk Assessment:** I have warned the client that if the objection is rejected, the municipality may proceed to enforcement (dwangsom) or refer the matter to the Public Prosecution Service for criminal liability under relevant provisions of the General Administrative Law Act.

**5. Conclusion**
The matter is urgent due to the approaching deadline. The client has instructed Meridiaan Advocaten B.V. to represent them in the objection procedure. I will contact Mr. van der Meer again on 18 March 2025 to review the first draft of the objection.
