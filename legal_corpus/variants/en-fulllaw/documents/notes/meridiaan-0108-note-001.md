**Subject:** Intake regarding the termination of the employment relationship with Forta Logistics B.V. and associated financial liabilities.

### 1. Introduction and Mandate

Mr. van der Meer attended our offices to discuss the urgent need to terminate the employment contract of Mr. J. Kowalski, who is employed by Helix Data B.V. but currently seconded to work on-site at Forta Logistics B.V. The client wishes to end this arrangement immediately due to a breakdown in trust and operational inefficiencies.

### 2. Facts as Presented by the Client

Mr. van der Meer reports that Mr. Kowalski has been present at the Forta Logistics B.V. facility since 1 January 2023. The secondment was originally intended for a period of six months to oversee IT infrastructure integration. However, tensions between Mr. Kowalski and the management of Forta Logistics B.V. have escalated significantly over the past three weeks.

The client states that Mr. Kowalski has refused to follow direct instructions from Forta’s site manager regarding data security protocols, citing his employment relationship remains solely with Helix Data B.V. Despite repeated warnings issued by Helix Data B.V. in February and April 2025, no improvement has been observed.

The client now seeks to dismiss Mr. Kowalski for urgent cause (*dringende reden*) or, alternatively, through a mutual termination agreement. The client is particularly concerned about the financial implications of such a dismissal, specifically the transition payment (*transitievergoeding*).

### 3. Legal Assessment and Advice

I have advised Mr. van der Meer on the legal framework governing employment contracts under Book 7 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC).

**3.1. Grounds for Dismissal**
I highlighted that dismissal for urgent cause under Article 6:74 DCC and general principles of reasonableness and fairness (*redelijkheid en billijkheid*) per Article 6:248(2) DCC requires a seriously culpable act by the employee. The refusal to follow instructions on-site may constitute such a breach, but given the secondary employment context involving Forta Logistics B.V., establishing urgent cause solely based on third-party complaints is legally precarious. A court dissolution (*kantonrechter*) under Article 7:671b DCC would be the safer, albeit longer, route if mutual consent fails.

**3.2. Transition Payment Liability**
The most critical aspect of this intake is the financial exposure regarding the transition payment. Under Article 7:673 DCC, an employer is obliged to pay a statutory transition payment upon termination of the employment contract, unless the dismissal is due to urgent cause attributable to the employee.

I calculated the potential liability based on Mr. Kowalski’s tenure (2 years and 4 months) and his monthly gross salary of € 3,500. The statutory formula applies one-third of the monthly salary per year of service.
*   Calculation: (€ 3,500 / 3) x 2.33 years ≈ € 2,716.
*   However, the client’s internal policy and previous case law suggest a negotiated settlement might be required to avoid litigation costs.

Mr. van der Meer emphasized that the total financial package for the exit, including severance, notice period pay, and administrative costs, must not exceed a strict budget cap. I confirmed that the figure of **8.750** euros represents the maximum outlay Helix Data B.V. is willing to accept as a full and final settlement to part ways with Forta Logistics B.V.’s operational requirements without further litigation. This amount covers the transition payment, indemnity in lieu of notice, and legal fees.

**3.3. Role of Forta Logistics B.V.**
I advised that while Forta Logistics B.V. is the beneficiary of Mr. Kowalski’s services, they are not the employer. Therefore, Helix Data B.V. bears the sole statutory liability for dismissal costs. We discussed drafting a tripartite agreement to release all parties from further claims, ensuring Forta Logistics B.V. acknowledges the termination and waives any right to claim damages from Helix Data B.V. for breach of the secondary service agreement.

### 4. Action Plan

1.  **Drafting:** Mr. Havelaar will draft a mutual termination agreement (*vaststellingsovereenkomst*) for Mr. Kowalski, incorporating the settlement figure of € 8.750 as the total compensation.
2.  **Negotiation:** We will initiate contact with Forta Logistics B.V. to align on the tripartite release of claims, ensuring they do not contest the dismissal grounds.
3.  **Execution:** The agreement must be signed by 30 May 2025 to avoid accrual of additional salary obligations in June 2025.

### 5. Conclusion

The file is weak regarding unilateral dismissal for urgent cause due to the complex secondment structure. A mutual termination agreement capping the total cost at € 8.750 is the recommended strategy to achieve a clean break with minimal legal risk and financial exposure.
