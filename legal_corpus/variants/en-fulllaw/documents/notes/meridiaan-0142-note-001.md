**Attendance Note**

- Ms. J. van der Meer (Director, Maaslicht Interactive B.V.)  

**Subject:** Intake regarding administrative penalty order issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven

**1. Introduction and Background**  
Mr. Slootweg welcomed Ms. van der Meer to the intake meeting. The purpose of the meeting was to discuss the legal standing of Maaslicht Interactive B.V. following the receipt of an administrative penalty order (*bestuurlijke boete*) issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven.  

Ms. van der Meer confirmed that she has been appointed as the director and legal representative of Maaslicht Interactive B.V., with its registered office at Stratumseind 12, 5611 ER Eindhoven. The company operates a digital marketing platform providing e-commerce solutions for local retailers.

**2. Facts of the Case**  
On 3 April 2025, Maaslicht Interactive B.V. received a formal notification from the Municipality of Eindhoven imposing a penalty of € 50.000. The order was issued pursuant to the Municipal Ordinance on Public Order and Safety (*Verordening Openbare Orde en Veiligheid*), specifically regarding alleged violations of zoning regulations related to the operation of a server room within a residential-complex building at Stratumseind 12.  

The Municipality asserts that the installation exceeds the allowable thermal output for mixed-use zones and poses a fire safety risk under municipal inspection protocols. Ms. van der Meer disputes the findings, arguing that the infrastructure was installed under a temporary permit granted by the city planning department in November 2024, which she claims should shield the company from immediate punitive measures.

**3. Legal Assessment and Procedural Deadlines**  
Mr. Slootweg reviewed the attached penalty order (*beschikking*). The document explicitly states that an objection (*bezwaar*) must be filed within **6 weeks** from the date of receipt, which is 3 April 2025. This means the final deadline for submitting the objection is 15 May 2025.  

Mr. Slootweg advised that:  
- The amount of **€ 50.000** represents a significant financial exposure and potential precedent for future enforcement actions.  
- Failure to file within the strict statutory period will result in the penalty becoming enforceable (*onherroepelijk*), allowing the Municipality to initiate debt collection proceedings through the bailiff (*deurwaarder*).  
- Given the complexity of interpreting the intersection between municipal zoning powers and prior administrative permissions, filing a detailed objection is legally advisable.

**4. Discussion of Options**  
Ms. van der Meer inquired about the possibility of negotiating a reduction of the penalty amount before proceeding to formal objection. Mr. Slootweg explained that while informal negotiations with the municipality’s legal department are possible, they carry no guarantee and must not delay the filing of the formal objection if the **6 weeks** deadline is approaching rapidly.  

It was agreed that Maaslicht Interactive B.V. wishes to contest the penalty entirely based on the existence of the prior temporary permit. The strategy will focus on demonstrating administrative consistency by the Municipality itself, rather than merely seeking a discount.

**5. Action Items and Next Steps**  
1. **Immediate Review:** Mr. Slootweg will review the original November 2024 permit documentation and all correspondence with the Eindhoven city planning office to establish a factual baseline.  
2. **Drafting Objection:** Meridiaan Advocaten will draft a formal objection (*bezwaarschrift*) arguing that the penalty is disproportionate and contradicts prior administrative assurances, citing principles of legitimate expectation and good administration under the General Administrative Law Act (Awb).  
3. **Submission Timeline:** The objection must be finalized and signed by 1 May 2025 to allow for internal review and potential last-minute negotiations, ensuring submission well before the **6 weeks** expiration on 15 May 2025.  
4. **Fee Agreement:** A separate fee agreement will be sent to Ms. van der Meer outlining costs for drafting the objection and potential subsequent administrative court proceedings.

**6. Conclusion**  
Ms. van der Meer expressed satisfaction with the clarity of the advice and confirmed her instruction to Meridiaan Advocaten to proceed with the defense. The matter is considered urgent due to the tight statutory timeline.
