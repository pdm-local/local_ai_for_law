**1. Background and Factual Context**

On 15 January 2025, Meridiaan Advocaten B.V. conducted an intake meeting with representatives of Orion Interactive B.V. ("Client"). The Client is a software development firm specializing in e-commerce platforms. Their primary concern relates to the current master service agreement (MSA) and associated service level agreements (SLAs) with their hosting provider, Novael Networks B.V. ("Counterparty").

The contract in question was executed on 1 March 2023 and is set for renewal on 1 March 2025. Over the last six months, Orion Interactive has experienced three significant service outages, each lasting between four and eight hours. During these incidents, the Client’s end-users suffered data loss and inability to access services. Novael Networks B.V. has refused to compensate for consequential damages, citing a liability cap clause in Article 8.2 of the MSA, which limits liability to the amount paid by the Client in the preceding twelve months (€12,000).

**2. Legal Issues Identified**

During the discussion, Mr. van der Meer and Ms. de Vries raised the following specific legal concerns:

1.  **Unreasonableness of Liability Caps:** The Client argues that limiting liability to €12,000 is grossly disproportionate given the potential damages caused by downtime, which they estimate at over €150,000 in lost revenue and reputational damage. They seek to challenge the validity of this cap under general principles of reasonableness and fairness.
2.  **Service Level Breach:** The SLA guarantees 99.9% uptime. Novael has failed to meet this threshold for three consecutive months. The Client demands a full refund of monthly fees for the affected periods and compensation for losses exceeding the contractual cap.
3.  **Duty of Care:** The Client questions whether Novael fulfilled its duty of care in maintaining adequate backup systems, potentially constituting a breach of professional standards.

**3. Legal Analysis and Advice**

Mr. Bergsma provided preliminary advice based on Dutch contract law:

*   **Reasonableness and Fairness (Redelijkheid en Billijkheid):** Under Article 6:248 DCC and specifically paragraph 2 thereof, a contractual term that creates an unjustified imbalance between the rights and obligations of the parties may be declared null or unenforceable. Case law from the Dutch Supreme Court suggests that liability caps in B2B contracts are not automatically invalid but must be reasonable. Given the high-value nature of the Client’s business and the low cost of Novael’s services, a €12,000 cap may be deemed unreasonable under Article 6:248(2) DCC if it excludes liability for gross negligence or intentional misconduct.
*   **Duty of Care:** Pursuant to Article 7:401 DCC, Novael Networks B.V. has a duty to perform its obligations with the care expected of a reasonable service provider. Failure to maintain adequate redundancy measures could constitute a breach of this duty under Article 6:74 DCC.
*   **General Terms:** If the MSA is considered a set of general terms, Article 6:233 DCC applies. Clauses that exclude liability for gross negligence are often invalid in standard form contracts unless explicitly negotiated and highlighted.

**4. Recommended Next Steps**

1.  **Document Review:** Meridiaan Advocaten B.V. will conduct a full review of the MSA, SLA, and all correspondence regarding the outages. This review will be completed by 22 January 2025.
2.  **Formal Demand Letter:** Based on the review, we will draft a formal demand letter to Novael Networks B.V. invoking Article 6:248(2) DCC to challenge the liability cap and demanding compensation for actual damages.
3.  **Negotiation Strategy:** We recommend initiating negotiations with Novael Networks B.V. before considering litigation. A threat of invoking inquiry proceedings under Article 2:345 DCC (if Novael is part of a larger group) or filing a claim in the Rotterdam District Court may encourage settlement.
4.  **Contract Renewal:** For the upcoming renewal on 1 March 2025, we advise drafting a new MSA with balanced liability clauses and clearer service level remedies.

**5. Action Items**

*   **Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.:** Review all contractual documents and outage reports by 22 January 2025.
*   **Orion Interactive B.V.:** Provide all technical logs related to the three outages and a detailed calculation of consequential damages by 18 January 2025.

**6. Conclusion**

The Client has strong grounds to challenge the liability cap under Article 6:248(2) DCC. We will proceed with a formal legal assessment following the document review. This matter is marked as privileged work product.
