**1. Purpose of Meeting**
The meeting was convened to discuss the current default position regarding the lease agreement between our client, Helix Networks B.V. (landlord), and Brightlane Retail B.V. (tenant) for the premises located at Herestraat 42, 5611 ER Eindhoven.

**2. Factual Background**
Ms. van der Meer confirmed that the tenancy commenced on 1 September 2024. According to the payment history provided during the intake, Brightlane Retail B.V. has failed to pay rent for the periods of November, December, January, and February 2025. The total outstanding amount is €24,000.00 (twenty-four thousand euros), excluding statutory interest and collection costs.

Our client reports that despite three written warnings sent on 1 December 2024, 5 January 2025, and 10 February 2025, no payments have been received. Consequently, Helix Networks B.V. has accrued **4 months** of rent arrears.

**3. Tenant’s Position and Defences**
Ms. van der Meer indicated that Brightlane Retail B.V. has not formally responded to the warnings but was verbally aggressive during a site visit on 12 March 2025. During this visit, the tenant alleged that the heating system in the retail unit is defective and that the landlord has failed to perform necessary repairs regarding the roof insulation, rendering the premises partially unusable.

We have noted this "defects defence." Under Article 7:206 DCC, a tenant may suspend rent payments if the landlord fails to maintain the property in good condition, provided the defect significantly impairs the use of the premises. However, Ms. van der Meer admitted that no formal complaint regarding these defects was lodged in writing prior to the current default.

**4. Legal Assessment**
The primary legal basis for eviction is Article 7:274 DCC (termination/eviction). Given the substantial arrears, we are well-positioned to seek a court order for dissolution of the lease and eviction.

However, the tenant’s potential reliance on repair defects under Article 7:206 DCC cannot be ignored. If Brightlane Retail B.V. files a counterclaim or raises this defence in preliminary injunction proceedings (*kort geding*), the court may examine whether the defects justify rent suspension. Given that the heating issue was not reported until after the arrears began, our position is strong that the defect does not justify withholding rent for four months entirely.

**5. Recommended Strategy**
1.  **Immediate Formal Notice:** Send a final notice of default (*ingebrekestelling*) to Brightlane Retail B.V., demanding payment of €24,000.00 plus interest within 7 days, under threat of immediate legal action for dissolution and eviction.
2.  **Evidence Preservation:** Request an independent expert report on the heating system to refute any claim that the premises are unusable.
3.  **Litigation Preparation:** Prepare the petition for the President of the District Court of Eindhoven for urgent proceedings (*kort geding*) if payment is not received within the 7-day period. We will cite Article 7:274 DCC as the primary ground for termination.

**6. Next Steps**
*   Meridiaan Advocaten B.V. to draft and send the final notice of default.
*   Helix Networks B.V. to provide all correspondence logs with Brightlane Retail B.V. by 18 March 2025.
*   Ms. van der Meer to confirm authorization to proceed with litigation if the deadline passes without payment.
