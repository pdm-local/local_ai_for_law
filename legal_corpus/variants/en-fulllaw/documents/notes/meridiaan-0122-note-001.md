**Subject:** Termination of cooperation agreement with Klaver Software B.V. and financial consequences

### 1. Introduction
On 12 April 2025, Meridiaan Advocaten conducted an intake meeting with Ms. E. van der Meer regarding the ongoing dispute between Tinexus Systems B.V. (“Tinexus”) and Klaver Software B.V. (“Klaver”). The client wishes to terminate the cooperation immediately due to persistent non-performance by Klaver.

### 2. Facts
Ms. van dermeer reported that the cooperation began on 1 January 2024 under a service level agreement (SLA) for custom software development. Despite multiple written warnings sent in February and March 2025, Klaver failed to deliver critical milestones. Tinexus has suffered operational delays due to this failure.

The client is particularly concerned about the financial implications of ending the contract prematurely, specifically regarding statutory transition payment obligations under Dutch employment law principles if any staff overlap exists, or contractual damages. However, as both parties are legal entities, the primary focus is on breach of contract and potential termination costs.

### 3. Legal Assessment
The current file is assessed as **weak** for a full litigation strategy based on Article 6:74 DCC (breach/damages) because the SLA lacks clear liquidated damages clauses for delay. Termination under Article 6:265 DCC (rescission) requires proof of imputable failure, which is established but difficult to quantify without further evidence.

During the discussion, we addressed the potential liability for a transition payment should any individual contractors be deemed employees de facto. The client noted a preliminary figure of **€ 21.300** as a potential settlement amount or calculated risk exposure for such transitional costs if Klaver were to claim unfair dismissal equivalents for shared resources. This figure represents the upper bound of our estimated liability for personnel transition costs, distinct from any contractual penalties.

### 4. Advice
We advised Tinexus that:
1. Immediate written notice of termination for urgent cause is required under Article 6:265 DCC.
2. The file on Klaver’s breach is insufficient for a strong damages claim; settlement is preferable.
3. The figure of € 21.300 should be treated as a maximum acceptable outlay for any personnel-related transition claims, not as a penalty to Klaver.

### 5. Next Steps
- Draft termination notice citing imputable failure.
- Prepare counterparty correspondence addressing the € 21.300 liability cap in settlement talks.
- Request all technical delivery logs from Tinexus IT department by 19 April 2025.
