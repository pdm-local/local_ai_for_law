**ATTENDANCE NOTE**

**Matter:** 2025.0139 – Klaver Logistics B.V. v. Dutch Tax Administration
**Date:** 14 March 2025
**Time:** 10:00 – 11:15 CET
**Location:** Meridiaan Advocaten, Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven (and via secure video link)
**Attendees:** mr. F. Lindeman (Meridiaan Advocaten), Mr. J. van der Meer (Director, Klaver Logistics B.V.)

### 1. Introduction and Mandate

Mr. Lindeman welcomed Mr. van der Meer and confirmed his mandate to represent Klaver Logistics B.V. ("the Client") in response to the tax assessment received on 28 February 2025. The primary objective of this session was to review the factual basis of the reassessment, assess the validity of the imposed penalty, and determine the appropriate procedural steps.

### 2. Factual Background and Assessment Details

The Dutch Tax Administration issued a reassessment for the fiscal year 2024. The administration contends that certain expenses related to cross-border fuel purchases were not deductible due to alleged incomplete documentation. Specifically, the Tax Administration has increased the taxable profit by **€ 460.000**.

In addition to the additional tax liability resulting from this increase, the Tax Administration has imposed a **50%** penalty for incorrect reporting. Mr. van der Meer confirmed that the Client maintains comprehensive digital logs of all transactions and that the missing documentation cited by the administration consists merely of administrative delays in scanning invoices, not the absence of underlying commercial reality.

### 3. Legal Analysis and Strategy

Mr. Lindeman provided legal advice regarding the prospects of success for an objection procedure:

**A. Substantive Dispute (The Additional Assessment)**
Under **Article 16 AWR**, the Tax Administration bears the burden of proof when issuing a new assessment based on new facts or estimates. The administration’s reliance on estimated figures without demanding specific corroborating evidence prior to the assessment is procedurally weak. We intend to challenge the causal link between the alleged documentation gaps and the disallowance of costs, citing **Article 67d AWR** principles regarding proportionality if the penalty were to be upheld without substantive fault.

**B. The Penalty (50% Surcharge)**
The imposition of a **50%** penalty is severe and typically reserved for cases involving gross negligence or intentional concealment. Given that Klaver Logistics B.V. has a clean compliance history over the past five years and acted in good faith, this penalty is disproportionate. We will argue that the error was minor and inadvertent, warranting no penalty or at most a reduced percentage under general administrative law principles.

**C. Procedural Steps**
1.  **Filing an Objection:** An objection against the assessment must be filed within six weeks of receipt (by 27 April 2025). The deadline is strict and non-extendable in this phase.
2.  **Evidence Submission:** We will prepare a detailed dossier containing sample invoices, fuel card logs, and transport manifests to disprove the administration’s estimate.
3.  **Request for Waiver of Penalty:** Concurrent with the objection, we will submit a formal request for waiver of the **50%** penalty based on the absence of culpability.

### 4. Recommendations and Next Steps

Mr. Lindeman recommended that Klaver Logistics B.V. immediately authorize the filing of the objection. The Client was advised that failure to file within the statutory period results in the assessment becoming final and binding.

**Action Items:**
*   Klaver Logistics B.V. to provide full access to the digital invoice archive by 21 March 2025.
*   Meridiaan Advocaten to draft the objection letter and penalty waiver request by 31 March 2025.
*   Review of final drafts by Mr. van der Meer for factual accuracy regarding logistics operations.

### 5. Conclusion

The meeting concluded with an understanding that the Client disagrees with both the factual basis of the **€ 460.000** adjustment and the severity of the **50%** penalty. The matter is classified as privileged litigation work product.
