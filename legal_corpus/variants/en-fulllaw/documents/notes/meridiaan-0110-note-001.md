**Attendance Note**

**Matter:** 2025.0110 – Criminal Defense (Money Laundering)
**Date:** 28 March 2025
**Time:** 14:30 – 16:15
**Location:** Meridiaan Advocaten B.V., Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven
**Attendees:** mr. R. Bahadoer (Advocate), Mr. J. van der Meer (CEO Novael Cloud B.V.)

**1. Context and Background**

This note records the consultation held following the client’s release from pre-trial detention. As per our previous correspondence, the Public Prosecution Service had requested a period of **14 days** for further investigation into the alleged financial irregularities associated with recent transactions processed through Novael Cloud B.V.’s accounts. The client has now been released on bail pending further proceedings.

The primary focus of today’s meeting was to address the specific allegations under Article 420bis Criminal Code (money laundering) concerning a sum of **€ 72.500**. The prosecution initially characterized this transfer as proceeds of crime derived from an undisclosed upstream predicate offense.

**2. Client’s Position and Statements**

Mr. van der Meer explicitly and unequivocally denied that Novael Cloud B.V. committed any act of money laundering. He stated that the company operates with full transparency regarding its anti-money laundering (CFT) obligations under the Dutch Financial Supervision Act (Wft).

Regarding the specific transaction in question, Mr. van der Meer provided a detailed explanation:
*   The amount of **€ 72.500** represents a legitimate service fee for cloud infrastructure hosting services rendered to a corporate client based in Germany.
*   The funds were transferred from a verified business account (IBAN ending in ...4412) and not from a private or anonymous source.
*   A valid invoice (Factuur 2025-0301) was issued prior to the receipt of funds, compliant with Dutch VAT regulations.

**3. Evidence Provided**

To substantiate the client’s denial and account for the origin of the funds, Mr. van der Meer submitted the following documentation to the firm:
1.  The signed service agreement with the German counterparty, dated 10 February 2025.
2.  The corresponding pro-forma invoice dated 12 February 2025.
3.  Bank statements from Rabobank Eindhoven confirming the receipt of the funds on 15 February 2025, matching the exact amount of **€ 72.500**.
4.  A certificate of incorporation and a recent extraction from the Dutch Trade Register (KvK) verifying the good standing of Novael Cloud B.V.

**4. Legal Assessment**

The facts presented by the client do not currently meet the threshold for "reasonable suspicion" sufficient to sustain criminal liability under Article 420bis Criminal Code, provided the upstream crime cannot be identified or proven. The existence of a clear commercial trail (invoice and service agreement) strongly suggests a lawful economic purpose rather than an attempt to conceal illicit proceeds.

However, we must remain cautious. The prosecution may still argue that the German counterparty was involved in predicate offenses. We will need to verify the identity of the German counterparty through the client’s KYC (Know Your Customer) records.

**5. Action Items and Next Steps**

1.  **Drafting a Written Statement:** mr. Bahadoer will draft a formal response to the Public Prosecution Service, refuting the money laundering allegation based on the evidence provided today. This document will cite the lack of intent (opzet) and the lawful origin of funds.
2.  **KYC Verification:** The firm’s compliance officer will review the internal KYC file for the German client to ensure no red flags were missed during onboarding.
3.  **Preparation for Hearing:** A hearing is scheduled for 10 April 2025 at the District Court of Eindhoven regarding the continuation of pre-trial detention (if reinstated) or the dismissal of charges. We will prepare a motion to dismiss based on insufficient evidence.

**6. Conclusion**

The client remains cooperative and confident in the legitimacy of the transactions. The immediate risk of criminal conviction appears low, provided the prosecution cannot identify a valid predicate offense linked to the source of funds. We will proceed with a defense strategy focused on the lawful nature of the business relationship.
