**ATTENDANCE NOTE**

**Matter:** 2025.0119 – Meridiaan Media B.V. / Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)
**Date:** 12 April 2025
**Author:** mr. L. Okafor, Privacy / GDPR Counsel
**Recipient:** Internal File / Client Instruction

### 1. Context and Incident Overview

This note records the strategic advice provided to Meridiaan Media B.V. following the discovery of a significant security breach on their primary customer database. The incident was detected by the client’s IT security team on the morning of **10 April 2025 at 08:30 CET**. Upon forensic analysis, it was confirmed that an unauthorized third party had accessed personal data belonging to approximately 4,500 users between **5 April 2025 and 9 April 2025**.

The compromised data included names, email addresses, hashed passwords, and IP addresses. No financial payment data or health-related sensitive data was involved. The breach originated from a vulnerability in the client’s legacy API endpoint, which has since been patched.

### 2. Legal Assessment under Article 33 GDPR

We have evaluated the facts against the requirements of **Article 33 GDPR** regarding notification to the supervisory authority.

**a) Risk Assessment**
The unauthorized access involves direct identifiers (names, emails) and technical data (IP addresses). While no financial loss is immediately apparent, the exposure of credentials poses a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, specifically the risk of identity theft, phishing attacks, and credential stuffing across other platforms. Consequently, this constitutes a personal data breach likely to result in a risk for the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

**b) Notification Obligation**
Under **Article 33 GDPR**, Meridiaan Media B.V. is obligated to notify this breach to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than **72 hours** after having become aware of the personal data breach.

The clock for the **72 hours** period started on 10 April 2025 at 08:30 CET. The final deadline for notification is therefore **12 April 2025 at 08:30 CET**. Given that today is **12 April 2025**, we are within the critical window. Failure to notify within this strict timeframe would constitute a separate violation of the GDPR, potentially leading to administrative fines.

### 3. Decision on Notification to Data Subjects (Article 34)

We have also assessed whether **Article 34 GDPR** applies, which mandates communication to data subjects if the breach is likely to result in a high risk.

While the risk is significant, we have determined that immediate communication to all 4,500 users is not legally required at this specific moment provided that appropriate remedial measures are taken immediately. These measures include:
1.  Mandatory password resets for all affected users (already initiated).
2.  Implementation of two-factor authentication (already mandated by the client).
3.  Provision of clear guidance on how to secure accounts.

We will monitor the situation for any signs of actual misuse. If there is evidence that the data has been used for fraud or if the risk escalates, we will advise immediate direct communication with the affected individuals. For now, a general transparency notice on the client’s website and in their newsletter suffices to maintain trust without triggering the complex logistics of individual notifications under Article 34.

### 4. Action Plan and Next Steps

1.  **Immediate Notification:** We are drafting the formal notification to the Dutch Data Protection Authority. The notification will include:
    *   The nature of the breach.
    *   The categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned (4,500).
    *   The likely consequences (identity theft, phishing).
    *   The measures taken to address the breach (patching, password resets).

2.  **Submission Deadline:** The notification will be submitted via the Authority’s online portal by **17:00 CET on 12 April 2025** to ensure compliance with the **72 hours** rule.

3.  **Documentation:** We are compiling a comprehensive record of the breach, including the facts, effects, and remedial actions, as required by Article 33(5) GDPR. This file will be retained for supervisory review.

4.  **Follow-up:** We will schedule a follow-up meeting with Meridiaan Media B.V.’s management on **15 April 2025** to review the Authority’s acknowledgment and discuss any further obligations.

### 5. Conclusion

It is legally imperative that we proceed with the notification to the Dutch DPA immediately to remain compliant with **Article 33 GDPR**. The risk mitigation steps already taken by the client reduce the likelihood of severe harm, justifying our decision not to mandate immediate individual notifications under Article 34 at this stage.
