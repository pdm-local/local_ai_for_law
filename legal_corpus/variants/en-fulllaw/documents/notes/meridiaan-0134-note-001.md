**Attendance Note – Matter 2025.0134**

**Date:** 12 April 2025
**Time:** 10:00 – 11:15 CET
**Location:** Meridiaan Advocaten B.V., Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven (via secure video link)
**Attendees:**
*   Mr. S. Reinders (Meridiaan Advocaten)
*   Ms. A. van der Meer, Director of Stroomdal Online B.V.

**1. Purpose of the Meeting**
The intake session was convened to evaluate a potential infringement of intellectual property rights concerning a specific product design held by Brightlane Networks B.V. The client, Stroomdal Online B.V., reported that their proprietary design for the "StreamLink" interface has been closely replicated by a competitor.

**2. Facts Presented by Client**
Ms. van der Meer provided evidence showing substantial visual and functional similarities between the StreamLink interface (developed in 2021) and the newly launched "LaneFlow" product by Brightlane Networks B.V., released in March 2025. The client asserts that the copy is not merely coincidental but constitutes a deliberate reproduction of protected elements.

**3. Reference to Prior Settlement**
During the discussion, the client highlighted a previous conflict with Brightlane Networks B.V. regarding a different technical module. That matter was resolved out of court via a settlement agreement concluded on 15 June 2023. As part of that resolution, Brightlane Networks B.V. agreed to pay a penalty of **€ 7.500** for the alleged prior infringement. The client argues that this precedent demonstrates Brightlane’s pattern of disregarding intellectual property rights and their willingness to settle quietly rather than contest claims in court.

**4. Legal Assessment**
Mr. Reinders advised that under Article 13 of the Dutch Copyright Act, the originality of the design may be protected if it constitutes a unique creation reflecting the author's personal touch. Given the high degree of similarity identified by the client’s technical team, there are reasonable grounds to suspect infringement under Article 1 and Article 27 of the Dutch Copyright Act.

However, Mr. Reinders noted that enforcing rights depends heavily on the strength of evidence regarding the originality of the specific design elements claimed. The previous settlement involving the **€ 7.500** penalty serves as an indicative factor but does not constitute legal proof of infringement in this new matter.

**5. Next Steps**
1.  **Evidence Preservation:** Stroomdal Online B.V. will immediately provide high-resolution screenshots and source code comparisons for expert analysis.
2.  **Cease and Desist Analysis:** Meridiaan Advocaten will draft a preliminary assessment of whether to send a formal letter of demand based on Article 1019h DCCP principles regarding cost recovery in potential litigation.
3.  **Cost Estimate:** A detailed quote for drafting the letter of demand and subsequent negotiations will be issued separately.

**6. Conclusion**
The matter is active. The client remains interested in an out-of-court settlement similar to the previous one, where possible, due to the clear financial precedent established by the **€ 7.500** penalty paid in 2023.
