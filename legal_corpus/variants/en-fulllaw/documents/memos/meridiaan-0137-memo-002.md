### 1. Introduction and Objective

This memorandum addresses the ongoing family law dispute between your client, Novael Systems B.V. (hereinafter "Party A"), and Hanze Interactive B.V. (hereinafter "Party B"). The core issues concern the establishment of a parenting plan, determination of primary residence, and a specific disagreement regarding the division of summer holidays. This advice is provided in light of Article 1:253a DCC, which governs joint parental authority.

### 2. Legal Framework: Joint Parental Authority

Under **Article 1:253a DCC**, both parents retain joint parental authority regardless of their marital status or cohabitation arrangements. This legal framework mandates that major decisions concerning the child’s upbringing, education, and health must be made jointly. Neither party may unilaterally decide on significant deviations from the established care schedule without the other’s consent, unless a court orders otherwise due to urgent necessity or conflict.

The recent impasse regarding the summer holidays of 2025 stems from a failure to reach mutual consensus under this statutory duty. While Party A proposed an extended holiday period in July, Party B insisted on maintaining the traditional August split. This unilateral approach is legally untenable under the current joint authority regime.

### 3. Proposed Primary Residence and Care Schedule

To ensure stability for the child, we propose formalizing the primary residence at **Eindhoven, Kennedyplein 15, 4th floor**, which has been the de facto home since January 2024. The care schedule should be structured as follows:

*   **Primary Residence:** Eindhoven, Kennedyplein 15.
*   **Weekdays:** The child resides primarily with Party A from Monday morning until Friday afternoon.
*   **Alternating Weekends:** The child resides with Party B every other weekend, from Friday at 18:00 hours until Sunday at 19:00 hours.
*   **Midweek Contact:** Party B has visitation rights on Wednesday evenings from 17:00 hours until 21:00 hours, including overnight stay if mutually agreed upon.

This schedule must be documented in a formal parenting plan to prevent future ambiguity. The transition points have been fixed at **18:00 hours** to allow for consistent routine and minimize disruption to the child’s schooling at **Basisschool De Kompas**, located on **Van der Maasplein 12, Eindhoven**.

### 4. Resolution of Holiday Dispute

The dispute concerns the division of the summer holidays in 2025. Party A wishes to take the entire period from **1 July 2025** to **31 August 2025**. Party B objects, citing their right to equal time under joint authority.

To resolve this conflict without litigation, we propose a balanced compromise:

*   **First Half of Summer Holiday:** From **1 July 2025** (after school ends) until **14 July 2025**, the child stays with Party A.
*   **Second Half of Summer Holiday:** From **15 July 2025** until **31 August 2025**, the child stays with Party B.

This split ensures equal duration for both parties during the peak holiday period. Any travel outside the Netherlands during these periods requires prior written consent from the other party, in accordance with general principles of parental responsibility. The cost of international travel shall be borne equally by both parties unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 5. Next Steps

1.  Draft a formal parenting plan incorporating the above schedule and holiday split.
2.  Circulate the draft to Party B’s legal representatives for review.
3.  Schedule a mediation session at **Meridiaan Advocaten, Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven** on **5 June 2025** if Party B does not accept the proposal by **1 June 2025**.

Failure to agree may result in an application to the District Court of 's-Hertogenbosch for a binding ruling. However, given the clear statutory preference for parental agreement under Article 1:253a DCC, we strongly advise settling this matter amicably to protect the child’s best interests.

Please indicate your acceptance of this proposed schedule so that we may proceed with drafting the formal agreement.
