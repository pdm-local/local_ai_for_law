**CONFIDENTIAL LEGAL ADVICE**

**TO:** The Board of Directors, Duinrand Networks B.V.
**FROM:** mr. M. Terhorst, Partner at Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**DATE:** 28 January 2025
**SUBJECT:** Compliance with the Distribution Test for Proposed Dividend Payment

### 1. Introduction

On behalf of Duinrand Networks B.V. (the "Company"), we have reviewed your proposal to distribute a dividend in the amount of **€480,000** to your sole shareholder, Cobalt Labs B.V. This memorandum serves as privileged legal advice regarding the statutory requirements that must be met prior to the execution of this distribution.

The Company is incorporated under Dutch law. Consequently, the proposed payout is subject to the strict solvency and net asset tests prescribed by Book 2 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC). Failure to comply with these requirements may result in personal liability for the directors who authorized the distribution.

### 2. Legal Framework: The Distribution Test

The core provision governing dividend distributions in the Netherlands is **Article 2:216 DCC**. This article mandates that a statutory company may only distribute assets to its shareholders if, after the distribution, the Company’s net assets remain sufficient to cover the paid-up and called-up part of the issued share capital plus the statutory legal reserve.

The test is not merely formal; it is substantive. The directors must assess the balance sheet position at the time of the resolution. If the distribution causes the net assets to fall below this threshold, the distribution is legally voidable, and the directors may be held jointly and severally liable for the amount distributed under Article 2:216(3) DCC.

### 3. Application to the Proposed €480,000 Dividend

We have analyzed the most recent unaudited financial statements of Duinrand Networks B.V. as of 31 December 2024. Based on our review:

1.  **Net Asset Position:** The Company’s net equity stands at **€1,250,000**.
2.  **Paid-up Capital and Reserve:** The sum of the paid-up share capital (€500,000) and the statutory legal reserve (€150,000) totals €650,000.
3.  **Buffer Calculation:** Before the distribution, the Company has a distributable surplus of **€600,000** (€1,250,000 - €650,000).

The proposed dividend of **€480,000** falls within this distributable surplus. However, the directors must ensure that no hidden liabilities or contingent obligations exist on the date of the resolution that would artificially inflate the net asset figure.

### 4. Director Liability Risks

If the distribution test is not properly conducted or if the net assets are insufficient at the time of payment, the directors who voted for the resolution become personally liable to the Company for the amount improperly distributed. This liability is joint and several among all consenting directors. Furthermore, if the Company subsequently enters insolvency proceedings within two years of the distribution, the bankruptcy trustee may demand repayment of the dividend under Article 42 Bankruptcy Act (fraudulent preference/voidable transaction).

### 5. Recommendations

To mitigate risk and ensure compliance with **Article 2:216 DCC**, we recommend the following steps before any funds are transferred to Cobalt Labs B.V.:

1.  **Board Resolution:** The Board must formally resolve that the distribution test has been passed, explicitly referencing the balance sheet figures as of the date of resolution.
2.  **Interim Balance Sheet:** Prepare a management balance sheet as of 31 January 2025 to confirm that no material adverse changes have occurred since the year-end accounts.
3.  **Verification:** Ensure that all trade payables and tax obligations due in February 2025 are accounted for in the net asset calculation.

### 6. Conclusion

The proposed distribution of **€480,000** to Cobalt Labs B.V. is legally permissible provided that the net assets immediately following the distribution remain above €650,000. The Board must document the satisfaction of this test in the minutes of the board meeting.
