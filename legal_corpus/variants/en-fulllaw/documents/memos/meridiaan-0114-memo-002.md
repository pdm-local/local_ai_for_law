**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** mr. P. Slootweg, Associate, Meridiaan Advocaten
**FROM:** Legal Department, Hanze Data B.V.
**DATE:** 12 May 2025
**SUBJECT:** Assessment of Administrative Penalty No. 2025-EIN-8892 and Application of Article 3:4 GALA

### 1. Introduction

This memorandum outlines our legal position regarding the administrative penalty notice received on 28 April 2025 from the Municipality of Eindhoven (hereinafter: "the Authority"). The Authority has imposed a fine of EUR 4,500 on Hanze Data B.V. for alleged non-compliance with reporting obligations under the Municipal Administrative Regulations regarding temporary event permits.

The purpose of this memo is to establish that the imposed penalty violates the principle of proportionality enshrined in Article 3:4 GALA (Awb) and that the decision should be overturned upon objection.

### 2. Factual Background

On 15 March 2025, Hanze Data B.V. organized a technology showcase at Kennedyplein 200 in Eindhoven. We submitted all required applications for temporary event permits on 01 February 2025. The Authority issued the permit on 10 February 2025 with specific conditions.

The incident giving rise to this penalty occurred on 16 March 2025, when our logistics team failed to submit a minor supplementary notification regarding the exact square footage of the exhibition hall within the statutory 24-hour window required for last-minute layout changes. The actual deviation was less than 2% of the total area and posed no safety risk or burden on public order. We rectified the administrative omission via email on 16 March 2025 at 14:00, within two hours of the event's completion.

Despite the immediate correction and the negligible impact on public administration, the Authority issued a penalty decision on 28 April 2025, demanding EUR 4,500.

### 3. Legal Analysis: The Principle of Proportionality

The core argument against this penalty is found in Article 3:4 GALA (Awb), which mandates that administrative bodies must exercise their powers in a manner that is proportionate to the interests at stake.

#### 3.1. Suitability and Necessity
While imposing a fine may theoretically serve as a deterrent, the Authority failed to consider less intrusive measures. A formal warning or a nominal administrative fee would have sufficed to enforce compliance given the trivial nature of the breach. The requirement to pay a substantial sum is not necessary to achieve the legitimate aim of ensuring proper event registration.

#### 3.2. Proportionality *Stricto Sensu*
The decisive factor here is the balance between the severity of the penalty and the gravity of the offense. 

*   **Gravity of the Offense:** The breach was purely administrative, minor in scope (less than 2% area deviation), and corrected immediately. It did not result in any harm to public safety, order, or the Authority's operational efficiency.
*   **Severity of the Sanction:** A penalty of EUR 4,500 is significant for a small enterprise like Hanze Data B.V., which operates on tight margins typical of the SME sector.

The disparity between the minor nature of the violation and the heavy financial burden imposed is manifestly unreasonable. The Authority has applied a rigid tariff without exercising its discretionary power to mitigate the penalty in light of the specific circumstances. This constitutes a clear violation of Article 3:4 GALA (Awb).

### 4. Conclusion and Recommendation

The penalty decision is legally flawed due to its disproportionate nature. We recommend filing an objection immediately, citing Article 3:4 GALA (Awb) as the primary ground for annulment. We should emphasize that the Authority’s failure to differentiate between malicious non-compliance and a minor, rectified administrative oversight renders the sanction unlawful.

We request your instruction to draft the formal objection letter and prepare for potential further legal steps if the Authority maintains its stance.
