**MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Helix Data B.V. (Legal Department)
**From:** mr. J. Havelaar, Partner at Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**Date:** 12 April 2025
**Re:** Matter 2025.0108 – Dismissal of Mr. J. van der Meer from Forta Logistics B.V.

### 1. Introduction and Scope

This memorandum serves as privileged legal advice regarding the proposed termination of the employment agreement between your client, Helix Data B.V., and its former subsidiary/contractor entity Forta Logistics B.V., specifically concerning the dismissal of Mr. J. van der Meer (hereinafter: "the Employee"). The purpose of this advice is to evaluate the legal risks associated with unilateral termination on grounds of urgent reason or company reasons, and to advise on a risk-mitigation strategy via settlement.

### 2. Assessment of Grounds for Dismissal under Article 7:669 DCC

We have reviewed the file provided regarding the performance issues and subsequent disciplinary measures leading to the current situation. The primary legal hurdle in any dismissal procedure is establishing valid grounds pursuant to **Article 7:669 DCC** of the Dutch Civil Code.

The file currently contains limited evidence to substantiate a claim for immediate termination based on urgent reason (*dringende reden*) or substantial company reasons (*bedrijfseconomische redenen*). Specifically:
1.  The documented warnings issued in January 2025 lacked sufficient specificity regarding the behavioral changes required.
2.  There is no record of a structured performance improvement plan that was clearly communicated and monitored over a reasonable period.

Consequently, we consider the file "thin" for ground d under **Article 7:669 DCC**. If Helix Data B.V. proceeds with a unilateral termination notice without mutual consent, there is a high probability that the Employee will seek dissolution of the employment contract via the sub-district court (*kantonrechter*). Given the procedural weaknesses in the disciplinary file, the likelihood of the court granting such dissolution and awarding compensation to the Employee is significant.

### 3. Financial Exposure: Transition Payment under Article 7:673 DCC

In the event of a court-ordered dissolution or a valid termination on company grounds, the Employee is entitled to a statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding*) pursuant to **Article 7:673 DCC**.

Based on the Employee’s length of service (4 years) and monthly salary structure, our calculation indicates that the statutory minimum transition payment amounts to exactly € 8.750. This figure represents the baseline liability. However, if the court finds the termination unreasonable (*onredelijk*) due to procedural flaws (as noted in Section 2), the court has the discretion to increase this amount significantly, potentially up to double the statutory rate or more, depending on the circumstances of the case.

Furthermore, legal costs and interest would accrue during the litigation period, which could last six to twelve months.

### 4. Recommendation: Settlement Agreement

To mitigate legal risk and control costs, we strongly advise against unilateral termination at this stage. Instead, we recommend negotiating a **Settlement Agreement** (*vaststellingsovereenkomst*) with Mr. van der Meer.

A settlement agreement offers the following advantages:
1.  **Certainty:** It avoids the unpredictability of court proceedings regarding the validity of dismissal grounds under **Article 7:669 DCC**.
2.  **Cost Control:** The financial exposure can be capped. We propose offering a transition payment of € 8.750, which aligns with the statutory minimum under **Article 7:673 DCC**, potentially supplemented by a modest waiver of notice period obligations in exchange for the Employee’s commitment to non-disparagement and confidentiality.
3.  **Finality:** The agreement should include a general release clause, preventing future claims related to the employment relationship.

### 5. Next Steps

1.  Instruct our negotiation team to draft a proposed settlement agreement reflecting the transition payment of € 8.750.
2.  Schedule a mediation session with Mr. van der Meer’s legal representative within two weeks.
3.  Upon successful negotiation, ensure the settlement is filed with the UWV (Employee Insurance Agency) if applicable, to finalize the separation legally.

We remain available for further consultation regarding the drafting of the settlement documents.
