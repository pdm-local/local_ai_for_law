**Re:** Legal Obligations Arising from the Data Breach Involving Veldhoven Interactive B.V.

### 1. Executive Summary

This memorandum provides urgent legal advice regarding the personal data breach detected on 10 April 2025 involving Veldhoven Interactive B.V. ("Veldhoven"), a processor acting on behalf of Meridiaan Media B.V. ("Meridiaan") as the controller. As the supervisory authority is the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens), strict adherence to notification deadlines is critical to mitigate administrative fines and reputational damage.

### 2. Factual Background

On 10 April 2025 at approximately 14:30 CET, Veldhoven Interactive B.V., located at Industrieweg 44, 5504 LG Veldhoven, notified Meridiaan Media B.V. of a security incident. An unauthorized third party accessed the customer database hosted by Veldhoven. The breach involved the exposure of names, email addresses, and hashed passwords of approximately 15,000 users.

Veldhoven acted as a processor under Article 28 GDPR. Meridiaan remains the controller responsible for ensuring compliance with the GDPR.

### 3. Notification to the Supervisory Authority

Under **Article 33 GDPR**, the controller must report a personal data breach to the competent supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it.

The term "where feasible" allows for a limited extension if the controller can demonstrate that the 72-hour deadline was genuinely impossible to meet due to the complexity of the initial assessment. However, Meridiaan must assume that the notification should have been submitted immediately upon confirmation of the breach's nature and scope.

**Calculation of Deadline:**
*   **Awareness Date:** 10 April 2025 at 14:30 CET
*   **72-Hour Deadline:** 13 April 2025 at 14:30 CET

Failure to notify the Dutch DPA within this window constitutes a violation of the GDPR. The notification must include:
1.  The nature of the breach;
2.  The categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned;
3.  The likely consequences of the breach;
4.  The measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the breach, including remediation steps.

### 4. Notification to Data Subjects

Pursuant to **Article 34 GDPR**, Meridiaan must communicate the breach to the data subjects without undue delay when the breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

Given that the breach involves hashed passwords, the risk assessment must consider whether the hashing algorithm was compromised or if the passwords were stored in plain text or weakly encrypted formats. If the hash function (e.g., SHA-256) is considered robust and no further personal data (such as credit card numbers or addresses) was exposed, the risk may be deemed low. However, if the breach allows for reverse-engineering or if additional sensitive data was involved, a high-risk scenario exists.

If a high risk is determined, Meridiaan must inform the affected users in clear and plain language, describing the nature of the breach and advising them on protective measures (e.g., password resets). This communication cannot delay unduly and should be coordinated with the supervisory authority's notification.

### 5. Contractual Obligations of Veldhoven Interactive B.V.

The Data Processing Agreement between Meridiaan Media B.V. and Veldhoven Interactive B.V. contains a specific clause requiring Veldhoven to notify Meridiaan of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any case within 36 hours of becoming aware of it.

Veldhoven notified Meridiaan on 10 April 2025 at 14:30 CET. This complies with their contractual obligation. However, Meridiaan’s statutory deadline under Article 33 gdpr is independent of Veldhoven’s performance. Meridiaan cannot rely on delays caused by Veldhoven’s internal reporting processes to justify late notification to the Dutch DPA.

### 6. Recommendations and Immediate Actions

1.  **Finalize Breach Report:** Complete the internal incident report detailing the timeline, technical causes, and impact assessment by 12 April 2025 at 17:00 CET.
2.  **Submit Notification to Dutch DPA:** File the notification via the Dutch DPA’s online portal no later than 13 April 2025 at 14:30 CET. If the full details are not yet available, submit an initial report acknowledging the breach and providing all currently known information, with a commitment to provide updates as facts emerge.
3.  **Assess Article 34 Trigger:** Immediately convene the Data Protection Officer (DPO) and legal team to assess whether the breach poses a high risk to data subjects. If yes, draft the communication for affected users.
4.  **Hold Veldhoven Accountable:** Issue a formal letter to Veldhoven Interactive B.V. reserving Meridiaan’s right to claim damages for any losses incurred due to their failure to implement adequate security measures under Article 28 GDPR and general contract law principles.

### 7. Conclusion

The window for compliance is closing rapidly. The statutory deadline of **72 hours** under **Article 33 gdpr** is absolute unless exceptional circumstances justify a delay, which are difficult to prove after the fact. Immediate action is required to ensure Meridiaan Media B.V. fulfills its legal duties and minimizes liability.
