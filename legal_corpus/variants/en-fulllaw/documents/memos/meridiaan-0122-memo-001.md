**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Tinexus Systems B.V.
**FROM:** mr. J. Havelaar, Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**DATE:** 12 April 2025
**SUBJECT:** Matter 2025.0122 – Assessment of dismissal grounds and recommendation for settlement with Klaver Software B.V.

### 1. Introduction

We refer to our previous discussions regarding the employment relationship between Tinexus Systems B.V. ("the Employer") and Mr. Lars de Vries (Employee), currently employed as Senior Developer. The purpose of this memorandum is to assess the legal viability of terminating the employment contract on urgent grounds pursuant to Article 7:669 DCC and to advise on the financial implications under Article 7:673 DCC, culminating in a recommendation to pursue a settlement agreement.

### 2. Assessment of Grounds for Termination (Article 7:669 DCC)

Tinexus Systems B.V. has indicated that the current situation warrants immediate termination due to urgent circumstances. Under Dutch law, such termination is governed by Article 7:669 DCC of the Dutch Civil Code. To succeed in obtaining a dissolution of the employment contract on these grounds, two cumulative requirements must be met:

1.  **Urgency:** The circumstances must be so severe that Tinexus Systems B.V. cannot reasonably be expected to wait until the end of a notice period or until the exhaustion of other legal remedies before terminating the contract.
2.  **Serious Reason:** There must be a serious reason attributable to the Employee that justifies immediate dismissal without prior warning.

Upon review of the file, we conclude that the evidentiary basis is thin. While Mr. de Vries has exhibited a pattern of unexcused absences and missed deadlines in Q1 2025, these incidents alone do not constitute a fundamental breach of trust sufficient for immediate dismissal without prior formal warnings. The documentation lacks the necessary severity to prove that continued employment poses an unacceptable risk to Tinexus Systems B.V.'s operations. Consequently, a petition for dissolution based solely on Article 7:669 DCC faces a high risk of rejection by the sub-district court (kantonrechter).

### 3. Financial Implications under Article 7:673 DCC

Regardless of the ground for termination, Tinexus Systems B.V. is obligated to pay a statutory transition payment (transitievergoeding) to Mr. de Vries upon dissolution of the employment contract, as stipulated in Article 7:673 DCC.

Based on the Employee’s monthly gross salary and length of service, the calculated statutory amount is **€ 21.300**. This figure represents the minimum liability Tinexus Systems B.V. faces if the relationship ends through no fault of its own or via mutual agreement where statutory protections apply. This payment is due within fourteen days after the termination date.

### 4. Recommendation: Settlement Agreement

Given the weak evidentiary position for unilateral dismissal under Article 7:669 DCC and the certainty of the transition payment liability, we strongly advise against proceeding with a contentious dismissal procedure. Litigation would not only likely fail but also incur additional legal costs and reputational risk.

Instead, we recommend negotiating a **settlement agreement** (vaststellingsovereenkomst) with Mr. de Vries. This approach offers Tinexus Systems B.V. the following advantages:

1.  **Legal Certainty:** A properly drafted settlement agreement, including a valid waiver clause, prevents future claims regarding unlawful termination.
2.  **Cost Control:** We can negotiate a final sum that may be structured favorably, potentially offsetting the statutory € 21.300 against other benefits or concluding matters at a lower total cost if negotiated effectively.
3.  **Efficiency:** The process avoids court proceedings and ensures a swift conclusion to the employment relationship.

### 5. Next Steps

We propose drafting a preliminary settlement proposal outlining the final compensation package. Please confirm your instruction to proceed with negotiations, keeping in mind that the baseline for any discussion is the statutory liability of € 21.300 plus standard administrative costs associated with the termination.

We await your prompt response to initiate contact with Mr. de Vries and his legal representation.
