Dear Colleagues,

I am writing to you regarding the ongoing administrative matter concerning the permit application for your proposed network infrastructure expansion in the Strijp-S district (Matter 2025.0128). As you are aware, the Municipality of Eindhoven issued its decision on February 15, 2025, refusing the requested variance from the local zoning plan.

This memo serves as a critical reminder and formal legal advice regarding the procedural deadlines applicable to this decision. It is imperative that we act immediately to preserve your right to challenge this administrative act.

**The Objection Deadline**

According to **Article 6:7 GALA (Awb)**, the period for filing an objection against a municipal decision is strictly limited. In this specific case, the eight-week objection period commenced on the day following the date of publication of the decision, which was February 16, 2025.

Consequently, you have exactly **8 weeks** to submit a written objection. The final day for filing this objection is April 11, 2025, at 17:00 hours. Any submission received after this deadline will be declared inadmissible by the Municipality.

**Legal Consequences of Inaction**

It cannot be overstated that time is of the essence. If we do not file a timely objection within this statutory window, the decision of February 15, 2025, will become final and binding (*in kracht van gewijsde*). This means that the refusal stands unchallenged, and Kempen Networks B.V. will lose the opportunity to have the merits of the case reviewed by the administrative court. Once the decision becomes final, it is virtually impossible to set aside on procedural grounds alone.

**Recommended Next Steps**

To mitigate this risk, I propose the following immediate actions:

1.  **Drafting:** I am currently preparing a preliminary objection memorandum that addresses the substantive errors in the Municipality's reasoning regarding the technical feasibility and public interest of your network expansion.
2.  **Review:** I will circulate the draft to your technical team for factual verification by March 18, 2025.
3.  **Filing:** Provided we receive timely feedback, we will file the objection electronically via the official government portal no later than April 7, 2025, ensuring a safety buffer before the deadline.

Please confirm your availability for a brief call tomorrow, March 15, 2025, at 10:00 hours, to discuss the strategy and authorize the filing of the objection.
