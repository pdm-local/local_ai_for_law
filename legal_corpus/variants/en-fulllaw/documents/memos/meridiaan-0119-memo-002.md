### 1. Introduction and Purpose

This memorandum accompanies the final draft of the Privacy Notice to be published on the Meridiaan Media B.V. website (https://www.meridianamedia.nl). As the designated data controller, Meridiaan Media B.V. is obligated under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) to provide transparent information to visitors and users regarding the processing of their personal data.

The primary objective of this document is to ensure compliance with transparency obligations while clearly defining the relationship between our company and Veldhoven Interactive B.V., which acts as the data processor for our web hosting, analytics, and CRM infrastructure. This notice must be easily accessible via the footer of every page on our website.

### 3. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data solely for specific, explicit, and legitimate purposes. Below is a detailed breakdown of the processing activities, the categories of data involved, and the corresponding legal bases as required by **Article 6 GDPR**.

#### A. Service Delivery and Contractual Performance
*   **Purpose:** To manage client accounts, process orders for media production services, and deliver contracted results to our clients.
*   **Data Categories:** Contact details (name, email, address), financial information (invoice data), and communication history.
*   **Legal Basis:** The processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract (**Article 6(1)(b) GDPR**).

#### B. Legitimate Interests (Business Administration and Security)
*   **Purpose:** To maintain our customer relationship management system, prevent fraud, ensure IT security, and improve our service quality through anonymized analytics.
*   **Data Categories:** IP addresses, browser types, device information, and business contact details of existing clients.
*   **Legal Basis:** The processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by us or by a third party (**Article 6(1)(f) GDPR**). Our legitimate interest lies in the proper administration of our business and the protection of our rights and assets.

#### C. Legal Obligations
*   **Purpose:** To comply with tax, accounting, and statutory reporting obligations.
*   **Data Categories:** Invoice data, identification documents (for KYC checks where applicable), and payment records.
*   **Legal Basis:** The processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject (**Article 6(1)(c) GDPR**).

### 4. Disclosure of Personal Data to Processors

Meridiaan Media B.V. does not sell personal data. We disclose personal data only to third parties who are bound by confidentiality and act under our instructions as processors.

The following processor is actively involved in the processing of your data:

**Veldhoven Interactive B.V.**
*   **Address:** Industrieweg 12, 5504 LL Veldhoven, The Netherlands
*   **Activities:** Cloud hosting, CRM platform maintenance, and website technical support.
*   **Safeguards:** We have executed a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with Veldhoven Interactive B.V. that ensures the processor provides sufficient guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures in conformity with the GDPR.

### 5. Data Retention Periods

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected.

*   **Client Data:** Retained for 7 years following the termination of the commercial relationship, in accordance with Dutch statutory limitation periods for contractual obligations.
*   **Web Visitor Data (Analytics):** Anonymized after 26 months; raw personal data (IP addresses) is deleted automatically after 14 months.
*   **Job Application Data:** Retained for 6 months from the date of application unless consent is given for future vacancies.

### 6. Rights of the Data Subject

In accordance with **Article 13 GDPR**, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

1.  **Right of Access:** You may request confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning you is being processed and obtain a copy of such data.
2.  **Right to Rectification:** You may request the correction of inaccurate personal data.
3.  **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You may request the deletion of your personal data where there is no good reason for us to continue processing it.
4.  **Right to Restrict Processing:** You may request the restriction of processing in certain circumstances, such as when verifying the accuracy of the data.
5.  **Right to Data Portability:** You may receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
6.  **Right to Object:** You may object to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, please send a written request to privacy@meridianamedia.nl. We will respond within one month. If you believe that Meridiaan Media B.V. has infringed upon the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

### 7. Contact and Updates

This Privacy Notice was last updated on 15 May 2025. We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any significant changes will be posted prominently on our website.

If you have any questions regarding this notice or our data processing activities, please contact our Data Protection Officer via the email address provided above.
