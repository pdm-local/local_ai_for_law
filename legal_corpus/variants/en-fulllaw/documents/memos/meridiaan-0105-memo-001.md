### 1. Executive Summary

This memorandum provides urgent legal advice regarding the data breach incident detected on 10 April 2025 involving personal data processed by your primary IT infrastructure vendor, Polderzicht Solutions B.V., located at Industrieweg 45, Rotterdam. As your lead counsel for privacy matters, I advise that Maaslicht Retail B.V., acting as the Data Controller, must comply with strict statutory deadlines under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Specifically, you are required to report this breach to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it. Furthermore, if the breach poses a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, direct notification to the affected data subjects is mandatory under **Article 34 GDPR**.

### 2. Factual Background

On 10 April 2025 at 09:15 CET, Polderzicht Solutions B.V. notified Maaslicht Retail B.V. that an unauthorized third party gained access to a cloud storage environment containing customer records. The compromised data includes names, email addresses, and hashed payment details of approximately 4,200 customers of Maaslicht Retail B.V. Polderzicht Solutions B.V. has isolated the vulnerability and is currently conducting forensic analysis.

Under your service agreement dated 15 January 2023, Section 14.2 imposes a contractual obligation on Polderzicht Solutions B.V. to notify Maaslicht Retail B.V. within 36 hours of discovering any security incident. While this contractual clause is enforceable between the two companies, it does not replace or extend your statutory obligations under EU law.

### 3. Legal Analysis and Obligations

#### 3.1 Notification to the Supervisory Authority (Article 33 GDPR)

As the Data Controller, Maaslicht Retail B.V. bears the primary responsibility for reporting the breach to the competent supervisory authority. Article 33 GDPR mandates that this notification must occur without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than **72 hours** after having become aware of it.

Given that we were formally notified by Polderzicht Solutions B.V. on 10 April 2025 at 09:15 CET, the statutory deadline for filing the notification with the Dutch DPA expires on **13 April 2025 at 09:15 CET**.

The notification must include:
1. The nature of the personal data breach;
2. The name and contact details of the Data Protection Officer (DPO);
3. A description of the likely consequences of the breach;
4. A description of the measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the breach, including mitigation of possible adverse effects.

Failure to report within this **72 hours** window without justification constitutes a violation of Article 33 GDPR and may result in administrative fines.

#### 3.2 Communication to Data Subjects (Article 34 GDPR)

Pursuant to **Article 34 GDPR**, Maaslicht Retail B.V. must communicate the breach to the affected data subjects without undue delay if the personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

In this instance, although payment details were hashed, the exposure of names and email addresses combined with the potential for credential stuffing attacks presents a clear high risk. Therefore, direct communication to the 4,200 affected customers is required. This communication must be in clear and plain language and describe:
1. The nature of the breach;
2. The name and contact details of the DPO or other point of contact;
3. A description of the likely consequences;
4. The measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

Exemptions to this requirement apply only if appropriate technical protection measures (such as strong encryption) were applied to the data, rendering it unintelligible to any person not authorized to access it. Given the hash vulnerability discussed in the initial report, we advise against relying on this exemption and recommend proceeding with direct customer notification immediately upon regulatory filing.

### 4. Recommended Immediate Actions

1. **File Regulatory Notification:** Draft and submit the breach notification to the Dutch DPA no later than 09:15 CET on 13 April 2025.
2. **Draft Customer Communication:** Prepare a transparent and empathetic notification letter for the affected customers, ensuring it complies with the requirements of **Article 34 GDPR**.
3. **Preserve Evidence:** Secure all logs and communications from Polderzicht Solutions B.V. to demonstrate due diligence and compliance with the contractual notification clause.
4. **Monitor Developments:** Continue close coordination with Polderzicht Solutions B.V. regarding their forensic findings, as this may refine the risk assessment for customer notifications.

### 5. Conclusion

The window for regulatory action is narrowing. Please confirm receipt of this memo and authorize the legal team to proceed with the filing with the Dutch DPA immediately. Do not rely on Polderzicht Solutions B.V.'s contractual timeline; your statutory duty under **Article 33 GDPR** stands independently.
