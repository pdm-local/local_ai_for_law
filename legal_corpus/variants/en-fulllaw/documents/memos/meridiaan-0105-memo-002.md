**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Maaslicht Retail B.V. (Client)
**FROM:** mr. L. Okafor, Privacy / GDPR Specialist, Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**DATE:** 12 April 2025
**SUBJECT:** Drafting of Privacy Notice and Data Processing Agreement Framework

### 1. Introduction

This memorandum outlines the structural and substantive requirements for the privacy notice to be published by Maaslicht Retail B.V. ("Maaslicht" or "the Controller") in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The purpose is to ensure transparency toward data subjects regarding the processing of their personal data, particularly in light of the engagement of **Polderzicht Solutions B.V.** ("Polderzicht" or "the Processor") for cloud-based inventory management and customer loyalty program administration.

### 2. Legal Framework: Article 6 GDPR (Lawfulness of Processing)

In accordance with **Article 6 GDPR**, Maaslicht must identify a valid legal basis for each category of personal data processed. For Maaslicht’s retail operations, the following bases are applicable and must be explicitly stated in the privacy notice:

1.  **Performance of a Contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)**
    *   **Processing:** Order fulfillment, delivery of goods, warranty claims, and customer service interactions.
    *   **Justification:** Processing is necessary to execute sales agreements with customers who have placed orders at Maaslicht’s physical stores or e-commerce platform.

2.  **Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR)**
    *   **Processing:** Direct marketing via email/SMS, participation in the "Maaslicht Loyalty Club," and tracking of website usage for personalized recommendations.
    *   **Justification:** Marketing activities require explicit, freely given consent. Data subjects must be able to withdraw this consent at any time with the same ease as it was given.

3.  **Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)**
    *   **Processing:** Fraud prevention, network security, IT maintenance, and analytical statistics for business optimization.
    *   **Justification:** Maaslicht has a legitimate interest in protecting its assets and optimizing service quality, provided this does not override the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject. A Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) should be retained on file.

4.  **Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR)**
    *   **Processing:** Retention of invoice data for tax and accounting purposes.
    *   **Justification:** Compliance with Dutch tax legislation requiring retention of financial documents for seven years.

### 3. Transparency Obligations: Article 13 GDPR (Information to be provided)

Pursuant to **Article 13 GDPR**, the privacy notice must contain specific information at the time personal data is obtained from the data subject. The following elements are mandatory:

*   **Identity and Contact Details:** The name and contact details of Maaslicht Retail B.V., including its registered address in Eindhoven.
*   **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** If applicable, or an alternative contact point for privacy queries.
*   **Purposes and Legal Basis:** A clear mapping between each processing activity and the corresponding legal basis under Article 6 GDPR as detailed above.
*   **Recipients of Data:** Identification of Polderzicht Solutions B.V. as a processor. It must be clarified that Maaslicht remains the Controller and that Polderzicht processes data only on documented instructions from Maaslicht.
*   **International Transfers:** If Polderzicht stores data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), the appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) must be disclosed.
*   **Retention Periods:** Specific criteria used to determine the retention period for each category of data (see Section 4).
*   **Data Subject Rights:** A comprehensive list of rights, including the right to access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, data portability, and objection.
*   **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Clear instructions on how to withdraw consent for marketing purposes.
*   **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** Information regarding the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

### 4. Data Retention Policy

Maaslicht must define and communicate specific retention periods. The following schedule is recommended:

| Category of Data | Purpose | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Customer Contact Details | Order Fulfillment | 7 years after last order | Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR / Tax Law |
| Loyalty Program Data | Marketing / Analytics | 3 years of inactivity | Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR |
| CVs (Job Applicants) | Recruitment | 6 months after rejection | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR |
| CCTV Footage | Security | 30 days | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR |

### 5. Processor Relationship: Polderzicht Solutions B.V.

Maaslicht Retail B.V. must conclude a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with **Polderzicht Solutions B.V.** pursuant to Article 28 GDPR. The DPA must stipulate that:
*   Polderzicht processes data only on documented instructions from Maaslicht.
*   Polderzicht ensures confidentiality of persons authorized to process the personal data.
*   Polderzicht implements appropriate technical and organizational measures (Article 32 GDPR).
*   Polderzicht assists Maaslicht in fulfilling its obligations regarding data subject rights and DPIAs.

### 6. Conclusion and Next Steps

Maaslicht Retail B.V. should finalize the privacy notice text based on this framework, ensuring it is concise, transparent, intelligible, and easily accessible. The document must be published on the company website and provided to customers at the point of data collection (e.g., at checkout or via registration forms).

We recommend reviewing the draft privacy notice with our team prior to publication to ensure no inconsistencies exist between the stated practices and the legal bases invoked.
