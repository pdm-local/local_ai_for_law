Dear Mr. Okafor,

We are writing to you in your capacity as our privacy counsel regarding the critical security incident discovered on 13 January 2025 involving our cloud infrastructure provider, Tinexus Networks B.V. The incident resulted in unauthorized access to a subset of personal data belonging to our clients. As discussed, we require immediate clarification on our statutory notification duties under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Based on our preliminary investigation, the unauthorized access was detected at 09:00 CET on 13 January 2025. Tinexus Networks B.V., as the processor, has a contractual obligation to notify Maaslicht Software B.V. within 36 hours of detection. They have fulfilled this contractual duty by alerting our incident response team at 18:00 CET on 13 January 2025. Consequently, we were made aware of the breach at that time.

The core of this memo addresses our obligations as the data controller under **Article 33 GDPR** and potential obligations toward data subjects under **Article 34 GDPR**.

### 1. Notification to the Supervisory Authority (Article 33 GDPR)

Under Article 33(1) GDPR, in the event of a personal data breach, the controller shall without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it, notify the breach to the competent supervisory authority. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch DPA).

The phrase "without undue delay" in conjunction with the explicit "72 hours" deadline means that we must assess whether notification is feasible within that window. Since we became aware of the breach at 18:00 CET on 13 January 2025, our 72-hour clock started ticking from that moment.

*   **Deadline Calculation:**
    *   Start: 13 January 2025, 18:00 CET
    *   +24 hours: 14 January 2025, 18:00 CET
    *   +48 hours: 15 January 2025, 18:00 CET
    *   +72 hours: 16 January 2025, 18:00 CET

Therefore, we are legally required to notify the Dutch DPA no later than **16 January 2025 at 18:00 CET**. Any delay beyond this point requires a justification for why the notification was not made within the prescribed period. Given that the breach involves sensitive client financial data, the risk to rights and freedoms is high, making immediate notification imperative to avoid penalties under Article 83 GDPR.

### 2. Communication to Data Subjects (Article 34 GDPR)

Article 34 GDPR stipulates that when the personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the controller shall communicate the breach to the data subject without undue delay.

In this incident, the exposed data includes names, email addresses, and encrypted financial transaction records. Although the encryption keys were not compromised due to our secure key management practices, the exposure of personal identifiers combined with financial context could still pose a high risk if the encryption is breached or if the data is used for phishing attacks.

We must determine if the risk is "high." If Tinexus Networks B.V. confirms that the data was exfiltrated and poses a tangible threat (e.g., identity theft risk), we are obligated to inform the affected individuals directly. This communication should be clear, plain language, and explain:
1. The nature of the breach;
2. The likely consequences;
3. Measures taken or proposed to mitigate the risk (e.g., password resets, monitoring services).

If the risk is not high (e.g., data was encrypted with strong standards and keys remain secure), we may exempt ourselves from direct notification to individuals, but we must still document this assessment thoroughly for supervisory authority review.

### 3. Immediate Action Plan

1.  **Confirm Feasibility of 72-hour Notification:** We will prepare the notification to the Dutch DPA immediately, aiming to submit it by **15 January 2025 at 12:00 CET**, well within the 72-hour window mandated by Article 33 GDPR. This allows time for internal legal review and ensures compliance with the "without undue delay" standard.
2.  **Assess Risk for Data Subjects:** Our technical team is finalizing the impact assessment regarding encryption integrity. If high risk is confirmed, we will draft communications to affected clients by **16 January 2025**.
3.  **Documentation:** All actions, including the timeline of awareness and the justification for any delays or exemptions, must be documented in accordance with Article 33(5) GDPR.

Please advise if you require further technical details from Tinexus Networks B.V. to finalize our notification to the supervisory authority. We await your urgent guidance on the classification of risk under Article 34 GDPR to ensure we do not miss any deadlines.
