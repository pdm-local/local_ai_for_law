**Re:** Matter 2025.0142 – Administrative Law Objection

Dear Director,

I am writing to you regarding the administrative decision issued by the Municipality of Eindhoven on 17 March 2025 concerning your application for an expanded environmental permit for our client’s premises at Kennedyplein 105.

It is with regret that I must inform you that the statutory period for filing an objection has now lapsed. According to **Article 6:7 Awb**, the period for submitting an objection against a decision of the Municipality of Eindhoven is **6 weeks** from the date on which the decision was notified to the interested party.

The municipality’s decision was officially served to Maaslicht Interactive B.V. on 17 March 2025. Consequently, the objection period ended on 28 April 2025. As no objection was filed within this **6 weeks** window, the decision has become final and legally binding.

This means that the Municipality of Eindhoven’s refusal to grant the expanded environmental permit is now unappealable through standard administrative channels. The consequences are immediate: the current operational constraints remain in force, and any further expansion activities without a valid permit would constitute a violation of local regulations, potentially leading to fines or enforcement orders.

**Recommendations for Immediate Action:**

1.  **Review Compliance:** Ensure that all current operations strictly adhere to the conditions set out in the final decision dated 17 March 2025 to avoid any enforcement actions.
2.  **Alternative Routes:** While the objection route is closed, we may explore whether a new application can be submitted based on modified plans or if there are procedural errors that might allow for a request for revocation under exceptional circumstances, though this is highly uncertain given the finality of the prior decision.
3.  **Future Notifications:** For all future municipal decisions, please ensure that legal counsel is engaged immediately upon receipt to monitor strict deadlines such as the **6 weeks** period mandated by Article 6:7 Awb.

Please let me know if you wish to schedule a meeting to discuss potential alternative strategies or to review the implications of this final decision in more detail.
