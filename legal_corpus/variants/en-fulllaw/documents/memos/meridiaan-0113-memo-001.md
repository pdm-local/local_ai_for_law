**CONFIDENTIAL PRIVILEGED MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** The Board of Directors, Maaslicht Cloud B.V.
**FROM:** mr. M. Terhorst, Corporate Law Department
**DATE:** 15 May 2025
**SUBJECT:** Compliance with the Distribution Test for the Proposed Dividend of € 480.000

**1. Introduction and Objective**

You have requested our advice concerning the legality of distributing a dividend amounting to € 480.000 from Maaslicht Cloud B.V. to its sole shareholder, Maaslicht Systems B.V., pursuant to the annual general meeting scheduled for 20 June 2025. This memorandum outlines the statutory requirements governing such distributions and assesses the specific risks associated with the proposed payment.

**2. Statutory Framework: The Distribution Test**

Under Dutch law, a public or private limited company (BV) may only distribute profits to shareholders if it satisfies the distribution test established in Article 2:216 DCC. This provision mandates that the value of the net assets shown in the balance sheet must remain at least equal to the paid-up and called-up part of the issued share capital plus the reserves required by law or the articles of association.

The calculation is not merely a formality but a substantive check on the company's solvency. The relevant date for this assessment is the last day of the financial year preceding the distribution, or an interim balance sheet drawn up specifically for the purpose of the distribution if permitted by the articles of association and prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

**3. Analysis of the Proposed Distribution**

The proposed dividend of € 480.000 represents a significant outflow of capital from Maaslicht Cloud B.V. Based on the preliminary financial statements for the fiscal year ending 31 December 2024, we have reviewed the equity position of the company.

To lawfully declare and pay this dividend, the distributable reserves must be sufficient to cover the € 480.000 without reducing the net assets below the statutory protection floor (the sum of share capital and mandatory reserves). If the distribution of € 480.000 causes the net assets to fall below this threshold at any point during the financial year leading up to or including the distribution date, the resolution declaring the dividend will be voidable, and the directors may be held personally liable for the amount distributed.

**4. Director Liability and Risk Assessment**

It is crucial to emphasize that the directors of Maaslicht Cloud B.V. bear personal liability if they declare a dividend in violation of Article 2:216 DCC. This liability is joint and several. In the event that the distribution test fails, and the company subsequently faces insolvency or creditor claims, the directors may be required to repay the entire € 480.000 to the company’s assets for the benefit of creditors.

Furthermore, if the balance sheet used for the calculation is inaccurate or misleading, the liability extends to damages suffered by third parties relying on that information. We note that the financial statements must fairly present the state of affairs and the results of operations. Any aggressive accounting practices aimed at artificially inflating distributable profits to accommodate the € 480.000 payout would constitute a breach of statutory duty under Article 2:9 DCC.

**5. Recommendations**

Before proceeding with the resolution for the dividend of € 480.000, we advise the following steps:

1.  **Finalize Financial Statements:** Ensure that the annual accounts for 2024 are definitively approved by the general meeting on 20 June 2025.
2.  **Verify Distributable Reserves:** Conduct a precise calculation of the distributable reserves as per the final balance sheet. Confirm that after deducting € 480.000, the net assets remain above the sum of the paid-up capital and mandatory reserves.
3.  **Interim Monitoring:** Monitor cash flow and liquidity closely between 31 December 2024 and 20 June 2025 to ensure no unforeseen liabilities arise that could jeopardize the solvency required by Article 2:216 DCC.

**6. Conclusion**

The distribution of € 480.000 is legally permissible only if it strictly adheres to the solvency test mandated by Article 2:216 DCC. Failure to comply exposes the directors of Maaslicht Cloud B.V. to severe personal liability. We recommend proceeding with caution and ensuring that the underlying financial data robustly supports the distributability of these funds.
