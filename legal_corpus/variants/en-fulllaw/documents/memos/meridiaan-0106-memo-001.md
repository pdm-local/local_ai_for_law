**PRIVILEGED LEGAL ADVICE**

**To:** Legal Department, Nordlicht Retail B.V.
**From:** mr. S. Reinders, Intellectual Property Practice Group, Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**Date:** 14 March 2025
**Re:** Matter 2025.0106 – Infringement by Verdana Digital B.V. regarding proprietary catalog imagery

### 1. Executive Summary and Strategic Recommendation

This memo serves as privileged legal advice concerning the ongoing dispute with Verdana Digital B.V. (“Verdana”) regarding their unauthorized reproduction and commercial exploitation of Nordlicht Retail B.V.’s (“Nordlicht”) product catalog images.

After reviewing the evidence collected on 28 February 2025, it is my professional assessment that Verdana has committed a clear infringement of your exclusive rights under the Dutch Copyright Act (*Auteurswet*). While you have the option to initiate full proceedings for injunctive relief and damages, this memo advises that the most efficient and financially advantageous route is to enforce the penalty clause contained in the Settlement Agreement dated 12 January 2025.

Enforcing this clause allows us to bypass complex litigation on the merits of damages calculation and immediately trigger the recovery of full legal costs under Article 1019h DCCP, as detailed below.

### 2. Factual Background

On 12 January 2025, Nordlicht and Verdana entered into a Settlement Agreement following previous unauthorized use of your intellectual property. A key component of this agreement was a strict prohibition on further use of Nordlicht’s copyrighted material, backed by a penalty clause (*dwangsom*) of € 5,000 per violation, plus additional compensation for actual damages if the penalty proved insufficient.

On 10 March 2025, our digital monitoring team identified that Verdana Digital B.V., registered at Herengracht 450, Amsterdam, had uploaded high-resolution versions of Nordlicht’s exclusive catalog images to their new e-commerce platform, *verdanda-shop.nl*. The images were used without modification and without attribution. This constitutes a direct breach of the Settlement Agreement and an independent infringement of copyright.

### 3. Legal Analysis: Copyright Infringement

The unauthorized use of your catalog images violates specific provisions of Dutch copyright law.

**3.1 Violation of Article 13 Dutch Copyright Act**
Article 13 of the Dutch Copyright Act grants the author exclusive rights to reproduce and publish their work. As the creator and rights holder, Nordlicht possesses these exclusive rights. Verdana’s act of uploading, hosting, and making these images available to the public constitutes an unauthorized reproduction and distribution. There is no statutory exception or license that covers this specific commercial use on a third-party platform.

**3.2 Violation of Article 27 Dutch Copyright Act**
Article 27 of the Dutch Copyright Act explicitly states that any waiver of the rights mentioned in Article 13 must be explicit and written. Verdana cannot claim implied consent. Furthermore, Article 27 reinforces that the author retains moral rights and economic control over the work. By using the images for commercial gain without a valid license or waiver, Verdana has directly contravened these statutory protections.

### 4. Enforcement Strategy: The Penalty Clause vs. Damages Litigation

You may consider filing a claim for full damages based on the actual loss suffered. However, proving the exact quantum of damages in copyright cases is often protracted and uncertain. We must weigh this against the contractual remedy available to us.

**4.1 The Efficiency of the Penalty Clause**
The Settlement Agreement contains a liquidated damages clause. Verdana’s actions on 10 March 2025 trigger this clause automatically. We can now issue a formal notice of default and demand payment of the agreed penalty amount. This method is faster, has lower upfront legal costs, and provides certainty regarding the outcome.

**4.2 Recovery of Full Costs under Article 1019h DCCP**
A critical advantage of this approach lies in the cost regime. Under **Article 1019h DCCP**, the court determines the standard rate for full reimbursement of legal costs. By enforcing the penalty clause through a straightforward procedure or summary proceedings (*kort geding*), we position Nordlicht to claim these statutory full costs from Verdana.

If we were to sue for ordinary damages, we would bear the burden of proving our lawyer’s hours and expenses at market rates, which is often subject to judicial scrutiny and reduction. By utilizing the penalty mechanism and citing the applicability of **Article 1019h DCCP**, we secure a higher likelihood of full cost recovery without the need to litigate the reasonableness of our fee structure.

### 5. Conclusion and Next Steps

I advise you to immediately instruct Verdana Digital B.V. via formal letter to comply with the penalty clause. The letter should:

1. Confirm the breach of the Settlement Agreement dated 12 January 2025.
2. Demand immediate removal of all infringing content from *verdanda-shop.nl*.
3. Require payment of the agreed penalty within 14 days.
4. Reserve the right to claim additional damages and full costs pursuant to **Article 1019h DCCP** if compliance is not achieved.

This approach ensures swift cessation of infringement and optimal financial recovery for Nordlicht Retail B.V.
