### 1. Introduction and Background

We refer to your instruction dated 3 April 2025 regarding the additional tax assessment (besluit op aanvullende aanslag) issued by the Dutch Tax Administration on 28 March 2025 against Kempen Labs B.V., registered at Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven. The assessment pertains to the corporate income tax year 2023 and results in a supplemental tax liability of € 127.300.

The Tax Administration justifies this assessment by claiming that they have discovered new facts which were not available during the initial examination period. Specifically, the inspector argues that certain intercompany service invoices provided by your Dutch subsidiary, Kempen Services B.V., to a related party in Luxembourg lacked sufficient substantive justification at the time of the original filing.

You have instructed us to determine whether the requirement of a 'new fact' (nieuw feit) is met under Article 16 AWR (State Taxes Act). If this requirement is not met, we must advise on the annulment of the assessment.

### 2. Legal Framework: Article 16 AWR (State Taxes Act)

The power of the tax inspector to issue an additional assessment after the statutory objection period has expired is strictly limited by Article 16 AWR. According to established jurisprudence, specifically the Hoge Raad decision of 13 June 2008 (ECLI:NL:HR:2008:BD7549), an inspector may only proceed with an additional assessment if two cumulative conditions are satisfied:

1.  **Existence of a new fact:** A fact must exist that was unknown to the inspector and could not have been known by the taxpayer at the time the tax return was filed, or which the taxpayer did not provide despite having a duty to do so.
2.  **Causal Link:** This new fact must lead to a different tax outcome than the one originally declared.

Crucially, for a fact to be considered 'new', it must be objectively distinguishable from facts that were already known or should have been known during the normal course of business and tax compliance. Mere reinterpretation of existing facts by the inspector does not constitute a new fact.

### 3. Analysis of the Case at Hand

In this matter, the Tax Administration relies on the insufficiency of the documentation regarding the intercompany service fees paid in 2023. However, Kempen Labs B.V. submitted these invoices and the corresponding board resolutions during the initial filing process for the 2023 tax year. These documents were part of the permanent file submitted to the inspector.

The Tax Administration’s current position is not based on newly discovered evidence or previously hidden transactions. Rather, it represents a **qualitative evaluation** of facts that were already present in the administration's knowledge base. The inspector now argues that the *substance* of those services did not meet the arm's length principle as strictly interpreted in their current audit guidelines.

Under Dutch case law (e.g., ECLI:NL:GHDHA:2019:4123), a reassessment cannot be based on a new legal qualification or a stricter interpretation of known facts. The 'new fact' requirement is not satisfied if the fact itself (the payment and the invoice) was known, and only the *legal assessment* of that fact has changed in the eyes of the inspector.

Therefore:
1.  The invoices and board minutes were **not** new facts; they were part of the original disclosure.
2.  The Tax Administration's claim that the services lacked substance is a **legal argument**, not a factual discovery.
3.  Consequently, the statutory precondition for an additional assessment under Article 16 AWR is absent.

### 4. Conclusion and Recommendation

Based on the above, we conclude that the Dutch Tax Administration lacks the legal authority to issue the additional assessment resulting in a liability of € 127.300. The requirement of a 'new fact' under Article 16 AWR is not met because the underlying facts were known and disclosed during the initial tax return process.

We recommend that you file an objection against the assessment immediately, explicitly citing the lack of a new fact under Article 16 AWR. We will draft this objection on your behalf to request the annulment of the assessment in its entirety.
