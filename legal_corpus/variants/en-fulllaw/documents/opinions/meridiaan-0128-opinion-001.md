**LEGAL OPINION**

**Matter:** 2025.0128
**Date:** 12 April 2025
**To:** Kempen Networks B.V., Rotterdamseweg 45, 5611 AP Eindhoven
**From:** mr. P. Slootweg, Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**Re:** Prospects of appeal against the administrative penalty decision dated 15 March 2025

### 1. Introduction and Mandate

Meridiaan Advocaten B.V. has been instructed by Kempen Networks B.V. ("the Client") to provide legal advice regarding the administrative penalty imposed by the Municipality of Eindhoven ("the Authority") in the context of unauthorized installation of network infrastructure on public property at Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven.

The Authority issued a decision on 15 March 2025 imposing a financial penalty of € 10.000 for violations of the Municipal Bylaw on Public Space (Verordening openbare ruimte gemeente Eindhoven). The Client filed an objection against this decision on 1 April 2025. This opinion addresses the legal prospects should the Authority reject the objection, specifically regarding the possibility of lodging an appeal with the competent administrative court pursuant to Article 8:1 GALA (Awb).

### 2. Factual Background

On 10 January 2025, municipal inspectors observed that Kempen Networks B.V. had installed fiber-optic cabling and support poles on the public sidewalk adjacent to Kennedyplein without a valid permit under the Municipal Bylaw on Public Space. Despite prior warnings issued by the Authority on 5 December 2024, the infrastructure remained in place.

Consequently, the Authority invoked its enforcement powers and issued a penalty decision on 15 March 2025. The decision cites violations of Article 3.1 of the Municipal Bylaw regarding the use of public land for commercial purposes without authorization. The imposed fine amounts to € 10.000.

The Client submitted an objection on 1 April 2025, arguing that the installation was necessary for emergency connectivity and that no prior enforcement warning was sufficient to justify the full penalty amount. As of this date, the Authority has not yet issued a decision on the objection. This opinion assumes, for the sake of legal strategy, that the Authority will reject the objection in its entirety.

### 3. Legal Framework: Appeal under Article 8:1 GALA (Awb)

If the objection is rejected, the Client will receive a formal rejection notice ("uitslag bezwaar"). Against this rejection, the Client has the right to lodge an appeal with the administrative court ("bestuursrechter"). The legal basis for this appeal is **Article 8:1 GALA (Awb)**.

**3.1 Competence and Jurisdiction**
The competent court for appeals against decisions of the Municipality of Eindhoven is the District Court of Noord-Brabant, located in 's-Hertogenbosch. The appeal must be lodged within six weeks after the date of the rejection notice, as prescribed by Article 6:7 GALA (Awb).

**3.2 Scope of Judicial Review**
Under **Article 8:1 GALA (Awb)**, the court will review both the legality and the appropriateness ("doelmatigheid en redelijkheid") of the decision. This means the court can assess not only whether the Authority acted in accordance with the law but also whether the imposition of a € 10.000 penalty was reasonable and proportionate given the circumstances.

### 4. Assessment of Prospects

**4.1 Legality of the Decision**
The primary ground for challenge regarding legality is whether the Authority correctly established the factual basis for the violation. The Municipal Bylaw clearly prohibits unauthorized use of public space. Since the cabling was installed without a permit, the factual basis for the violation appears solid. However, we may argue that the Authority failed to adequately consider the proportionality principle under Article 3:4 GALA when determining the severity of the penalty.

**4.2 Appropriateness and Proportionality**
The core of our appeal will focus on the amount of € 10.000. While the violation is undeniable, the court will evaluate whether this amount is disproportionate to the gravity of the offense. Key arguments include:
*   The short duration of the unauthorized installation (approximately three weeks).
*   The lack of actual harm or obstruction to public safety.
*   The Client’s willingness to regularize the situation immediately upon request.

The court has broad discretion under **Article 8:1 GALA (Awb)** to modify or annul the decision if it deems the penalty excessive. Given that the Client acted in an emergency context and remedied the situation promptly, there is a moderate to good prospect that the court will reduce the fine significantly, potentially to € 1.000 or even waiving it entirely, provided the Authority’s assessment of intent is contested effectively.

### 5. Recommendations

1.  **Monitor Objection Deadline:** Await the Authority's response to the objection filed on 1 April 2025.
2.  **Prepare Appeal:** If the objection is rejected, draft the appeal petition immediately to ensure compliance with the six-week deadline under Article 6:7 GALA (Awb).
3.  **Focus on Proportionality:** In the appeal, emphasize that while the violation occurred, the € 10.000 penalty is disproportionate given the remedial actions taken by Kempen Networks B.V. and the absence of malicious intent.

### 6. Conclusion

While the rejection of the objection is a likely procedural outcome, the prospects for success on appeal under **Article 8:1 GALA (Awb)** are favorable regarding the reduction of the penalty amount. The court’s power to assess appropriateness offers a strong avenue to mitigate the financial impact of the € 10.000 fine.

We remain available to represent Kempen Networks B.V. in the subsequent appeal proceedings.
