**Matter:** 2025.0106 – Dispute regarding Intellectual Property Infringement and Contractual Breach

### 1. Introduction and Background

You have requested our assessment of the current litigation prospects following the recent denial by Verdana Digital B.V. of your claims concerning the unauthorized use of proprietary software modules and breach of the licensing agreement dated 12 November 2023. The parties engaged in settlement negotiations on 10 March 2025, during which Verdana Digital B.V. provided a partial acknowledgment of the underlying facts but refused to pay the demanded compensation.

This opinion evaluates how this acknowledgment impacts your position in summary proceedings (*kort geding*) and provides an estimate of the recoverable full costs under Dutch procedural law.

### 2. Legal Assessment of Prospects in Summary Proceedings

#### 2.1 Impact of the Acknowledgment in Settlement

The core strategic advantage gained from the settlement discussions is the **acknowledgment** provided by Verdana Digital B.V. Although this acknowledgment occurred outside of court, it serves as critical evidence regarding the existence of the contractual relationship and the scope of the licensed rights.

Under Dutch law, an admission made during settlement negotiations can be utilized in subsequent proceedings if it clearly establishes the factual basis of your claim. By admitting to the technical integration of your codebase without proper authorization, Verdana Digital B.V. has effectively waived their right to dispute the fundamental facts of infringement in summary proceedings. The denial of liability is now limited to the quantum of damages, which is a matter for the judge's discretion rather than a factual dispute.

#### 2.2 Urgency and Merits (*Kwade Schaamte*)

In summary proceedings, the President requires a sufficient interest (urgency) and a prima facie case on the merits.
*   **Urgency:** The ongoing use of your IP by Verdana Digital B.V. constitutes continuing damage. The urgency is established by the risk of further irreparable harm to your market position and revenue streams.
*   **Merits:** Given the written acknowledgment, your claim for an injunction prohibiting further use and a contribution to damages appears strong. The President is likely to grant provisional relief based on the clear contractual terms and the technical evidence already exchanged during negotiations.

### 3. Estimation of Full Costs (Article 1019h DCCP)

You have inquired about the potential recovery of legal costs should you succeed in the summary proceedings. Dutch procedural law distinguishes between standard statutory fees and full actual costs.

#### 3.1 Statutory vs. Full Costs

In summary proceedings, the default rule under Article 269 Rv is that the losing party pays only a small statutory fee to the winning party's lawyer. However, for intellectual property disputes and cases of significant complexity, it is possible to claim **full costs** (*volledige proceskosten*) pursuant to **Article 1019h DCCP**.

This article allows the court to award full costs if the case presents special difficulties or if the nature of the dispute justifies such an award. Given that this matter involves complex technical evidence regarding software architecture and specific licensing violations, we consider the threshold for Article 1019h DCCP to be met.

#### 3.2 Cost Estimate

Based on our hourly rates and the anticipated billable hours for the summary proceedings (estimated at 40 hours for preparation, hearing, and post-hearing actions), the estimated full costs are as follows:

*   **Legal Fees:** €18,500.00
*   **Disbursements (Expert Reports, Filing Fees):** €2,500.00
*   **Total Estimated Full Costs (Article 1019h DCCP):** **€21,000.00**

If the court accepts our plea for full costs under Article 1019h DCCP, Verdana Digital B.V. would be ordered to pay this amount in addition to any damages awarded for the infringement itself.

### 4. Recommendations

1.  **File Summary Proceedings:** We recommend filing a writ of summons for summary proceedings no later than 28 March 2025 to secure an urgent hearing date before 15 April 2025.
2.  **Plea for Full Costs:** Explicitly plead for the award of full costs under Article 1019h DCCP in the writ, emphasizing the technical complexity and the need to deter future IP violations.
3.  **Preserve Evidence:** Ensure all logs and code comparisons from the settlement phase are formally preserved as evidence.

We await your instruction to proceed with drafting the writ of summons.
