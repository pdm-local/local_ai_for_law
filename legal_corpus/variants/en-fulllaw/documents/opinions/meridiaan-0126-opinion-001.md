**LEGAL OPINION**

**Matter:** 2025.0126
**Date:** 14 May 2025
**To:** Helix Networks B.V.
**From:** mr. A. Koster, Meridiaan Advocaten
**Subject:** Prospects of dissolution and eviction given the arrears versus the defects defence

### 1. Introduction

You have instructed Meridiaan Advocaten to provide a legal opinion regarding the termination of the business tenancy agreement between Helix Networks B.V. ("Landlord") and Brightlane Retail B.V. ("Tenant") for the premises located at Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven. The lease commenced on 1 September 2023.

The primary issue concerns the Tenant's failure to pay rent for the months of March, April, and May 2025. You have valued the outstanding claim at € 4.250 per month. Consequently, the total claimed arrears amount to € 12.750. Brightlane Retail B.V. has defended its non-payment by citing significant defects in the heating system and façade, alleging that these constitute a failure of performance by the Landlord under Article 7:206 DCC.

This opinion assesses the legal prospects of dissolution based on **Article 7:274 DCC** and evaluates whether the Tenant's defence regarding defects will succeed in blocking eviction proceedings.

### 2. Legal Framework: Dissolution for Arrears

Under Dutch law, a business tenancy can be terminated for cause. The most immediate ground for termination in cases of non-payment is statutory dissolution pursuant to **Article 7:274 DCC**. This article provides that the landlord may dissolve the lease by written notice if the tenant has failed to pay rent or other charges for two consecutive months, or for more than three months within a calendar year.

In this case, Brightlane Retail B.V. has failed to pay for March, April, and May 2025. This constitutes a clear breach of contract exceeding the statutory threshold. The arrears are undisputed in terms of their existence; the Tenant acknowledges receipt of invoices but refuses payment based on the alleged quality of the premises.

### 3. Analysis of the Defects Defence

Brightlane Retail B.V. argues that the heating system (HR-combiketel) has been non-functional since February 2025 and that the façade shows structural dampness. They claim these defects relieve them of their obligation to pay rent.

#### 3.1 The Principle of Simultaneous Performance
Generally, a tenant may only withhold rent if the defect is so severe that it fundamentally undermines the purpose of the lease or if the Landlord has refused to perform their duty to repair under Article 7:206 DCC after formal notice. However, Dutch case law establishes that a mere reduction in value does not justify total non-payment.

#### 3.2 Proportionality and Formalities
For the defects defence to succeed in blocking dissolution, Brightlane Retail B.V. must prove:
1. That they have formally notified you of the defects within a reasonable timeframe;
2. That you have failed to remedy these defects despite the notification;
3. That the defects significantly impair the usability of the premises for retail operations.

Our file review indicates that you received an informal email from Brightlane on 15 February 2025 regarding the heating system. You engaged a technician, who attempted repairs on 20 February and 10 March 2025. The heating was restored temporarily but failed again on 28 April 2025. Regarding the façade, no formal written notice of defect was received until 1 May 2025.

The court is likely to view the partial performance regarding the heating as sufficient to prevent total rent withholding. Furthermore, the lack of timely formal notification for the façade issue weakens the Tenant's position. The defence is therefore legally fragile. It may reduce the rent proportionally (reduction in value) but will not justify a complete stoppage of payments for three months.

### 4. Valuation and Quantification

You have stated that the monthly rent is € 4.250. We must verify if this figure is consistent with the lease agreement. According to Clause 3.1 of the signed tenancy contract dated 1 September 2023, the base rent is indeed set at **€ 4.250** per month, exclusive of service charges and VAT.

The total arrears for the period March 2025 through May 2025 are calculated as follows:
*   March 2025: € 4.250
*   April 2025: € 4.274 (adjusted for service charge indexation)
*   May 2025: € 4.319 (adjusted for service charge indexation)

For the purpose of the dissolution request, we will cite the base rent arrears. The core claim remains anchored in the failure to pay the agreed sum of **€ 4.250** per month as stipulated in the primary obligation clause.

### 5. Strategic Advice and Next Steps

Given the statutory clarity of **Article 7:274 DCC**, the prospects for obtaining a dissolution order are strong. The Tenant's defence regarding defects is unlikely to succeed in halting the eviction process entirely, though the court may grant a short grace period for payment if the full amount is settled.

We recommend the following immediate actions:

1.  **Formal Notice of Default:** Send a final formal notice (ingebrekestelling) to Brightlane Retail B.V., setting a deadline of eight days to pay the total arrears of € 12.843,40 including statutory interest and collection costs.
2.  **Dissolution Request:** If payment is not received by the deadline, file an application for dissolution with the Kantongerecht (Subdistrict Court) in Eindhoven under Article 7:274 DCC. This procedure can be expedited given the clear breach.
3.  **Counter-claim Management:** We anticipate Brightlane will file a counterclaim regarding the defects. We will argue that any reduction in rent is limited to the period of actual disablement and does not validate the total non-payment for three months.

### 6. Conclusion

Meridiaan Advocaten advises that you proceed with dissolution proceedings. The breach of contract is substantial, and the Tenant's reliance on defects as a total defence is legally untenable under current jurisprudence. The claim for arrears is valid, quantified at **€ 4.250** per month, and the statutory requirements for eviction are met.

We await your instruction to draft the formal notice of default.
