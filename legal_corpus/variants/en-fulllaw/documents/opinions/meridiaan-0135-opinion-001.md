**LEGAL OPINION**

**To:** Board of Directors, Aurora Media B.V.
**From:** mr. N. Vrugt, Meridiaan Advocaten B.V.
**Date:** 12 May 2025
**Subject:** Compliance Obligations under the Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065)

### 1. Introduction

You have requested our legal advice regarding the compliance obligations of Aurora Media B.V. ("Aurora") under European Union law, specifically concerning its role as an online platform hosting user-generated content. This opinion addresses the duties imposed by **Regulation (EU) 2022/2065** (hereinafter: "the DSA"), with a specific focus on notice systems for illegal content and transparency requirements.

Aurora operates a social media platform registered at Kennedyplein 200, Eindhoven, Netherlands. Given that Aurora facilitates the storage of information provided by users, it qualifies as an online platform under Article 3(j) DSA. This status triggers specific due diligence obligations distinct from mere hosting providers under the E-Commerce Directive.

### 2. Notice-and-Action Mechanism (Article 16 DSA)

As an online platform, Aurora must establish and maintain a clearly accessible, easily usable, and predominantly electronic mechanism for users to notify the platform of content they consider illegal. This is governed by Article 16 of **Regulation (EU) 2022/2065**.

**2.1. Design Requirements**
The notice system must allow users to:
*   Specify why they believe the content is illegal, including the factual and/or legal grounds for their claim;
*   Indicate how they can be contacted by Aurora;
*   State whether they have made the content available on another online platform or website.

**2.2. Processing of Notifications**
Upon receipt of a notice, Aurora is obligated to:
1.  Promptly evaluate the notice and the alleged illegal content, taking into account its own code of conduct or codes of conduct under Article 17 DSA;
2.  Take necessary decisions regarding the content, including whether to remove it or disable access;
3.  Inform the user who uploaded the content about the decision within a reasonable time, providing reasons for any removal or restriction.

Aurora must ensure that its internal procedures allow for human review of such decisions. Automated filtering systems alone are insufficient if they prevent adequate human oversight and appeal mechanisms as required by Article 20 DSA.

### 3. Transparency Reporting (Article 15 DSA)

Aurora is required to publish semi-annual transparency reports. The first report covering the period from 17 February 2024 to 31 December 2024 was due in April 2025. Subsequent reports must be published within one month after the end of each six-month period.

**3.1. Content of the Report**
The transparency report must include:
*   The number and type of notices received;
*   The number and type of decisions taken as a result (e.g., content removed, accounts terminated);
*   The age distribution of users on the platform;
*   Information on the use of automated decision-making systems for content moderation.

### 4. Points of Contact and Cooperation

Under Article 12 DSA, Aurora must designate a single point of contact for communications with Member State supervisory authorities and the European Commission. This contact must be available during normal business hours and capable of providing information in a timely manner.

Furthermore, as an online platform, Aurora may be designated as a Very Large Online Platform (VLOP) if it reaches 45 million monthly active recipients in the Union. Assuming Aurora currently falls below this threshold, it remains subject to the standard obligations outlined above. However, immediate monitoring of user growth is essential to determine if additional compliance layers under Chapters VI and VII DSA will apply.

### 5. Conclusion and Recommendations

Aurora Media B.V. must ensure its current terms of service and internal moderation workflows align strictly with **Regulation (EU) 2022/2065**. We recommend the following immediate actions:

1.  **Audit the Notice System:** Verify that the user interface for reporting illegal content meets all technical and procedural requirements of Article 16 DSA.
2.  **Draft Transparency Report:** Prepare the required semi-annual report ensuring data accuracy regarding notice volumes and moderation outcomes.
3.  **Designate Contact Point:** Formally appoint and publicly list the designated contact person for regulatory inquiries.

Failure to comply with these obligations may result in fines of up to 6% of the total worldwide annual turnover of Aurora Media B.V. under Article 52 DSA.
