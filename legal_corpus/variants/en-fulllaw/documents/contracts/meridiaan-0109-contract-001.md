# SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT ON DIVISION OF COMMUNITY AND SPOUSAL MAINTENANCE

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Helix Media B.V.**, established and having its registered office at Kennedyplein 200, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands, acting through its statutory director Mr. J. van der Meer, hereinafter referred to as "**Party A**";

AND

2. **Saffier Digital B.V.**, established and having its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, acting through its statutory director Ms. L. van Dijk, hereinafter referred to as "**Party B**".

Party A and Party B are collectively referred to as the "**Parties**".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Parties have maintained a joint community of property under Dutch law, governed by the legal regime of community of goods.
B. The Parties wish to dissolve this community of property and settle all related matters, including the division of assets, liabilities, and spousal maintenance obligations.
C. The Parties intend to regulate their mutual rights and obligations in accordance with **Article 1:100 DCC** and other applicable provisions of Dutch civil law.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

## Article 1 – Division of the Community of Property

1.1. Pursuant to **Article 1:100 DCC**, the community of property between Party A and Party B is hereby dissolved with effect from 15 March 2025.

1.2. The Parties shall divide the net assets of the community equally, each party receiving 50% of the total value as determined by the independent auditor’s report dated 1 February 2025.

1.3. Party A shall retain exclusive ownership of the following assets:
   a) The office premises located at Kennedyplein 200, 5611 ER Eindhoven;
   b) All intellectual property rights related to "Helix Analytics";
   c) Bank account NL91ABNA0417164300 held at ABN AMRO.

1.4. Party B shall retain exclusive ownership of the following assets:
   a) The residential property located at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam (the "Matrimonial Home");
   b) All intellectual property rights related to "Saffier Cloud";
   c) Bank account NL20INGB0001234567 held at ING Bank.

## Article 2 – The Matrimonial Home

2.1. Party B shall be solely entitled to use and occupy the Matrimonial Home. Party A waives any right to claim a portion of the equity in the Matrimonial Home beyond the equal division already effected under Article 1.

2.2. Party A agrees to sign all necessary deeds of transfer or waiver required by the Land Registry to formalize Party B’s sole title to the property within thirty (30) days of the execution of this Agreement.

## Article 3 – Spousal Maintenance

3.1. Party A shall pay spousal maintenance to Party B in the amount of **€ 3.100 per month**.

3.2. Payments shall be made via bank transfer to Party B’s designated account (NL20INGB0001234567) by the first day of each calendar month, starting on 1 April 2025.

3.3. The maintenance obligation shall continue indefinitely unless terminated by mutual written agreement or by operation of law, including but not limited to remarriage or death of either party.

3.4. The amount of € **3.100** is fixed for a period of five (5) years from the date of this Agreement, after which it may be reviewed upon mutual consent or judicial request based on proven changes in income.

## Article 4 – Liabilities and Indemnification

4.1. Each Party shall assume full responsibility for any debts or liabilities incurred in their respective names prior to the dissolution of the community of property.

4.2. The Parties indemnify each other against any claims arising from breaches of warranty regarding the assets assigned to them under Article 1.

## Article 5 – Final Provisions

5.1. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or warranties, whether oral or written.

5.2. Any amendment to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both Parties.

5.3. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

5.4. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement on 15 March 2025 in two originals, each Party receiving one original.
