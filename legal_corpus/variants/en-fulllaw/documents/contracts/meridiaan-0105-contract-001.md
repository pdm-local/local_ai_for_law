# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

**This Data Processing Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into on **12 April 2025** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Maaslicht Retail B.V.**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands (hereinafter referred to as the "**Controller**");

AND

**Polderzicht Solutions B.V.**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Herengracht 456, 1017 BV Amsterdam, the Netherlands (hereinafter referred to as the "**Processor**").

The Controller and the Processor may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

## 1. BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE

A. The Controller has engaged the Processor to provide cloud-based IT infrastructure and software maintenance services for the Controller’s retail management systems (the "Services").

B. In the course of providing the Services, the Processor will process personal data on behalf of the Controller.

C. The Parties wish to set out their respective responsibilities regarding the processing of personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation).

## 2. SCOPE AND NATURE OF PROCESSING

The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organization, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which the Processor is subject; in such a case, the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

The subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of the processing are as follows:
1.  **Subject matter**: Customer names, contact details, purchase history, and employee payroll data hosted in cloud environments.
2.  **Duration**: For the term of the main Service Agreement between the Parties, commencing on 1 May 2025, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.
3.  **Nature**: Processing involves storage, backup, retrieval, and secure deletion of personal data.
4.  **Purpose**: To enable the Controller to operate its retail management platform efficiently and securely.

## 3. SUB-PROCESSORS

### 3.1 Authorized Sub-processors
The Controller hereby grants prior general written authorization to the Processor to engage sub-processors. The specific sub-processors authorized under this Agreement are strictly limited to:
1.  **Amazon Web Services EMEA (Ierland)**; and
2.  **Microsoft Azure (Nederland)**.

### 3.2 New Sub-processors
Any additional sub-processors must be approved in advance by the Controller via written notice. The Processor shall not engage a new sub-processor without prior written approval from the Controller. If the Processor intends to appoint a new sub-processor, it shall provide the Controller with at least thirty (30) days' written notice of the intended appointment, setting out the reasons for the new sub-processor and providing sufficient information to enable the Controller to exercise its rights under this Agreement.

### 3.3 Liability
The Processor remains fully liable for any failure by its sub-processors to fulfill their data protection obligations.

## 4. SECURITY MEASURES

Pursuant to **Article 32 GDPR**, the Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including as regards:
1.  The pseudonymization and encryption of personal data;
2.  The ability to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems and services;
3.  The ability to restore the availability and access to personal data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident; and
4.  A process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures for ensuring the security of the processing.

These measures shall include:
*   Encryption of data at rest and in transit using AES-256 standards.
*   Multi-factor authentication for all administrative access.
*   Regular penetration testing conducted by independent third parties no less than annually.
*   Strict access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

## 5. BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the event of a personal data breach, the Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay. Specifically, the Processor guarantees that it will report any confirmed or suspected data breach to the Controller within **36 hours** of becoming aware of such breach.

This notification shall include:
1.  The nature of the personal data breach;
2.  The categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned;
3.  The categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned;
4.  The likely consequences of the personal data breach; and
5.  The measures taken or proposed to be taken by the Processor to address the breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

The Processor shall assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with its obligations under **Article 33 GDPR** (notification of a personal data breach to the supervisory authority) and **Article 34 GDPR** (communication of a personal data breach to the data subject), where applicable.

## 6. ASSISTANCE AND ADVICE

The Processor shall:
1.  Take instructions from the Controller regarding any request by the Controller to exercise the rights of the data subject;
2.  Assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller’s obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject’s rights laid down in Chapter III of the GDPR;
3.  Advise the Controller, where relevant, regarding compliance with **Article 28 GDPR** and other provisions of the GDPR related to processing.

## 7. RETURN AND DELETION OF DATA

Upon termination of the Services or at the choice of the Controller, the Processor shall, at the Controller’s choice, delete or return all personal data to the Controller and delete existing copies unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data. The deletion shall be certified in writing by the Processor within fourteen (14) days of termination.

## 8. AUDITS

The Controller shall be entitled to audit, or have an independent auditor audit, the Processor’s compliance with this Agreement. Such audits may take place during normal business hours upon reasonable notice and no more than once per calendar year, unless a data breach has occurred, in which case the Controller may request immediate audits.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
