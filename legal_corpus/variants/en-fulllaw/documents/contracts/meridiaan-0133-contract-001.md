# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on **12 April 2025** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Maaslicht Software B.V.**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 456, 1017 EW Amsterdam, the Netherlands, acting in the capacity of **Controller** ("Controller"),

AND

**Tinexus Networks B.V.**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Industrieweg 82, 3542 AD Utrecht, the Netherlands, acting in the capacity of **Processor** ("Processor").

(The Controller and the Processor are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".)

## RECITALS

A. The Controller engages the Processor to provide cloud infrastructure and network hosting services (the "Services") for the Controller’s software platform, Maaslicht Suite.

B. In providing the Services, the Processor will process personal data on behalf of the Controller.

C. The Parties wish to ensure that such processing complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable national data protection laws.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. SUBJECT MATTER AND DURATION OF PROCESSING

1.1 The subject matter of the processing is the technical hosting, storage, and management of data containing personal information related to the Controller’s end-users. This includes user profiles, authentication logs, transaction records, and operational metadata necessary for the provision of the Services.

1.2 The duration of the processing is indefinite, commencing on the Effective Date and continuing until terminated in accordance with Clause 9 of this Agreement or until the termination of the principal service agreement between the Parties dated **10 April 2025**.

1.3 The nature of the processing includes the storage, backup, transmission, and analysis of personal data for system maintenance, security monitoring, and service optimization.

1.4 The type of personal data processed includes names, email addresses, IP addresses, device identifiers, and financial transaction data. The categories of data subjects are the end-users of the Controller’s software.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PROCESSOR

2.1 The Processor shall process the personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organization, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which the Processor is subject; in such a case, the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

2.2 The Processor ensures that persons authorized to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

## 3. SECURITY OF PROCESSING

3.1 In accordance with **Article 32 GDPR**, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

3.2 The measures referred to in paragraph 1 shall, as appropriate, include:
   a) the pseudonymization and encryption of personal data;
   b) the ability to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of processing systems and services;
   c) the ability to restore the availability and access to personal data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident;
   d) a process for regularly testing, assessing and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures for ensuring the security of the processing.

3.3 The Processor shall provide all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Clause and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Controller or another auditor mandated by the Controller.

## 4. SUB-PROCESSING

4.1 The Processor is authorized to engage sub-processors for the provision of infrastructure services. The list of approved sub-processors includes:
   a) **Microsoft Azure (Nederland)**; and
   b) **OVHcloud (Frankrijk)**.

4.2 The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of other sub-processors by giving at least thirty (30) days’ prior notice in writing, thereby providing the Controller the opportunity to object to such changes before the new sub-processor becomes engaged.

4.3 If the Controller objects to a proposed sub-processor in writing within the notice period, the Processor shall not engage the proposed sub-processor. In such event, the Processor may terminate this Agreement or continue the provision of Services using alternative compliant infrastructure, provided that such alternative meets the security requirements set forth herein.

4.4 Where the Processor engages a sub-processor, it shall impose upon the sub-processor obligations no less protective than those imposed on the Processor by this Agreement. The Processor remains fully responsible for the performance of its sub-processor’s obligations.

## 5. NOTIFICATION OF A DATA BREACH

5.1 In the event of a personal data breach, the Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay.

5.2 Specifically, the Processor agrees to report any confirmed or suspected data breach to the Controller within **36 hours** of becoming aware of the breach. This notification period is stricter than the statutory requirement for notifying supervisory authorities under Article 33 GDPR, ensuring the Controller can meet its own obligations under **Article 33 GDPR** and **Article 28 GDPR**.

5.3 The notification shall include:
   a) the nature of the personal data breach;
   b) the categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned;
   c) the categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned;
   d) the likely consequences of the personal data breach; and
   e) the measures taken or proposed to be taken by the Processor to address the personal data breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

## 6. ASSISTANCE AND ADVICE

6.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller’s obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject’s rights under Chapter III of the GDPR.

6.2 The Processor shall assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with the obligations laid down in Articles 32 through 36 GDPR taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to the Processor.

## 7. DATA TRANSFERS

7.1 The Parties acknowledge that data may be transferred to sub-processors located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), specifically to OVHcloud in France, which is within the EEA but subject to specific monitoring due to evolving case law regarding transatlantic data flows.

7.2 Where personal data is transferred to a third country not ensuring an adequate level of protection, the Processor shall ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission, and that those safeguards confer enforceable rights on data subjects and effective legal remedies.

## 8. RETURN AND ERASURE OF DATA

8.1 At the choice of the Controller, upon termination of the provision of processing services for personal data, the Processor shall, at the Controller’s discretion, return all the personal data to the Controller or erase it, and delete existing copies unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data.

8.2 The Processor shall certify compliance with Clause 8.1 upon request by the Controller.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other Party commits a material breach of its obligations under this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days after receiving written notice thereof.

9.2 Termination of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations of the Parties arising prior to termination.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or understandings, whether written or oral.

10.2 Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.3 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Netherlands. The courts of Amsterdam shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
