# MASTER SAAS SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master SaaS Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on **15 March 2025** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Vesting Systems B.V.**, a private limited company established under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 87654321 ("Supplier");

AND

**Maaslicht Online B.V.**, a private limited company established under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Wilhelminaplein 10, 5611 ND Eindhoven, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 98765432 ("Customer").

The Supplier and the Customer are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **"Services"** means the cloud-based software platform known as "VestingCore" (the "Platform"), including all updates, upgrades, and technical support provided by the Supplier.
1.2 **"User"** means any employee or authorized representative of the Customer who accesses the Platform.
1.3 **"Data"** means all information processed by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer through the use of the Services.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Provision of Services.** The Supplier shall provide the Customer with access to the Platform in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and any applicable Order Forms.
2.2 **Standard of Care.** The Supplier shall perform its obligations with the degree of care expected of a reasonably competent technology service provider. In performing its duties, the Supplier shall adhere to the standards of reasonableness and fairness as required by Article 6:248 DCC. Furthermore, the Supplier acknowledges its duty of care towards the Customer in accordance with **Article 7:401 DCC**, ensuring that the infrastructure hosting the Platform is secure, up-to-date, and resilient against known threats.
2.3 **Modifications.** The Supplier reserves the right to modify the Services provided that such modifications do not materially reduce the functionality available to the Customer under this Agreement.

## 3. SERVICE LEVELS AND AVAILABILITY

3.1 **Availability Guarantee.** The Supplier guarantees that the Platform will be available for use by Users with a monthly uptime percentage of at least **99.9%** ("Uptime Percentage").
3.2 **Calculation.** Uptime Percentage is calculated monthly as: `(Total Minutes in Month - Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month`.
3.3 **Exclusions.** Maintenance windows scheduled in advance with at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice are excluded from the calculation of Downtime Minutes. Force majeure events, including but not limited to acts of God, war, or widespread internet infrastructure failures, are also excluded.
3.4 **Remedies for Breach of SLA.** If the Uptime Percentage falls below 99.9% in any given month, the Customer shall be entitled to a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of the monthly subscription fee for that month, capped at one hundred percent (100%) of the monthly fee. This remedy is the sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the availability guarantee.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees specified in the applicable Order Form. For the initial term, the annual subscription fee is set at € 25,000.00 (excluding VAT), payable in advance on a quarterly basis.
4.2 **Invoicing.** Invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments shall incur statutory interest as provided by law.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA PROTECTION

5.1 **Ownership.** The Supplier retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, including all intellectual property rights. The Customer acquires no ownership rights in the Platform, only a limited license to use it as specified herein.
5.2 **Data Protection.** Both Parties shall comply with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR. The Supplier acts as a processor of Data on behalf of the Customer (the controller). The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR.

## 6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 **Limitation of Liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by mandatory law, the total aggregate liability of the Supplier, its directors, employees, and agents for any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall be strictly limited.
6.2 **Cap Amount.** Such liability is capped at a maximum amount of **€ 50,000** per individual event and € 50,000 in total for the entire term of this Agreement. This cap applies regardless of the form of action or legal theory upon which the claim is based.
6.3 **Exclusions.** The liability cap set forth in Section 6.2 shall not apply to: (i) liability for death or personal injury caused by the Supplier's gross negligence or willful misconduct; (ii) breaches of confidentiality obligations; or (iii) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights attributable to the Supplier.
6.4 **Consequential Damages.** In no event shall either Party be liable for any indirect, special, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of profits, loss of data, or business interruption, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial period of twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.
7.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of any provision of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice thereof.
7.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, the Supplier shall cease providing access to the Platform. The Customer may, at its own expense, retrieve its Data from the Platform within thirty (30) days following termination.

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
8.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations if such failure or delay is caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, strikes, or government restrictions.
8.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written.
