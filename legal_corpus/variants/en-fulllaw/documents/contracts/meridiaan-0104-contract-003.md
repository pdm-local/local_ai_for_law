**MANAGED SERVICES AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

**Novael Networks B.V.**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 78945612 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Service Provider**"),

**AND:**

**Orion Interactive B.V.**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Witte de Withstraat 50, 3012 ER Rotterdam, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 65432198 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Client**").

(Collectively referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".)

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Client operates an interactive digital platform requiring ongoing technical maintenance, monitoring, and support services.
B. The Service Provider specializes in providing managed IT infrastructure services and has represented that it possesses the necessary expertise to perform such services.
C. The Parties wish to enter into this Agreement to define the terms under which the Service Provider will provide these services to the Client.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

### 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Services"** shall mean the technical maintenance, monitoring, and support services described in Annex A attached hereto.
1.2 **"Service Level Agreement (SLA)"** shall mean the performance metrics outlined in Section 3 of this Agreement.
1.3 **"Incident"** shall mean any event that disrupts or threatens to disrupt the normal operation of the Client’s infrastructure.
1.4 **"Effective Date"** shall mean 15 January 2025.

### 2. Scope of Services

2.1 The Service Provider shall provide the Services with the degree of care, skill, and diligence expected of a professional service provider in the IT sector. Specifically, the Service Provider warrants that it will perform its obligations in accordance with **Article 7:401 DCC** (Dutch Civil Code), ensuring that the services are executed with due regard for the legitimate interests of the Client.

2.2 The specific technical specifications, scope of work, and deliverables are detailed in Annex A.

### 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

3.1 **Priority Classification:** Incidents shall be classified according to their severity as follows:
*   **P1 (Critical):** Complete system outage or critical functionality loss.
*   **P2 (High):** Partial functionality loss with significant business impact.
*   **P3 (Medium):** Minor issue or workaround available.
*   **P4 (Low):** General inquiries or non-urgent requests.

3.2 **Response Times:** The Service Provider guarantees the following initial response times for P1 incidents:
*   **P1 Response Time:** Four hours from the time of notification by the Client.
*   P2 Response Time: Eight hours.
*   P3 Response Time: One business day.

3.3 "Response" means the acknowledgment of the incident and the initiation of diagnostic procedures by a qualified engineer. Resolution times are estimates and may vary based on the complexity of the issue.

### 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 **Monthly Fee:** The Client shall pay a fixed monthly fee of EUR 4,500.00 (four thousand five hundred euros) for the Services.
4.2 **Invoicing:** Invoices shall be issued monthly in advance, payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.
4.3 **Late Payment:** Overdue amounts shall accrue statutory interest as prescribed by Dutch law.

### 5. Liability and Limitation of Liability

5.1 **General Liability:** The Service Provider’s liability for any damages resulting from breach of this Agreement, including but not limited to breach of the duty of care under **Article 6:74 DCC** (Dutch Civil Code), is subject to the limitations set forth in this Section.

5.2 **Cap on Liability:** Except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct by the Service Provider or its employees, the total aggregate liability of the Service Provider for any and all claims, damages, losses, or costs arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be strictly limited to the total fees paid by the Client to the Service Provider during the preceding **twelve months**.

5.3 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages:** In no event shall the Service Provider be liable for any indirect, special, incidental, or consequential damages, including loss of profits, loss of data, or business interruption, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

### 6. Term and Termination

6.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of one (1) year, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.

6.2 **Termination for Convenience:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving three (3) months’ written notice to the other Party, provided that such notice is given no later than six months prior to the end of any annual period.

6.3 **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days after receiving written notice thereof.

### 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information obtained from the other Party during the term of this Agreement ("Confidential Information"). The receiving Party shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party, except as required by law.

### 8. General Provisions

8.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.
8.2 **Dispute Resolution:** Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court located in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
8.3 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, or representations.
