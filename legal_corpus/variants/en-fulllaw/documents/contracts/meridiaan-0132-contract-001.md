**MASTER SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT**

**THIS AGREEMENT** is entered into on 12 May 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**Hanze Networks B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersveld 45, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 88291037 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Supplier**");

AND

**Lumen Digital B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Stratumseind 12, 5611 EK Eindhoven, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 74382910 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Customer**").

The Supplier and the Customer are collectively referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".

**WHEREAS:**
A. The Supplier is engaged in the business of developing, hosting, and maintaining cloud-based network management software;
B. The Customer desires to subscribe to the Supplier’s Software-as-a-Service platform for its internal operational needs;
C. The Parties wish to set forth the terms and conditions under which the Supplier will provide such services to the Customer.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

### 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Services**" means the cloud-based network management platform and related support services provided by the Supplier to the Customer pursuant to this Agreement.
1.2 "**Platform**" means the Supplier’s proprietary software infrastructure hosted in its data centers.
1.3 "**Subscription Term**" means the initial period of twelve (12) months commencing on the Effective Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

### 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Provision of Services**. The Supplier shall provide the Customer with access to the Platform during the Subscription Term. The specific features and service levels are described in Appendix A (Service Level Agreement).
2.2 **Duty of Care**. The Supplier shall perform its obligations under this Agreement with the degree of care expected of a reasonable service provider in the ICT sector, in line with **Article 7:401 DCC** (duty of care in an employment and service contract context) as applied mutatis mutandis to commercial service agreements.
2.3 **Reasonableness and Fairness**. Any interpretation or execution of this Agreement shall be guided by the standards of reasonableness and fairness as set forth in **Article 6:248 DCC** and Article 6:248(2) DCC.

### 3. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 **Annual Fee**. The Customer shall pay the Supplier an annual fee (the "**Annual Fee**") of **€50,000.00** (fifty thousand euros), exclusive of applicable VAT.
3.2 **Payment Schedule**. The Annual Fee is due in advance on the Effective Date and annually thereafter on each anniversary of the Effective Date.
3.3 **Late Payment**. In the event of late payment, the Customer shall be liable for statutory interest as provided by Dutch law.

### 4. SERVICE LEVELS AND AVAILABILITY

4.1 **Uptime Guarantee**. The Supplier guarantees that the Platform will be available and accessible for ninety-nine point five percent (**99.5%**) of all time in any given calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.
4.2 **Service Credits**. If the Platform fails to meet the 99.5% availability target, the Customer shall be entitled to service credits as detailed in Appendix A, subject to the limitation of liability set forth in Section 5.

### 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

5.1 **Cap on Damages**. To the maximum extent permitted by mandatory Dutch law, the Supplier’s total aggregate liability for any and all direct damages, claims, or losses arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be strictly limited to the amount of one Annual Fee paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
5.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages**. Under no circumstances shall the Supplier be liable for indirect, special, incidental, or consequential damages, including but not limited to loss of profits, data, or business opportunities, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

### 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 **Ownership**. The Supplier retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, including all intellectual property rights therein. The Customer acquires no ownership rights in the Platform, only a limited license to use it for internal business purposes during the Subscription Term.

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 **Obligation**. Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party ("Confidential Information") and to use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement.

### 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 **Term**. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for the Subscription Term, automatically renewing for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the current term.
8.2 **Termination for Cause**. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving notice thereof.

### 9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 **Governing Law**. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands, excluding its conflict of law rules.
9.2 **Disputes**. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
