**LEASE AGREEMENT FOR RETAIL PREMISES**

This Lease Agreement (hereinafter: the "Agreement") is entered into on March 15, 2025, in Eindhoven, The Netherlands, by and between:

**Nimbus Retail B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Herestraat 45, 5611 EH Eindhoven, registered in the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 78901234 (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord"),

acting in accordance with Article 2:9 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC),

and

**Stroomdal Media B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizer Karelweg 12, 5611 DK Eindhoven, registered in the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 91234567 (hereinafter referred to as the "Tenant"),

acting in accordance with Article 2:9 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC),

The Landlord and the Tenant may collectively be referred to as the "Parties".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Landlord is the owner of certain retail business premises located at Kennedyplein 50, 5611 EW Eindhoven, The Netherlands (hereinafter: the "Premises");

B. The Tenant wishes to lease the Premises for the purpose of operating a media and retail store, and the Landlord agrees to let the Premises to the Tenant under the terms and conditions set forth herein;

C. The Parties have agreed that this lease shall be governed by Book 7, Title 3, Chapter 4 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC), specifically Article 7:290 DCC regarding business tenancies.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. Object of the Lease**

1.1 The Landlord lets to the Tenant, and the Tenant hires from the Landlord, the retail premises located at Kennedyplein 50, 5611 EW Eindhoven, consisting of a ground-floor shop with a surface area of approximately 200 square meters, including the rear storage area and the sanitary facilities (hereinafter: the "Premises").

1.2 The Tenant shall use the Premises solely for retail trade in media products, marketing materials, and related digital services, or any other permitted commercial activity that does not violate local zoning regulations or disturb the peace of neighboring businesses.

**2. Term**

2.1 The term of this lease shall be for a period of five (5) years, commencing on April 1, 2025, and ending on March 31, 2030 (hereinafter: the "Term").

2.2 Upon expiration of the Term, this Agreement may be renewed for additional periods if both Parties agree in writing at least three months prior to the end of the current Term.

**3. Rent and Payment Terms**

3.1 The monthly rent shall be **€ 9.500**, exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT), payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month.

3.2 The first month's rent shall be due upon signing this Agreement. Subsequent payments shall be made via bank transfer to the Landlord’s designated account: NL12ABNA0123456789, IBAN: NL12ABNA0123456789, BIC: ABNANL2A, in the name of Nimbus Retail B.V.

3.3 In the event of late payment, the Tenant shall be liable for statutory interest on the outstanding amount from the due date until the date of full payment, without the need for a formal notice of default.

**4. Utilities and Service Charges**

4.1 The rent includes basic maintenance of the building structure and common areas (if applicable). However, all costs related to electricity, water, gas, heating, internet connectivity, and telecommunications shall be paid directly by the Tenant to the respective service providers or reimbursed to the Landlord based on actual consumption.

4.2 The Tenant shall ensure that separate meters are installed for electricity and water if not already present, at the Tenant's expense.

**5. Maintenance and Repairs**

5.1 In accordance with Article 7:206 DCC, the Landlord is responsible for major repairs to the structural integrity of the building, including the roof, exterior walls, and main installations (heating, plumbing, electrical wiring).

5.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for day-to-day maintenance and minor repairs within the Premises, including but not limited to:
   a) Replacement of light bulbs;
   b) Maintenance of the interior painting and flooring;
   c) Repair of damage caused by the Tenant, its employees, or visitors;
   d) Cleaning of windows on the tenant side and sanitary facilities.

5.3 The Tenant shall inform the Landlord immediately of any defects or damages that require major repairs. Failure to do so may result in the Tenant being held liable for increased repair costs.

**6. Alterations and Improvements**

6.1 The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Such consent shall not be unreasonably withheld if the alterations do not diminish the value of the property or violate zoning laws.

6.2 Any improvements made with the Landlord’s consent shall remain the property of the Landlord upon expiration of the Term, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

**7. Insurance**

7.1 The Landlord shall maintain adequate insurance for the building structure and its own liability.

7.2 The Tenant shall, at its own expense, insure its contents, fixtures, fittings, and business interruption risks, as well as take out public liability insurance covering any damage caused to third parties during the lease term. Proof of such insurance must be provided to the Landlord upon request.

**8. Subletting and Assignment**

8.1 The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Agreement to a third party without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which may be withheld on reasonable grounds.

**9. Termination**

9.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement before the end of the Term only in case of serious and immediate grounds as defined by Dutch law, including Article 7:274 DCC regarding termination and eviction procedures.

9.2 In the event of a breach of contract by either Party, the non-breaching Party may grant a reasonable period to cure the defect. If the defect is not cured within this period, the non-breaching Party may terminate the Agreement immediately by written notice.

9.3 Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises in good condition, fair wear and tear excepted, and hand over possession to the Landlord no later than 17:00 hours on the last day of the Term.

**10. Applicable Law and Dispute Resolution**

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

10.2 Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, The Netherlands.

**11. Final Provisions**

11.1 Any amendments or additions to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both Parties. Oral agreements are invalid unless confirmed in writing.

11.2 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

11.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the lease of the Premises and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements, or understandings, whether written or oral.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement in duplicate, each Party retaining one original copy, on the date first above written.
