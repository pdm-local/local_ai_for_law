**MAIN SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT**

This Main Software as a Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on **15 May 2025** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Novael Networks B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 88123456 (hereinafter referred to as "**Provider**");

AND

**Orion Interactive B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Copernicuslaan 90, 2333 VR Leiden, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 76543210 (hereinafter referred to as "**Customer**").

Provider and Customer may collectively be referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, Provider develops and operates a cloud-based software platform for data analytics and visualization (the "Service");
WHEREAS, Customer desires to subscribe to the Service for its internal business operations;
WHEREAS, Provider agrees to provide the Service to Customer under the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. DEFINITIONS**

1.1 **"Service"** means the cloud-based analytics platform accessible via the Internet, including all software, documentation, and updates provided by Provider.
1.2 **"Subscription Fee"** means the fees payable by Customer for the use of the Service as detailed in Annex A.
1.3 **"Downtime"** means any period during which the Service is unavailable to Customer due to reasons attributable to Provider.

**2. SCOPE OF SERVICES**

2.1 **Provision of Service**. Provider shall make the Service available to Customer via the Internet in accordance with the specifications agreed upon in Annex A.
2.2 **Duty of Care**. In performing its obligations under this Agreement, Provider shall exercise the degree of care and skill expected of a reasonable provider in the SaaS industry. The Parties acknowledge that Article 7:401 DCC regarding the duty of care in employment contracts does not apply to this commercial relationship, but the general standard of due performance applies.
2.3 **Reasonableness and Fairness**. Any interpretation or execution of this Agreement shall be guided by the principles of reasonableness and fairness as prescribed in Article 6:248 DCC.

**3. SERVICE LEVELS AND AVAILABILITY**

3.1 **Availability Guarantee**. Provider guarantees that the Service will be available for use at least **99.8%** of any given calendar month (the "Uptime Percentage"), excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Force Majeure events.
3.2 **Measurement**. Availability shall be calculated by measuring the total number of minutes in a calendar month minus the minutes of Downtime, divided by the total minutes in that month.
3.3 **Remedies for Non-Availability**. If the Uptime Percentage falls below 99.8% in any calendar month, Customer shall be entitled to a credit against the Subscription Fee for that month equal to 10% of the monthly fee for every full percentage point below the guarantee, up to a maximum credit of 50%.

**4. FEES AND PAYMENT**

4.1 **Subscription Fees**. Customer shall pay Provider the fees specified in Annex A.
4.2 **Invoicing**. Invoices shall be issued monthly in advance and are payable within 30 days of receipt.
4.3 **Late Payment**. Overdue amounts shall bear statutory interest from the date payment is due until the date of full payment.

**5. LIABILITY**

5.1 **Cap on Liability**. To the maximum extent permitted by mandatory law, Provider’s total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall be limited to a maximum amount of **€ 100,000** per event or occurrence.
5.2 **Exclusions**. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the liability cap set forth in Section 5.1 shall not apply to:
    (a) Liability for death or personal injury caused by Provider’s negligence;
    (b) Fraud or willful misconduct by Provider;
    (c) Breach of confidentiality obligations; or
    (d) Violations of intellectual property rights.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other Party ("Confidential Information") and to use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement.

**7. TERM AND TERMINATION**

7.1 **Term**. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of twelve (12) months, automatically renewing for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of termination at least three (3) months prior to the end of the current term.
7.2 **Termination for Cause**. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice thereof.

**8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

8.1 **Governing Law**. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
8.2 **Dispute Resolution**. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

**9. GENERAL PROVISIONS**

9.1 **Entire Agreement**. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements.
9.2 **Amendments**. Any amendments to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both Parties.
9.3 **Severability**. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.
