**MANAGED SERVICES MAINTENANCE AND SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT**

This Managed Services Maintenance and Service Level Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 15 April 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

1.  **Lumen Digital B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, represented by its Director, Mr. J. van der Meer (hereinafter referred to as the "**Client**"); and

2.  **Hanze Networks B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 45, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands, represented by its Managing Director, Ms. A. de Vries (hereinafter referred to as the "**Service Provider**").

The Client and the Service Provider may individually be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, the Client requires ongoing technical maintenance, support, and monitoring of its IT infrastructure;

WHEREAS, the Service Provider is engaged in the business of providing managed IT services and possesses the necessary expertise to perform such services;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. SCOPE OF SERVICES**

1.1. The Service Provider shall provide managed maintenance services for the Client’s IT infrastructure, including but not limited to server monitoring, software updates, backup management, and helpdesk support (the "Services").

1.2. The specific technical specifications and inventory of covered assets are detailed in **Appendix A** attached hereto.

**2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)**

2.1. The Service Provider guarantees the following response times for reported incidents:

| Priority Level | Description | Response Time | Resolution Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1** | Critical System Outage | Four hours (4h) | 24 hours |
| P2 | Major Functionality Loss | Eight hours (8h) | 3 business days |
| P3 | Minor Issue / Query | One business day | 5 business days |

2.2. "Response Time" is defined as the time elapsed between the Client’s notification of an incident via the designated helpdesk portal and the Service Provider’s acknowledgment and initial assessment of the issue.

**3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES**

3.1. **Duty of Care.** The Service Provider shall perform the Services with the degree of care, skill, and diligence expected of a reasonably competent managed service provider. In accordance with general principles of reasonableness and fairness as codified in **Article 7:401 DCC**, the Service Provider is obligated to act in the best interest of the Client while maintaining the integrity of the technical environment.

3.2. **Client Obligations.** The Client shall provide timely access to necessary systems, hardware, and personnel required for the Performance of the Services. The Client shall promptly report any faults or irregularities to the Service Provider.

**4. FEES AND PAYMENT**

4.1. The Client shall pay a monthly fixed fee of € 2,500.00 (two thousand five hundred euros) plus applicable VAT for the Services described in this Agreement.

4.2. Invoices shall be issued monthly in advance and are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

**5. LIABILITY AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

5.1. **Breach of Contract.** In the event that either Party breaches any of its obligations under this Agreement, resulting in direct damages to the other Party, such Party shall be liable for compensation in accordance with **Article 6:74 DCC**. The claimant must prove both the breach and the causal link between the breach and the damage suffered.

5.2. **Cap on Liability.** Notwithstanding any other provision in this Agreement or applicable law, the total aggregate liability of the Service Provider towards the Client for all claims, damages, losses, or causes of action arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be strictly limited to a maximum amount of € 25,000 (twenty-five thousand euros).

5.3. This limitation of liability applies regardless of the basis of the claim (whether in contract, tort, negligence, or otherwise) and shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

5.4. The limitation in Clause 5.2 shall not apply to damages resulting from intentional wrongdoing or gross negligence by the Service Provider or its directors.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

6.1. Both Parties agree to keep confidential all non-public information obtained during the term of this Agreement ("Confidential Information"). Neither Party shall disclose such information to third parties without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law.

**7. TERM AND TERMINATION**

7.1. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of twelve (12) months. Thereafter, it shall automatically renew for successive periods of one (1) year unless either Party gives written notice of termination at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the current term.

7.2. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice thereof.

**8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

8.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

8.2. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.
