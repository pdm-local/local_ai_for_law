# SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT REGARDING DIVISION OF COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY AND SPOUSAL MAINTENANCE

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Novael Systems B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 200, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 98765432 (hereinafter referred to as "**Novael**"), represented by its director, Mr. J. van den Berg;

**AND:**

2. **Hanze Interactive B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizer Karelweg 15, 6702 RD Wageningen, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 12345678 (hereinafter referred to as "**Hanze**"), represented by its director, Ms. A. de Vries;

Novael and Hanze are collectively referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".

## PREAMBLE

A. The Parties were married on 12 May 2015 in Amsterdam, Netherlands, under the regime of community of property.

B. The Parties have decided to divorce by mutual consent and wish to settle all matters arising from their marriage, including the division of the community of property and spousal maintenance, amicably and without litigation.

C. In accordance with **Article 1:100 DCC** (Dutch Civil Code), the Parties wish to regulate the dissolution of the community of property and the distribution of the joint assets and liabilities.

D. The Parties agree that Hanze shall be entitled to the matrimonial home, subject to the payment of compensation to Novael, and that Novael shall be entitled to spousal maintenance from Hanze.

E. Both Parties have been advised by their respective legal counsel regarding the consequences of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

### ARTICLE 1: DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE AND COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY

1.1 The Parties shall jointly file a petition for divorce with the District Court of Amsterdam at the earliest opportunity.

1.2 Pursuant to **Article 1:100 DCC**, the community of property between the Parties shall be dissolved as of the date of the legally binding decision of the District Court granting the divorce (the "**Dissolution Date**").

### ARTICLE 2: DIVISION OF ASSETS AND LIABILITIES

2.1 **Matrimonial Home:** The real estate located at Keizer Karelweg 15, 6702 RD Wageningen, the Netherlands (hereinafter referred to as the "**Matrimonial Home**"), shall be awarded solely to Hanze. Hanze shall assume full responsibility for all existing mortgages and liens attached to the Matrimonial Home from the Dissolution Date onwards.

2.2 **Compensation Payment:** In consideration for the award of the Matrimonial Home, Hanze shall pay Novael a lump sum compensation of € 450.000 (four hundred fifty thousand euros) within thirty (30) calendar days after the Dissolution Date. This payment shall be made via bank transfer to Novael’s designated account: IBAN NL91 ABNA 0417 1643 02, BIC ABNANL2A, held at ABN AMRO Bank N.V., Eindhoven branch.

2.3 **Financial Assets:** All joint bank accounts held at ING Bank N.V. and Rabobank Eindhoven shall be closed as of the Dissolution Date. The remaining balances shall be divided equally (50/50) between the Parties.

2.4 **Business Interests:** Each Party shall retain exclusive ownership of their respective business interests. Novael Systems B.V. remains the sole property of Novael, and Hanze Interactive B.V. remains the sole property of Hanze. No claims for compensation regarding goodwill or future profits of either entity shall be made by the other Party.

### ARTICLE 3: SPOUSAL MAINTENANCE

3.1 **Obligation to Pay:** Hanze shall pay spousal maintenance to Novael in the amount of € 2.400 per month.

3.2 **Payment Terms:** The first payment shall be due on 01 February 2025, and subsequent payments shall be made monthly in arrears on the first business day of each month until further notice or until a new court order is issued. Payments shall be made to Novael’s designated account: IBAN NL67 RABO 0300 4567 89, BIC RABONL2U, held at Rabobank Eindhoven.

3.3 **Duration:** The obligation to pay spousal maintenance shall commence on the Dissolution Date and continue until Novael remarries, enters into a registered partnership, or dies, whichever occurs first. It may also be terminated upon a significant change in circumstances affecting either Party’s income needs or ability to pay, subject to judicial review.

3.4 **Indexation:** The spousal maintenance amount shall be indexed annually on 01 January in accordance with the official consumer price index (CPI) published by Statistics Netherlands (CBS).

### ARTICLE 4: TAX MATTERS

4.1 Each Party shall bear their own tax liabilities arising from the division of assets and spousal maintenance payments, unless Dutch law imposes joint liability or specific tax advantages apply to either Party.

4.2 The Parties shall cooperate in filing any necessary tax returns related to the divorce, including the submission of Form 5038 (Divorce) to the Dutch Tax Administration within thirty days of the Dissolution Date.

### ARTICLE 5: GENERAL PROVISIONS

5.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

5.2 **Confidentiality:** The Parties agree to keep the terms of this Agreement confidential, except as required by law or for professional advice (e.g., tax, legal).

5.3 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or understandings, whether written or oral.

5.4 **Amendments:** Any amendment to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both Parties.

5.5 **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement in duplicate on 15 January 2025 in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
