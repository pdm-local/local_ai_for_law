**SHAREHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

1. **VERDANA RETAIL B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, having its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 87654321 (hereinafter referred to as **“Verdana”**);

AND

2. **NOVAEL DIGITAL B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, having its registered office at Prinses Beatrixlaan 500, 2595 AN The Hague, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 91234567 (hereinafter referred to as **“Novael”**).

Verdana and Novael are individually referred to as a **“Party”** and collectively as the **“Parties”**.

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Parties hold shares in **MERIDIAN TECH SOLUTIONS B.V.** (hereinafter referred to as **“Meridian”**), a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, having its registered office at Kennedyplein 50, 5611 EP Eindhoven, and registered with the KvK under number 76543210.

B. Verdana holds **26%** of the issued shares (and voting rights) in Meridian.

C. Novael holds **74%** of the issued shares (and voting rights) in Meridian.

D. The Parties wish to regulate their relationship as shareholders of Meridian, specifically regarding decision-making processes, the handling of conflicts of interest, specific approval rights, and dispute resolution, by entering into this Shareholders’ Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the **“Agreement”**).

**NOW, THEREFORE,** the Parties agree as follows:

### 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:
*   **Board**: The Board of Directors of Meridian.
*   **Company**: Meridian Tech Solutions B.V.
*   **Conflict Transaction**: Any transaction in which a director or shareholder has a personal interest that conflicts with the interest of the Company.
*   **Material Asset Sale**: Any sale, lease, or transfer of assets exceeding €500,000 individually or €1,500,000 in aggregate per fiscal year.
*   **Veto Rights**: The rights outlined in Article 4 to approve specific resolutions.

### 2. SHAREHOLDING STRUCTURE

2.1 The Parties acknowledge that Verdana holds **26%** of the shares in the Company and Novael holds **74%** of the shares in the Company.

2.2 The Parties shall exercise their voting rights at general meetings of the Company in a manner consistent with this Agreement. In the event of any inconsistency between this Agreement and the Articles of Association of the Company, this Agreement shall prevail between the Parties, though it may not bind third parties unless incorporated into the Articles.

### 3. DECISION-MAKING AND BOARD COMPOSITION

3.1 The Board of Directors shall consist of three (3) members:
*   Two (2) directors appointed by Novael;
*   One (1) director appointed by Verdana.

3.2 The Parties agree that major strategic decisions regarding the Company require a unanimous resolution of the Board, unless otherwise specified in Article 4.

3.3 In the event of a deadlock at the Board level regarding day-to-day operational matters, the matter shall be escalated to the Shareholders’ Meeting for final determination, subject to the Veto Rights set out in Article 4.

### 4. APPROVAL RIGHTS (VETO RIGHTS)

Notwithstanding any provision in the Articles of Association or this Agreement regarding majority voting, the following actions require the prior written consent of **both** Parties:

4.1 Amendments to the Articles of Association of the Company.
4.2 Issuance of new shares or any dilution of existing shareholdings.
4.3 Approval of the annual budget and business plan for the upcoming fiscal year.
4.4 Any loan or guarantee exceeding €250,000.
4.5 Appointment or dismissal of the statutory auditor.
4.6 Dissolution or liquidation of the Company.

### 5. CONFLICT OF INTEREST (ARTICLE 2:239 DCC)

5.1 The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that **Article 2:239 DCC** shall apply to all transactions involving a potential conflict of interest between any director/shareholder and the Company.

5.2 In the event that a director or shareholder has an interest that conflicts with the interests of the Company (a “Conflict Transaction”), such Party must immediately disclose the nature and extent of their interest in writing to the other Parties and the Board.

5.3 The interested Party shall not participate in the deliberations or voting of the Board regarding the Conflict Transaction. The remaining disinterested directors shall decide whether to approve, reject, or conditionally approve the Conflict Transaction based on fair market value and the best interests of the Company.

5.4 If the Board cannot reach a decision due to lack of quorum or unanimity among disinterested directors, the matter must be referred to the Shareholders’ Meeting, where the interested Party shall abstain from voting on the specific resolution concerning the Conflict Transaction.

### 6. INFORMATION RIGHTS

6.1 Novael and Verdana are entitled to receive:
*   Monthly management accounts within thirty (30) days after the end of each month;
*   The annual financial statements within four (4) months after the end of the fiscal year;
*   Prompt notification of any litigation, regulatory investigation, or material adverse change in the business of the Company.

### 7. TRANSFER OF SHARES

7.1 Neither Party may transfer all or part of its shares to a third party without first offering such shares to the other Party on the same terms and conditions (Right of First Refusal).

7.2 The offer must remain open for thirty (30) days. If the other Party does not accept the offer in writing within this period, the selling Party may sell to a third party within the next ninety (90) days on terms no more favorable than those offered.

### 8. DISPUTE MECHANISM

8.1 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be resolved by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the Netherlands Arbitration Institute (NAI).

8.2 The seat of arbitration shall be Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
The language of the arbitration shall be English.
The tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator appointed jointly by the Parties. If the Parties fail to agree on an arbitrator within fourteen (14) days of a request for appointment, the arbitrator shall be appointed by the President of the District Court of Eindhoven.

8.3 The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon the Parties. Judgment upon the award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof.

### 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

9.2 **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or warranties, whether written or oral.

9.3 **Amendments**: Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

9.4 **Severability**: If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Shareholders’ Agreement on **15 March 2025**.
