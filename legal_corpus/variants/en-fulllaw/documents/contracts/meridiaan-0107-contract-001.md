## 4. NOTICE-AND-TAKEDOWN PROCEDURE

4.1 **General Principle**. Despite the limitation of liability described in Section 2, Lumen Cloud respects the intellectual property rights and other legal interests of third parties. In accordance with standard intermediary practices and relevant case law, Lumen Cloud employs a **notice-and-takedown** procedure to address alleged infringements.

4.2 **Submission of Notice**. Any third party (including Meridiaan Interactive acting as a rights holder) claiming that content on the Platform infringes their rights may submit a written notice ("**Notice**") to Lumen Cloud's designated legal department at `legal@lumencloud.nl`.

4.3 **Requirements for Valid Notice**. A valid **notice-and-takedown** request must include:
   a) A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder;
   b) Identification of the copyrighted work or right allegedly infringed;
   c) Sufficient information to locate the infringing content (specific URL or unique identifier);
   d) Contact details of the complaining party, including address, telephone number, and email address;
   e) A statement that the complaining party has a good faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

4.4 **Expeditious Action**. Upon receipt of a compliant Notice regarding content hosted on the Platform, Lumen Cloud will act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing content. This action constitutes the exercise of the safe harbor provisions under Article 6:196c DCC.

4.5 **Counter-Notice**. If Meridiaan Interactive believes that the removal of content was made in error or misidentification, it may submit a counter-notice containing:
   a) A physical or electronic signature;
   b) Identification of the material that has been removed and its location prior to removal;
   c) A statement under penalty of perjury that Meridiaan Interactive has a good faith belief that the material was removed in error or misidentification;
   d) Contact information.

4.6 **Restoration**. If a valid counter-notice is received, Lumen Cloud will forward it to the original complainant. Unless the complainant provides notice within fourteen (14) business days that legal proceedings have been initiated, Lumen Cloud may restore the material.
