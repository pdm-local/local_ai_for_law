**BUSINESS TENANCY AGREEMENT**

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made on 15 March 2025.

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Brightlane Technologies B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizer Karelpad 45, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 88291034 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Landlord**");

AND

2. **Polderzicht Digital B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Stratumseind 12, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 91034556 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Tenant**").

The Landlord and the Tenant are hereinafter jointly referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Landlord is the owner of certain business premises located at Kennedyplein 100, 5611 ER Eindhoven, the Netherlands (hereinafter referred to as the "**Premises**").

B. The Tenant desires to lease the Premises for retail and digital services purposes, and the Landlord agrees to let such Premises to the Tenant on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

C. The Parties acknowledge that this tenancy falls within the scope of Book 7, Title 4 (Business Tenancies) of the Dutch Civil Code, specifically Article 7:290 DCC, which applies provisions regarding business tenancies mutatis mutandis to this agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:**

**1. DEMISE AND PURPOSE**

1.1. The Landlord hereby lets to the Tenant, and the Tenant hereby hires from the Landlord, the Premises situated at Kennedyplein 100, 5611 ER Eindhoven, comprising approximately 120 square meters of retail space on the ground floor, including the adjacent storage area and one designated parking space.

1.2. The Premises shall be used exclusively for retail operations and digital technology services (the "**Permitted Use**"). Any change in the Permitted Use requires the prior written consent of the Landlord, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

**2. TERM**

2.1. The term of this tenancy shall be for a fixed period of five (5) years, commencing on 1 April 2025 and ending on 31 March 2030 (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2. Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of five (5) years, unless either Party gives written notice of termination at least six (6) months prior to the end of the then-current term, in accordance with Article 7:290 DCC and general principles of Dutch law regarding business tenancies.

**3. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS**

3.1. The Tenant shall pay the Landlord a monthly gross rent of **€ 3.100** (three thousand one hundred euros) (the "**Base Rent**").

3.2. The Base Rent is exclusive of VAT. If applicable, VAT shall be added to the Base Rent at the statutory rate in force at the time of each payment.

3.3. The first month's rent and a security deposit equivalent to two months' rent shall be paid upon signing this Agreement. Subsequent rent payments shall be made in advance on the first business day of each calendar month via bank transfer to the Landlord’s nominated account: IBAN NL91ABNA0417164302, BIC ABNANL2A, Account Name: Brightlane Technologies B.V.

3.4. In the event of late payment, the Tenant shall pay default interest at the statutory rate as referred to in Article 6:119a DCC, without prejudice to the Landlord’s right to terminate this Agreement immediately for material breach.

**4. ADJUSTMENT OF RENT**

4.1. The Base Rent shall be adjusted annually on 1 January of each year following the commencement date, based on the percentage change in the consumer price index (CPI) as published by Statistics Netherlands (CBS). If the CPI change is nil or negative, the rent shall not be decreased.

**5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS**

5.1. The Tenant shall, at its own expense, keep the interior of the Premises in good and tenantable repair and condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. This includes, but is not limited to, painting, flooring, lighting fixtures, and internal plumbing.

5.2. The Landlord shall be responsible for the maintenance of the structural elements of the building, including the roof, exterior walls, foundation, and main load-bearing structures, in accordance with Article 7:206 DCC as applied mutatis mutandis to business tenancies under Article 7:290 DCC.

5.3. The Tenant shall promptly notify the Landlord in writing of any defects or necessary repairs requiring the Landlord’s attention. Failure to do so may result in liability for damages resulting from such delay.

**6. UTILITIES AND SERVICE CHARGES**

6.1. The Tenant shall be solely responsible for the costs of electricity, water, gas, internet, and telecommunications services consumed at the Premises.

6.2. The Tenant shall contribute to the common service charges related to the maintenance of the building’s common areas (e.g., lobby, elevators, shared restrooms) in proportion to the rented area relative to the total lettable area of the building. These charges are estimated at € 150 per month and will be reconciled annually based on actual invoices.

**7. INSURANCE**

7.1. The Landlord shall maintain a comprehensive fire and property insurance policy for the building structure.

7.2. The Tenant shall, at its own expense, insure its own contents, fixtures, fittings, and business operations against all risks, including public liability insurance with a minimum coverage of € 1.000.000 per occurrence. Proof of such insurance must be provided to the Landlord upon request.

**8. ALTERATIONS AND IMPROVEMENTS**

8.1. The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Such consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for non-structural interior modifications required for the Permitted Use.

8.2. Any improvements made with the Landlord’s consent shall become the property of the Landlord at the end of the tenancy, unless otherwise agreed in writing. No compensation shall be due for such improvements unless explicitly stipulated in a separate agreement.

**9. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT**

9.1. The Tenant shall not sublet any part of the Premises or assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which may be withheld on reasonable grounds.

9.2. In the event of a change in control of the Tenant (e.g., merger, acquisition, or sale of more than 50% of shares), the Tenant shall notify the Landlord immediately. The Landlord reserves the right to review the new controlling party’s financial standing.

**10. TERMINATION**

10.1. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by the other Party if such breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice, provided that the breach is of such a nature that immediate termination is justified under Article 2:248 DCC and general principles of reasonableness and fairness.

10.2. Upon expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises in a clean and broom-clean condition, returning all keys and access cards to the Landlord.

**11. APPLICABLE LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

11.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands. Specifically, Article 7:290 DCC applies to this tenancy agreement as referenced in the preamble and Clause 5.1.

11.2. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**12. FINAL PROVISIONS**

12.1. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or warranties, whether oral or written.

12.2. Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement on the date first above written.
