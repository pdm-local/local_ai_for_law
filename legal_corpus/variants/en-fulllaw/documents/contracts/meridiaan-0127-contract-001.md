**SHAREHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT**

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made and entered into on **14 March 2025** (the “Effective Date”) by and between:

1. **Duinrand Networks B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizer Karelshof 68, 5611 VX Eindhoven, the Netherlands, represented by its Managing Director, Mr. J. van der Meer (hereinafter referred to as **“Duinrand”**); and

2. **Cobalt Labs B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Strijp-S 12, 5611 ET Eindhoven, the Netherlands, represented by its CEO, Ms. A. Kowalski (hereinafter referred to as **“Cobalt”**).

Duinrand and Cobalt are collectively referred to as the **“Parties”** and individually as a **“Party”**.

**RECITALS**

A. The Parties intend to collaborate on the development and commercialization of advanced network infrastructure software through their respective holdings in **Meridiaan Tech Solutions B.V.** (the “Company”), incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands with registered office at Kennedyplein 200, 5611 AK Eindhoven.

B. Duinrand holds **twenty-six percent (26%)** of the issued shares in the Company. Cobalt holds the remaining fifty-four percent (54%) of the issued shares, while two other minority shareholders hold the remainder. The Parties wish to regulate their rights and obligations as significant shareholders of the Company through this Agreement.

C. The Parties desire to establish clear rules regarding decision-making, conflict of interest management, approval rights, and dispute resolution to ensure the stable governance and strategic growth of the Company.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

### 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Company** means Meridiaan Tech Solutions B.V., Chamber of Commerce number 89234567.
1.2 **Board** means the Board of Directors of the Company.
1.3 **Major Decisions** means those decisions listed in Article 3.2 which require prior written approval from Duinrand.
1.4 **Conflict of Interest** means any situation where a Party or its designated director has a personal interest that conflicts, or may reasonably appear to conflict, with the interests of the Company or the other Party.

### 2. SHAREHOLDING AND RIGHTS

2.1 The Parties acknowledge that Duinrand holds exactly **26%** of the shares in the Company.
2.2 The Parties shall exercise their voting rights at general meetings of the Company in a manner consistent with the principles of this Agreement, aiming to maximize the long-term value of the Company.
2.3 In the event of a capital increase or issuance of new shares, Duinrand and Cobalt shall have preemptive rights proportional to their existing shareholdings, unless waived in writing by both Parties.

### 3. DECISION-MAKING AND APPROVAL RIGHTS

3.1 **Ordinary Business**
The Board shall manage the ordinary business of the Company. Decisions regarding daily operations, including hiring senior staff below the level of C-suite executives and operational expenditures up to €50,000 per transaction, may be made by the Board without prior shareholder approval.

3.2 **Major Decisions Requiring Approval**
Notwithstanding Article 3.1, no action shall be taken by the Company or its Board regarding the following matters (the “Major Decisions”) without the prior written approval of Duinrand:
(a) Amendment of the Articles of Association;
(b) Dissolution, liquidation, or merger of the Company;
(c) Issuance of new shares or transfer of control;
(d) Acquisition or disposal of assets exceeding €200,000 in value;
(e) Entry into any contract outside the ordinary course of business with a duration longer than three (3) years;
(f) Declaration of dividends or distribution of profits.

3.3 **Information Rights**
Duinrand shall receive quarterly financial statements and an annual management report within forty-five (45) days after the end of each respective period. Cobalt shall have equal access to all management information relevant to its role as a majority shareholder.

### 4. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

4.1 **General Obligation**
In accordance with **Article 2:239 DCC**, any member of the Board who has a personal interest in a matter before the Board shall inform the Board thereof immediately. Such member shall not participate in the discussion or voting regarding that specific matter.

4.2 **Procedure for Conflict Resolution**
Where a conflict of interest arises involving one of the Parties’ designated directors:
(a) The interested director must recuse themselves from all deliberations and votes concerning the conflict;
(b) The remaining independent directors shall decide on the matter in good faith, acting in the best interest of the Company as a whole;
(c) If no independent directors remain, the matter must be referred to the Shareholders’ Meeting for resolution.

4.3 **Non-Circumvention**
Neither Party nor its designated directors shall utilize their position or access to confidential information to derive personal gain at the expense of the Company or the other Party. Any benefits derived from such breaches shall be forfeited to the Company.

### 5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION MECHANISM

5.1 **Amicable Settlement**
In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement (a “Dispute”), the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the Dispute through good-faith negotiations between their respective CEOs within thirty (30) days of written notice of the Dispute.

5.2 **Mediation**
If the Dispute is not resolved through negotiation within the period specified in Article 5.1, the Parties shall submit the Dispute to non-binding mediation administered by the Netherlands Arbitration Institute (NAI) in accordance with its fast-track rules. The mediation shall take place in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

5.3 **Arbitration**
If mediation fails to resolve the Dispute within sixty (60) days of the appointment of the mediator, the Dispute shall be finally settled by binding arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the NAI. The tribunal shall consist of three arbitrators. The seat of arbitration shall be Eindhoven. The language of the arbitration shall be English. The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon the Parties.

### 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for an indefinite period, unless terminated by mutual written agreement of both Parties.
6.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving six (6) months’ prior written notice to the other Party, provided that such termination does not violate any mandatory provisions of Dutch corporate law applicable to the Company’s Articles of Association.

### 7. GENERAL PROVISIONS

7.1 **Governing Law**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

7.2 **Entire Agreement**
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements, or understandings, whether written or oral.

7.3 **Amendments**
No amendment or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by both Parties.

7.4 **Severability**
If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

7.5 **Counterparts**
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be considered valid and binding.
