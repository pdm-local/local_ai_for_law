**SETTLEMENT AND CEASE-AND-DESIST AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

1.  **Cobalt Software B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, represented by its director, Mr. J. van der Meer (hereinafter referred to as "**Cobalt**");

    AND

2.  **Tinexus Digital B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Prinsengracht 456, 1017 HT Amsterdam, the Netherlands, represented by its director, Ms. A. de Vries (hereinafter referred to as "**Tinexus**").

Cobalt and Tinexus are hereinafter collectively referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".

**WHEREAS:**

A.  Cobalt is the sole and exclusive owner of all intellectual property rights, including but not limited to copyright, in certain original graphical user interface designs and source code architecture known commercially as "**Cascade**" and "**Helix**" (hereinafter referred to as the "**Licensed Works**").

B.  Tinexus has developed and deployed a software platform that utilizes visual elements and structural logic substantially similar to the Licensed Works without authorization from Cobalt.

C.  The Parties wish to resolve all disputes arising from this matter amicably, avoid litigation, and establish clear boundaries for future conduct regarding the intellectual property rights of Cobalt.

D.  This Agreement is concluded pursuant to **Article 7:900 DCC** (settlement) and acknowledges the infringement of rights protected under **Article 1 Dutch Copyright Act**.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. Acknowledgment of Rights**
Tinexus explicitly acknowledges that Cobalt is the rightful owner of all copyright and related intellectual property rights in the Licensed Works ("Cascade" and "Helix"). Tinexus confirms that it has not obtained any license, express or implied, to use, reproduce, distribute, or create derivative works based on the Licensed Works.

**2. Cease-and-Desist Undertaking**
Tinexus undertakes to immediately:
   a. Remove all instances of the Licensed Works from its servers, websites, applications, and internal documentation;
   b. Refrain from any further use, reproduction, adaptation, or distribution of the Licensed Works in any format;
   c. Ensure that any third-party contractors or partners acting on behalf of Tinexus also comply with these restrictions immediately upon notification.

**3. Penalty Clause**
In the event that Tinexus breaches any obligation set forth in Clause 2 (Cease-and-Desist Undertaking) or violates Cobalt’s intellectual property rights regarding the Licensed Works, Tinexus shall be liable to pay Cobalt a contractual penalty of **€ 5.000** for each individual breach.
   a. The amount of € 5.000 is considered a reasonable estimate of the damage caused by such infringement and does not preclude Cobalt from claiming additional damages insofar as they exceed this penalty, subject to the provisions of **Article 7:900 DCC**.
   b. Each distinct unauthorized use or distribution constitutes a separate breach entitling Cobalt to the full penalty amount per incident.

**4. No Admission of Liability for Costs**
Except for the obligations explicitly stated in this Agreement, neither Party admits liability for any other damages or costs incurred prior to the date of signing. Both Parties agree that this settlement is final and binding regarding all claims related to the Licensed Works up to the date of this Agreement.

**5. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with Dutch law. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court at Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

**6. Entry into Force**
This Agreement enters into force immediately upon signature by both Parties.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Settlement and Cease-and-Desist Agreement in duplicate on **15 November 2023**.
