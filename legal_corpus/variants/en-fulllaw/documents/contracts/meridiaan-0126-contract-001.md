**LEASE AGREEMENT FOR RETAIL PREMISES**

**THIS LEASE AGREEMENT** (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is entered into on March 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**1. THE LANDLORD**
**Helix Networks B.V.**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 200, 5611 EZ Eindhoven, the Netherlands, registered in the Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 89234567 (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord");

AND

**2. THE TENANT**
**Brightlane Retail B.V.**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, registered in the Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 91456789 (hereinafter referred to as the "Tenant").

The Landlord and the Tenant may individually be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

**WITNESSETH THAT:**

**1. PREMISES**
The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant, and the Tenant hereby leases from the Landlord, the retail premises located at Kennedyplein 200, Ground Floor, Unit 4B, 5611 EZ Eindhoven, the Netherlands (hereinafter referred to as the "Premises"). The Premises comprise approximately 150 square meters of usable floor space, intended for general retail use.

**2. TERM**
The term of this lease shall be for a period of five (5) years (the "Term"), commencing on April 1, 2025 (the "Commencement Date") and ending on March 31, 2030 (the "Termination Date"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement or applicable law.

**3. RENT**
The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a monthly rent of **€ 4.250** (four thousand two hundred fifty euros) (the "Monthly Rent"). The Monthly Rent is exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT), which shall be added to each invoice where applicable.

Rent payments shall be made in advance, on or before the first day of each calendar month, via bank transfer to the Landlord’s designated account: IBAN NL12ABNA0123456789, BIC ABNANL2A, in the name of Helix Networks B.V.

**4. INDEXATION**
The Monthly Rent shall be annually adjusted on January 1st of each subsequent year in accordance with the official consumer price index for all households (CPI-H) as published by Statistics Netherlands (CBS), provided that such adjustment is permitted under Article 7:290 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC).

**5. UTILITIES AND SERVICE CHARGES**
The Tenant shall be responsible for the payment of all utilities consumed at the Premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, and gas. The Tenant shall also contribute to the common service charges related to the building’s shared areas, estimated at € 150 per month, subject to annual adjustment based on actual costs.

**6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS**
In accordance with Article 7:290 of the Dutch Civil Code and standard practice for business tenancies:
a) The Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs and maintenance of the building’s exterior, roof, and common areas.
b) The Tenant shall be responsible for all interior maintenance, minor repairs, and upkeep of the Premises, including but not limited to plumbing fixtures within the unit, interior painting (except on initial handover), and replacement of light bulbs.
c) The Tenant shall promptly notify the Landlord of any defects or damage requiring the Landlord’s attention.

**7. USE OF PREMISES**
The Premises shall be used solely for general retail purposes related to consumer electronics and accessories, in compliance with all applicable zoning laws, regulations, and permits. The Tenant shall not use the Premises for any illegal purpose or in a manner that causes nuisance to neighboring tenants or residents.

**8. INSURANCE**
The Tenant shall, at its own expense, maintain comprehensive business insurance covering its inventory, fixtures, fittings, and third-party liability arising from the use of the Premises. The Landlord’s property insurance does not cover the Tenant’s assets.

**9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING**
The Tenant shall not assign this Agreement or sublet any part of the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any permitted assignment shall only be valid if the assignee provides sufficient financial guarantees to the Landlord.

**10. DEFAULT**
If the Tenant fails to pay rent or any other amount due within fourteen (14) days after written notice from the Landlord, or breaches any other material term of this Agreement, the Landlord shall have the right to terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice, without prejudice to its right to claim damages.

**11. APPLICABLE LAW AND JURISDICTION**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date first above written.
