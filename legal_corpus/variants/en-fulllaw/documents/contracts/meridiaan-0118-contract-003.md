**MANAGED SERVICES AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT**

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made on 15 April 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

1. **Vesting Systems B.V.**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 456, 1017 DR Amsterdam, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 89234567 (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider"); and

2. **Maaslicht Online B.V.**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Dommelstraat 12, 5611 GH Eindhoven, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 76543210 (hereinafter referred to as the "Client").

The Service Provider and the Client are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**WHEREAS:**
A. The Service Provider specializes in the provision of IT infrastructure maintenance, technical support, and managed services.
B. The Client wishes to engage the Service Provider to maintain its online platforms and backend systems under specific service level guarantees.
C. The Parties wish to set forth the terms and conditions governing this engagement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

### 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
1.1. The Service Provider shall provide continuous maintenance, monitoring, and technical support services for the Client’s IT infrastructure, including but not limited to server management, software updates, security patching, and database backups (the "Services").
1.2. The specific technical specifications and scope of work are detailed in **Appendix A**, which forms an integral part of this Agreement.

### 2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
2.1. **Priority Classification**: Incidents reported by the Client shall be classified based on severity. Priority 1 ("P1") is defined as a critical system outage affecting core business operations where no workaround exists.
2.2. **Response Time**: For P1 incidents, the Service Provider guarantees a maximum initial response time of **four (4) hours** from the moment the incident is reported via the designated support channel.
2.3. **Resolution Targets**: While the Service Provider shall use reasonable efforts to resolve P1 incidents within eight (8) hours, actual resolution times depend on the complexity of the technical issue and external dependencies.

### 3. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
3.1. The Client shall pay the Service Provider an **annual fee** of €24,000.00 (twenty-four thousand euros), exclusive of VAT.
3.2. The annual fee is payable in twelve equal monthly installments of €2,000.00, due on the first business day of each month.
3.3. Invoices shall be sent to the Client’s billing address: Accounts Payable Dept., Maaslicht Online B.V., Dommelstraat 12, 5611 GH Eindhoven.

### 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES
4.1. **Service Provider**: The Service Provider shall perform the Services with the care and diligence of a professional provider, in accordance with **Article 7:401 DCC**. The Service Provider shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding data security and privacy.
4.2. **Client**: The Client shall provide necessary access credentials, hardware, and network configuration details required for the performance of the Services. The Client shall promptly report any suspected security breaches or technical anomalies to the Service Provider.

### 5. LIABILITY AND DAMAGES
5.1. **Limitation of Liability**: To the maximum extent permitted by mandatory Dutch law, the total aggregate liability of the Service Provider towards the Client for any and all damages, including but not limited to direct losses, consequential damages, loss of profit, or data loss, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be strictly capped at the amount of **one annual fee** paid under this Agreement.
5.2. **Exclusions**: This limitation of liability does not apply to damages resulting from gross negligence or willful misconduct by the Service Provider, nor to cases where the Service Provider fails to comply with its obligations regarding the protection of personal data as required by applicable privacy laws.
5.3. **Breach of Contract**: In the event of a breach of this Agreement by either Party, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to claim damages in accordance with **Article 6:74 DCC**, subject to the cap established in Clause 5.1.

### 6. TERM AND TERMINATION
6.1. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of twelve (12) months.
6.2. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of one year unless either Party provides written notice of termination at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the current term.
6.3. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of its obligations and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days after receiving written notice thereof.

### 7. GENERAL PROVISIONS
7.1. **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands, excluding its conflict of law rules.
7.2. **Dispute Resolution**: Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
7.3. **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, or representations.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement by their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
