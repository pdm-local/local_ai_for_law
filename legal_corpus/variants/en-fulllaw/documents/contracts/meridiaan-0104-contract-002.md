**ADDENDUM TO HOSTING AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Orion Interactive B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, registered in the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 87654321 (hereinafter referred to as "**Client**"),

AND

2. **Novael Networks B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Technologiepark 90, 5656 AE Eindhoven, registered in the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 54321678 (hereinafter referred to as "**Provider**").

Client and Provider may individually be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Parties entered into a Hosting Agreement dated January 10, 2025 (the "Main Agreement") regarding the provision of cloud hosting services by the Provider to the Client.

B. The Parties wish to amend the liability clauses within the Main Agreement to provide greater certainty regarding potential claims for damages.

**IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. Amendment to Liability Provisions**

1.1. Article 8 (Liability) of the Main Agreement is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following text:

> **"8. Limitation of Liability**
>
> 8.1. The Provider’s liability arising from or in connection with the Main Agreement shall be limited to the maximum extent permitted by mandatory Dutch law.
>
> 8.2. In any event, the total aggregate liability of the Provider for all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses suffered by the Client, whether based on contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall be strictly capped at **€ 50,000** per individual event and in no case exceeding **€ 50,000** in total per calendar year.
>
> 8.3. This limitation of liability applies regardless of the legal basis of the claim. The Parties acknowledge that this cap reflects the commercial balance of the agreement and is based on the premium paid for the services. Any breach of fundamental obligations by the Provider shall not override this cap unless explicitly agreed in writing by both Parties, provided such waiver does not violate mandatory provisions of law, including Article 6:74 DCC regarding the assessment of damages."

**2. General Provisions**

2.1. Except as expressly amended by this Addendum, all terms and conditions of the Main Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

2.2. In the event of any conflict between the provisions of this Addendum and the Main Agreement, the provisions of this Addendum shall prevail.

2.3. This Addendum shall enter into force on the date of the last signature below.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Addendum in duplicate on March 15, 2025, at Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
