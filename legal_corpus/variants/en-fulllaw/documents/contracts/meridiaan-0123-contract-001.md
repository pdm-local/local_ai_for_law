**DEED OF DIVISION OF COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY AND SPOUSAL MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Zenith Cloud B.V.**, established and registered in the Netherlands under trade register number of the Chamber of Commerce (KvK) 87654321, having its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, hereinafter referred to as "**Party A**";

and

2. **Meridiaan Technologies B.V.**, established and registered in the Netherlands under trade register number of the Chamber of Commerce (KvK) 98765432, having its registered office at Kennedyplein 200, 5611 EK Eindhoven, hereinafter referred to as "**Party B**";

Party A and Party B are collectively referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Parties were married under the regime of community of property on March 12, 2018, in the municipality of Amsterdam.
B. The Parties have decided to dissolve their marriage and consequently terminate the community of property between them.
C. The Parties wish to regulate the division of the community of assets and liabilities, the disposition of the matrimonial home, and the obligation of spousal maintenance by mutual agreement, in accordance with Article 1:100 of the Dutch Civil Code (Burgerlijk Wetboek, hereinafter "**DCC**").
D. The Parties have agreed that the division shall be effectuated by Party A retaining certain assets and Party B retaining others, subject to a monetary compensation and maintenance obligation as detailed herein.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

### Article 1 – Termination of Community of Property
In accordance with **Article 1:100 DCC**, the Parties hereby agree that the community of property between them shall be terminated effective as of June 15, 2025 (the "**Termination Date**"). From this date forward, each Party shall be solely entitled and liable for assets and debts acquired or incurred in their own name.

### Article 2 – Division of Assets
The Parties have reached agreement on the following division of the community assets:

1. **Real Estate.** The matrimonial home located at Willem de Zwijgerlaan 45, 3081 AB Rotterdam (hereinafter: "**the Property**"), currently registered in the joint names of both Parties in the Land Register of Rotterdam under section 2, page 102, shall be transferred solely to Party B. Party B shall indemnify Party A against any claims related to the mortgage on the Property from the Termination Date onward.
2. **Financial Assets.** The joint bank account held at ING Bank N.V., account number NL91INGB0005123456, shall be closed and the remaining balance of € 45,000.00 (forty-five thousand euros) shall be divided equally, resulting in a payment of € 22,500.00 from Party A to Party B within fourteen days of signing this Deed.
3. **Business Interests.** All shares and interests in business entities currently held jointly or individually by the Parties as part of the community estate shall remain with the legal holder thereof. No further compensation is due regarding these specific interests.

### Article 3 – Spousal Maintenance
1. In consideration for the division of assets described in Article 2, Party A agrees to pay spousal maintenance to Party B.
2. The amount of spousal maintenance shall be **€ 1.850** per month (one thousand eight hundred fifty euros).
3. Payments shall commence on July 1, 2025, and continue until the first day of the month following the death of either Party or Party B’s remarriage or registered partnership.
4. Payments shall be made by bank transfer to Party B’s designated account number NL20ABNA0417164329 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

### Article 4 – Taxes and Costs
Each Party shall bear its own legal, notarial, and tax costs incurred in connection with this Deed. Any transfer taxes or registration fees related to the transfer of the Property described in Article 2.1 shall be borne by Party B.

### Article 5 – Final Provisions
1. **Governing Law.** This Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with Dutch law.
2. **Amendments.** Any amendment or addition to this Deed must be made in writing and signed by both Parties.
3. **Counterparts.** This Deed may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have read this Deed over and have signed it on June 15, 2025, in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
