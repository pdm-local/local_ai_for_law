Any person or entity (including Zenith) who believes that content available on the Platform infringes upon their intellectual property rights, privacy, or other legal interests must submit a formal notice to Aurora’s designated compliance officer.

The notice must be sent via email to `legal@aurorasystems.nl` and must contain:

2. Identification of the copyrighted or infringing work claimed to have been infringed, or other relevant legal right;
3. Sufficient information to locate the allegedly infringing content (including specific URLs);
4. Contact details of the complainant; and
5. A statement of good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or the law.

Upon receipt of a compliant notice, Aurora will evaluate the claim. If the claim meets the requirements for expeditious action under Dutch law, Aurora will disable access to or remove the identified content immediately. Zenith agrees that Aurora’s liability for such content is excluded provided this procedure is followed and acted upon in a timely manner.
