# SHAREHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT

**THIS SHAREHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT** (the “Agreement”) is entered into on **15 January 2025** in Eindhoven, the Netherlands, by and between:

1. **MAASLICHT CLOUD B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 100, 5611 EP Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered in the Dutch Trade Register at the Chamber of Commerce under number 88291044 (the “Cloud Shareholder”);

2. **MAASLICHT SYSTEMS B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 105, 5611 EP Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered in the Dutch Trade Register at the Chamber of Commerce under number 91023455 (the “Systems Shareholder”);

(each a “Party” and together the “Parties”)

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Parties are shareholders of **Maaslicht Digital Solutions B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 100, 5611 EP Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered in the Dutch Trade Register at the Chamber of Commerce under number 77482910 (the “Company”);

B. The Cloud Shareholder holds **49%** of the issued share capital of the Company;

C. The Systems Shareholder holds the remaining 51% of the issued share capital of the Company;

D. The Parties wish to regulate their rights and obligations as shareholders of the Company, including rules on decision-making, conflict of interest, approval rights, and dispute resolution;

**NOW, THEREFORE,** the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:
- **“Board”** means the Board of Directors of the Company;
- **“Business Day”** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the Netherlands;
- **“Conflict Transaction”** means any transaction in which a Party or its affiliate has a personal interest that conflicts with the interests of the Company;
- **“Major Decision”** means any decision listed in Annex A to this Agreement;

## 2. SHAREHOLDING STRUCTURE

2.1 The Parties confirm that their respective shareholdings in the Company are as follows:
- Maaslicht Cloud B.V.: **49%** of the shares;
- Maaslicht Systems B.V.: 51% of the shares.

2.2 Each Party shall maintain its shareholding in the Company for a minimum period of five (5) years from the date of this Agreement, subject to the transfer restrictions set out in Article 6.

## 3. DECISION-MAKING AND APPROVAL RIGHTS

3.1 The Parties agree that certain decisions regarding the Company require the prior written approval of both Parties (a “Supermajority Approval”), regardless of the voting rights attached to their shares. These Major Decisions include:
a) Changes to the Articles of Association;
b) Amendment of the Company’s business objectives;
c) Issuance of new shares or financial instruments;
d) Acquisition or disposal of assets exceeding €500,000;
e) Entry into any loan agreement exceeding €1,000,000;
f) Appointment or dismissal of the CEO or CFO.

3.2 The Party holding 49% of the shares shall have veto rights over all Major Decisions. Any attempt to pass a Major Decision without the written consent of both Parties shall be null and void ab initio.

## 4. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

4.1 In the event that any Party or its representative on the Board has a personal interest in a matter to be decided by the Board, such Party shall immediately disclose the nature and extent of such interest in writing to the other Party and the Board.

4.2 The interested Party shall not participate in the deliberations or voting on such matter. This obligation is expressly grounded in **Article 2:239 DCC**, which mandates that a director acting in a situation where he has an interest conflicting with the interests of the company must inform the other directors thereof and may not vote on the resolution unless permitted by law or the Articles of Association.

4.3 The Board shall document the conflict disclosure and the exclusion from voting in the minutes of the relevant meeting. Failure to comply with Article 2:239 DCC shall render the related resolution voidable at the instance of any shareholder.

## 5. INFORMATION RIGHTS

5.1 Each Party shall have the right to receive, within thirty (30) days after the end of each quarter, unaudited financial statements of the Company.

5.2 Within six (6) months after the end of each fiscal year, each Party shall receive audited annual accounts prepared by an independent registered auditor appointed by mutual agreement of the Parties.

## 6. TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS

6.1 Neither Party may transfer any shares in the Company to a third party without the prior written consent of the other Party.

6.2 Any proposed transfer must first be offered to the other Party on the same terms and conditions as those offered by the third party (Right of First Refusal).

## 7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

7.1 In the event of a dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter through good-faith negotiations between their respective legal representatives within thirty (30) days.

7.2 If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, it shall be referred to and finally determined by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the Netherlands Arbitration Institute (NAI). The seat of arbitration shall be Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

7.3 The arbitral award shall be final and binding on the Parties. Judgment upon the award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof.

## 8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

## 9. FINAL PROVISIONS

9.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or understandings, whether written or oral.

9.2 Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

9.3 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
