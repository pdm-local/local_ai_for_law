**SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Helix Data B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 88921456 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Employer**");

AND

2. **Forta Logistics B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Wilhelminaplein 45, 5611 ER Eindhoven, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 99887766 (hereinafter referred to as the "**Employee**").

The Employer and the Employee are hereinafter jointly referred to as the "**Parties**".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Parties have entered into an employment contract dated 1 January 2022, whereby the Employee was employed by the Employer as a Senior Data Analyst (hereinafter the "**Employment Contract**").

B. The Parties have mutually agreed to terminate the Employment Contract by mutual consent, effective as of the Termination Date defined below.

C. The Parties wish to regulate the financial consequences of this termination and expressly waive any further claims against each other arising from or in connection with the Employment Contract or its termination, subject to the statutory minimum protections under Dutch law.

D. The Employer acknowledges that Article 7:673 of the Dutch Civil Code (DCC) governs the transition payment applicable in cases of dissolution of an employment contract at the initiative of the employer. However, as this termination is by mutual consent, the Parties agree on a specific settlement amount that satisfies any potential entitlement under Article 7:673 DCC and any other statutory or contractual obligations.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. Termination of Employment**
The Employment Contract shall be terminated by mutual consent effective on **15 May 2025** (the "**Termination Date**"). The Employee’s final day of work will be **30 April 2025**.

**2. Final Salary and Holiday Pay**
The Employer shall pay the Employee his full salary for the month of April 2025, proportional to the days worked, in accordance with standard payroll practices. Additionally, the Employer shall pay out any accrued but unused holiday entitlements equivalent to **14 days** of work.

**3. Settlement Payment (Severance)**
In full and final settlement of all financial claims, including but not limited to any transition payment under Article 7:673 DCC, bonus entitlements, expense reimbursements, or damages for unfair dismissal, the Employer shall pay the Employee a gross amount of **€ 8.750** (eight thousand seven hundred fifty euros).

**4. Payment Terms**
The Settlement Payment described in Clause 3 shall be transferred to the Employee’s bank account (ING Bank, IBAN: NL91INGB0002345678) no later than **30 days** after the signature of this Agreement by both Parties. The payment will be subject to applicable wage tax and social security contributions as required by Dutch law.

**5. Handover and Cooperation**
The Employee agrees to cooperate in a reasonable manner during the notice period ending on the Termination Date to ensure an orderly transition of his duties. This includes handing over all company data, devices, access keys, and documents belonging to the Employer. The Employee confirms that he has returned all company property on **30 April 2025**.

**6. Reference**
Subject to the Employee’s written consent, the Employer agrees to provide a positive reference for the Employee upon request by future prospective employers. Such reference will be provided via email from the HR department of Helix Data B.V.

**7. Final Discharge**
Except for the obligations explicitly set forth in this Agreement, both Parties hereby discharge each other from all claims, demands, and liabilities arising from or in connection with the Employment Contract, the performance thereof, or its termination, up to and including the Termination Date. This discharge does not affect any rights that arise after the Termination Date.

**8. Confidentiality**
The Parties agree to keep the existence and content of this Agreement confidential, except as required by law, professional obligations, or tax authorities. Neither Party shall make any public statements regarding the termination unless specifically authorized in writing by the other Party.

**9. Applicable Law and Jurisdiction**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with Dutch law. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court at Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**10. Entire Agreement**
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or understandings, whether written or oral.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement in duplicate, each retaining one original copy.
