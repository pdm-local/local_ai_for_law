**SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND TERMINATION BY MUTUAL CONSENT**

This Settlement Agreement and Termination by Mutual Consent (hereinafter: the "Agreement") is entered into on 12 May 2025 in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**BETWEEN:**

1. **Nordlicht Networks B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 45, 5611 EP Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 88210945 (hereinafter referred to as the "Employer");

   represented by mr. L. de Vries, in his capacity as Managing Director;

**AND:**

2. **Novael Technologies B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Griftpark 12, 1031 KV Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 91023456 (hereinafter referred to as the "Former Employee's Company");

   represented by mr. A. van den Berg, in his capacity as Sole Shareholder;

Collectively referred to as the "Parties".

**PREAMBLE**

A. Mr. A. van den Berg (hereinafter: the "Employee") has been employed by Nordlicht Networks B.V. since 1 September 2019 under an indefinite employment contract as Chief Technology Officer, with a monthly gross salary of € 8,500.

B. The Employee’s company, Novael Technologies B.V., represents the Employee in this matter due to his active involvement in the negotiations regarding the termination of his employment relationship.

C. The Parties have discussed the future of the employment relationship and have reached the conclusion that it is appropriate to terminate the employment contract by mutual consent, effective as set out below.

D. The Parties wish to regulate the effects of this termination in a comprehensive settlement agreement, including a severance payment and a final discharge from all claims arising from or during the employment relationship.

**NOW THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

**ARTICLE 1: TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT**

1.1 The employment contract between the Employee and the Employer is terminated by mutual consent, effective on 30 June 2025 (hereinafter: the "Termination Date").

1.2 On the Termination Date, all duties of the Employee shall cease. The Employee shall return all company property, including but not limited to laptops, access cards, and documents, no later than two business days prior to the Termination Date.

**ARTICLE 2: SEVERANCE PAYMENT**

2.1 In consideration for the termination of the employment contract and in full settlement of any entitlements under Article 7:673 DCC (transition payment), the Employer shall pay the Employee a gross amount of **€ 21,300** (twenty-one thousand three hundred euros).

2.2 This payment shall be made within 30 days after the Termination Date, provided that all conditions precedent in this Agreement have been met. The payment will be subject to applicable Dutch wage tax and social security premiums, which shall be withheld by the Employer in accordance with statutory requirements.

2.3 The Employee acknowledges that this amount constitutes full satisfaction of any transition payment claims under Article 7:673 DCC and that no additional amounts are owed regarding the calculation of the transition payment based on length of service or age.

**ARTICLE 3: FINAL DISCHARGE AND WAIVER OF CLAIMS**

3.1 Upon receipt of the full severance payment referred to in Article 2, the Parties hereby mutually and irrevocably discharge each other from all claims, demands, and liabilities arising from or during the employment relationship up to and including the Termination Date.

3.2 This final discharge includes, but is not limited to:
   a) Claims for unpaid wages, overtime, holiday pay, or holiday allowances;
   b) Claims regarding the calculation or payment of the transition payment under Article 7:673 DCC;
   c) Claims regarding unlawful dismissal or wrongful termination;
   d) Claims related to injury in connection with the performance of work;
   e) Any other claims arising from the employment contract or the termination thereof.

3.3 The Employee waives any right to appeal this Agreement before the sub-district court (kantonrechter) or any other judicial authority, pursuant to Article 7:658 DCC.

**ARTICLE 4: COOPERATION AND REFERENCES**

4.1 The Employer agrees to provide a neutral reference letter upon request by the Employee for future employment purposes. The content of such reference letter shall be agreed upon in writing by both Parties prior to issuance.

4.2 The Employee agrees to cooperate reasonably with any reasonable requests from the Employer regarding the handover of duties and knowledge transfer during the notice period leading up to the Termination Date.

**ARTICLE 5: CONFIDENTIALITY**

5.1 The Parties agree to keep the existence and content of this Agreement confidential, except as required by law or for professional advisory purposes (e.g., tax advisors, accountants).

**ARTICLE 6: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

6.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

6.2 Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement in duplicate on the date first above written.
