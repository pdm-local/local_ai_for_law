**ADDENDUM TO HOSTING SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Addendum (the "Addendum") is entered into on 12 April 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**Lumen Digital B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, having its registered office at Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands, represented by its Director, Mr. J. van der Meer (hereinafter referred to as "**Lumen**");

AND

**Hanze Networks B.V.**, a private company with limited liability incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, having its registered office at Wieringerwerfstraat 45, 9723 KX Groningen, the Netherlands, represented by its CEO, Ms. A. de Vries (hereinafter referred to as "**Hanze**").

Lumen and Hanze may individually be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

**PREAMBLE**

A. The Parties have entered into a Main Hosting Services Agreement dated 15 January 2024 (the "Main Agreement"), pursuant to which Lumen provides cloud hosting infrastructure and related services to Hanze.

B. The Parties wish to amend the liability provisions of the Main Agreement to better reflect the risk profile of the services provided, specifically regarding standard operational breaches.

C. This Addendum forms an integral part of the Main Agreement. In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Addendum and the Main Agreement, the terms of this Addendum shall prevail.

**1. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

1.1. Subject to Section 1.2 below, Lumen’s total aggregate liability towards Hanze for any and all claims arising out of or in connection with the performance of the Main Agreement, whether based in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise, shall be strictly limited to the fees paid by Hanze to Lumen in the preceding **twelve months** prior to the occurrence of the event giving rise to the claim.

1.2. The limitation of liability set forth in Section 1.1 shall not apply to:
(a) Cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct by Lumen or its direct employees;
(b) Liability for death or personal injury caused by Lumen’s fault;
(c) Breach of confidentiality obligations; or
(d) Any claim where the limitation is excluded by mandatory provisions of Dutch law.

1.3. In the event that a court of competent jurisdiction determines that the liability cap in Section 1.1 is unenforceable under **Article 6:74 DCC** or any other mandatory provision of Dutch law, the Parties agree that Lumen’s liability shall be limited to the maximum extent permitted by such mandatory provisions.

**2. EXCLUSION OF CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES**

Except for the liabilities expressly assumed by Lumen in Section 1.2, Lumen shall not be liable for any indirect, special, incidental, or consequential damages, including but not limited to loss of profits, loss of data, loss of business opportunity, or damage to reputation, even if Lumen has been advised of the possibility of such damages.

**3. CONTINUATION OF SERVICES**

This Addendum does not constitute a termination or suspension of the services provided under the Main Agreement. All other terms and conditions of the Main Agreement remain in full force and effect.

**4. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

4.1. This Addendum shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.
4.2. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Addendum shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court seated in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Addendum as of the date first above written.
