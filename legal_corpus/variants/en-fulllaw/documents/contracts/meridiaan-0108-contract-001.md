**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

**BETWEEN:**

1.  **HELIX DATA B.V.**, established in Eindhoven, Netherlands, with its registered office at Kennedyplein 50, Eindhoven, 5611 ER, acting under the name Helix Data; hereinafter referred to as the **"Employer"**;

    represented by its Managing Director, Ms. A. van der Meer, acting in accordance with the provisions of its Articles of Association;

2.  **FORTA LOGISTICS B.V.**, established in Amsterdam, Netherlands, with its registered office at Herengracht 100, Amsterdam, 1015 BH, acting under the name Forta Logistics; hereinafter referred to as the **"Employee"**;

    represented by its Director, Mr. J. de Vries, acting in accordance with the provisions of its Articles of Association;

The Employer and the Employee are hereinafter collectively referred to as the **"Parties"**.

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Employer carries out business activities in the field of data processing and cloud infrastructure services and requires specialized technical expertise.
B. The Employee possesses the necessary professional qualifications and experience to perform such duties.
C. The Parties wish to establish an employment relationship governed by Dutch law, specifically Article 7:610 of the Dutch Civil Code (Burgerlijk Wetboek).

**NOW IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

### ARTICLE 1: NATURE AND EFFECTIVE DATE OF THE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

1.1. The Employer engages the Employee, and the Employee accepts engagement, in the capacity of **Senior Systems Architect**.

1.2. This employment relationship shall commence on **1 May 2025** (the "Effective Date").

1.3. The employment agreement is concluded for an indefinite period.

### ARTICLE 2: PLACE OF WORK AND DUTIES

2.1. The Employee shall perform his duties primarily at the business premises of the Employer located at Kennedyplein 50, Eindhoven, or at such other location as the Employer may designate from time to time.

2.2. The Employee’s duties include, but are not limited to:
    a) Designing and implementing scalable cloud infrastructure solutions;
    b) Leading technical teams in the development of data processing algorithms;
    c) Advising on data security and integrity standards;
    d) Performing any other tasks reasonably associated with the role of Senior Systems Architect.

2.3. The Employee shall devote his full professional attention and best efforts to the performance of these duties during normal business hours, defined as Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 hours, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### ARTICLE 3: REMUNERATION

3.1. In consideration for the services rendered, the Employer shall pay the Employee a gross monthly salary of **€9,500.00** (nine thousand five hundred euros), payable in arrears at the end of each calendar month via bank transfer to the Employee’s designated account.

3.2. The Employee is entitled to an annual holiday allowance of 8% of the gross annual salary, paid out prior to the commencement of the holiday period.

3.3. The Employer shall deduct all applicable taxes and social security contributions from the remuneration in accordance with prevailing Dutch legislation.

### ARTICLE 4: HOLIDAYS AND LEAVE

4.1. The Employee is entitled to twenty-four (24) paid holiday days per calendar year, based on a full-time working week of forty (40) hours.

4.2. If the employment relationship terminates during the calendar year in which it commenced, the Employee shall be entitled to a pro-rata portion of the annual holiday entitlement.

### ARTICLE 5: NON-COMPETE OBLIGATION

5.1. During the term of this employment agreement and for a period of **12 months** following the termination of the employment relationship (the "Restricted Period"), the Employee shall not, either directly or indirectly, within the Netherlands, engage in any activity that competes with the business activities of the Employer, nor shall he provide services to clients or competitors of the Employer.

5.2. The Parties acknowledge that this restriction is necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Employer, including its client relationships and technical know-how. This clause is drafted in accordance with Article 7:653 DCC.

5.3. If a court of competent jurisdiction deems the geographical scope or duration of the non-compete obligation too broad, such obligation shall be automatically reduced to the maximum extent permitted by law without rendering the remainder of this Article void.

### ARTICLE 6: CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1. The Employee agrees to keep confidential all information regarding the Employer’s business, clients, technical processes, and trade secrets, both during and after the term of employment. This obligation survives the termination of this agreement indefinitely.

### ARTICLE 7: TERMINATION

7.1. Either Party may terminate this employment agreement by giving written notice to the other Party. The notice period shall be in accordance with mandatory provisions of Dutch law applicable to indefinite contracts at the time of termination.

7.2. In the event of summary dismissal for a urgent reason as defined by law, the Employer reserves the right to terminate the agreement immediately without notice or compensation.

### ARTICLE 8: APPLICABLE LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1. This employment agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

8.2. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, Netherlands.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Employment Agreement in duplicate, each Party retaining one original, on **15 April 2025**.
