**ADDENDUM TO HOSTING AGREEMENT**

**Date:** 12 April 2025

**Between:**

1.  **Vesting Systems B.V.**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Keizer Karelweg 45, 5611 EP Eindhoven, the Netherlands, acting herein as the Service Provider (hereinafter referred to as "**Provider**");

    and

2.  **Maaslicht Online B.V.**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Nijmegenlaan 88, 3565 KV Utrecht, the Netherlands, acting herein as the Client (hereinafter referred to as "**Client**").

The Provider and the Client are collectively referred to as the "Parties".

**PREAMBLE**

WHEREAS the Parties have entered into a Hosting Agreement dated 10 January 2024 (hereinafter the "Main Agreement") regarding the provision of cloud hosting services for the Client’s e-commerce platform;

WHEREAS the Parties wish to amend certain terms of the Main Agreement, specifically concerning the limitation of liability in the event of breach of contract;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. AMENDMENT OF LIABILITY CLAUSE**

1.1. The Parties hereby agree to derogate from the general statutory provisions on damages, including but not limited to Article 6:74 DCC, regarding the Provider's liability for any and all claims arising out of or in connection with the Main Agreement.

1.2. Notwithstanding any other provision in the Main Agreement or applicable law, the total aggregate liability of the Provider towards the Client for any single incident or series of related incidents shall be strictly limited to a maximum amount of **€ 25,000** (twenty-five thousand euros).

1.3. This cap applies regardless of the legal basis of the claim, whether in contract, tort, negligence, or otherwise, and excludes cases of intent or gross negligence as defined under mandatory Dutch law where such exclusion is not permitted by statute.

**2. EFFECTIVE DATE**

This Addendum shall enter into force on 1 May 2025 and shall apply retroactively to all services rendered under the Main Agreement from that date forward. For incidents occurring prior to this date, the liability terms of the original Main Agreement shall remain in effect.

**3. GENERAL PROVISIONS**

3.1. All other terms and conditions of the Main Agreement remain in full force and effect and are hereby confirmed by the Parties.

3.2. In the event of any inconsistency between this Addendum and the Main Agreement, the provisions of this Addendum shall prevail.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Addendum in duplicate on the date stated below.
