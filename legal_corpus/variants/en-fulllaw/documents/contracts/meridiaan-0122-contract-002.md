**SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT REGARDING TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT**

**BETWEEN:**

**Tinexus Systems B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at De Eindhovenseweg 45, 5612 AB Eindhoven, and registered in the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 87 65 43 21 (hereinafter referred to as "**the Employer**"),

represented by:
Ms. Elena Vos, acting in her capacity as Managing Director;

**AND:**

**Mr. David K. Van der Meer**, holding a Dutch passport with number AB1234567, residing at Keizersgracht 102, 1015 CJ Amsterdam (hereinafter referred to as "**the Employee**"),

**AND:**

**Klaver Software B.V.**, a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, with its registered office at Prinses Beatrixlaan 82, 2595 AN The Hague, and registered in the Dutch Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 74 12 36 90 (hereinafter referred to as "**the Third Party Guarantor**"),

represented by:
Mr. Thomas de Vries, acting in his capacity as Director;

The Employer, the Employee, and the Third Party Guarantor may collectively be referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**".

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Employee has been employed by the Employer as Senior Software Architect since 1 September 2019, pursuant to an indefinite employment contract (hereinafter the "**Employment Contract**").

B. The Parties have mutually agreed that it is in their best interest to terminate the Employment Contract by mutual consent, effective from a specific date, and to settle all rights and obligations arising from or in connection with the Employment Contract, including the termination thereof.

C. Klaver Software B.V. has agreed to act as guarantor for the performance of the Employer's obligations under this Agreement.

D. The Parties wish to record this agreement in writing to prevent any future disputes regarding the termination of the Employment Contract and the financial consequences thereof.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. Termination of Employment Contract**

1.1. The Parties mutually agree that the Employment Contract shall be terminated by mutual consent on 30 April 2025 (the "**Termination Date**").

1.2. Upon the Termination Date, all rights and obligations arising from the Employment Contract shall cease, save for those provisions which expressly or by nature are intended to survive termination.

**2. Severance Payment**

2.1. In consideration of the termination of the Employment Contract and as a full and final settlement of any claims the Employee may have against the Employer arising from the Employment Contract (including but not limited to claims for wages, bonuses, holiday allowances, overtime, and statutory severance pay under Article 7:673 DCC), the Employer shall pay the Employee a gross amount of **€ 21.300** (twenty-one thousand three hundred euros).

2.2. The Severance Payment referred to in Clause 2.1 shall be paid by the Employer to the bank account specified by the Employee in writing at least ten days prior to the payment date. If no alternative account is provided, payment shall be made to the employee’s last known salary account of Tinexus Systems B.V.

2.3. The Parties acknowledge that the Severance Payment constitutes a final settlement. The Employee agrees that he has no further claims against the Employer regarding the termination of the Employment Contract or the period prior thereto.

**3. Final Discharge and Waiver**

3.1. Upon receipt of the full Severance Payment, the Employee hereby fully and irrevocably discharges the Employer, its directors, officers, employees, agents, successors, and assigns from any and all claims, demands, liabilities, damages, costs, or expenses of any kind, whether known or unknown, arising out of or in connection with the Employment Contract or its termination.

3.2. The Employee waives any right to claim statutory severance pay under Article 7:673 DCC or any other legal provision regarding dismissal compensation, as this right is fully compensated by the Severance Payment described in Clause 2.1.

3.3. Each Party hereby confirms that it has not relied on any representations, warranties, or statements made by the other Party concerning the consequences of the termination, except for those expressly set forth in this Agreement.

**4. Return of Property and Confidentiality**

4.1. The Employee agrees to return all company property, including laptops, access cards, documents, and data belonging to the Employer, no later than 29 April 2025.

4.2. The obligations of confidentiality imposed on the Employee under the Employment Contract shall continue to apply after the Termination Date. Specifically, the Employee agrees not to disclose any confidential information of the Employer to any third party.

**5. Cooperation and Reference**

5.1. The Employer agrees to provide a neutral reference letter upon request by the Employee.

5.2. The Parties agree to cooperate in good faith to ensure an orderly transition of duties prior to the Termination Date.

**6. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution**

6.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

6.2. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

**7. Miscellaneous**

7.1. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or understandings, whether written or oral.

7.2. Any amendment to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both Parties.

7.3. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Settlement Agreement in duplicate on 15 March 2025.
