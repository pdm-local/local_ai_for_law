    Privacy Policy

    Local-first & private. pdm Legal Workspace runs entirely on your machine. All documents, matters, notes, and chat history are stored locally in the data/ directory and are never transmitted to any external server.

    The assistant. Answers are written on this machine. No document or question leaves your network.

    No analytics or telemetry. This application does not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or any personal data.

    Data retention. You are in full control. Deleting a knowledge group permanently removes all associated documents and generated content. Use the export feature to back up your data at any time.

    Contact. If you have questions about data handling, contact the system administrator who deployed this instance.
